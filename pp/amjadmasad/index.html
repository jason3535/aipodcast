阿姆贾德·马萨德 Amjad Masad

Replit 创始人兼 CEO · Founder & CEO, Replit

生于约旦，赴美先后在 Codecademy 和 Facebook 做开发者工具，2016 年创办 Replit，押的是「一个浏览器标签页就该够写完并发布软件」。2024 年的 Replit Agent 把这件事推到智能体编程，Agent 4 能从一句大白话描述直接搭出一家初创公司的产品。2026 年 3 月 Georgian 领投 4 亿美元 D 轮，估值翻三倍到 90 亿美元，注册用户超 5000 万，他的目标是年底 10 亿美元年化收入。他在教育话题上直言不讳：年轻人该学的是指挥机器，而不是跟机器竞争。

Born in Jordan, moved to the US to work on developer tools at Codecademy and then Facebook, and founded Replit in 2016 on the idea that a browser tab should be enough to write and ship software. Replit Agent (2024) turned that into agentic coding, and Agent 4 will build a startup from a plain-English description. A $400M Series D led by Georgian in March 2026 tripled the company to a $9B valuation on more than 50 million registered users, with Masad targeting $1B ARR by year end. Outspoken about education: his argument is that young people should learn to direct machines rather than compete with them.
