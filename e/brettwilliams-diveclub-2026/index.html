从怀疑到创造：设计师的 AI 跃迁

From Skeptic to Builder: A Designer's Leap into AI

本期速览 · Overview

一位曾对 AI 心存疑虑的视觉设计师，如今通过构建 Mac 应用找到全新创作掌控力。

A visual designer who once feared AI discovers the joy of building a Mac app, unlocking new creative control.

要点 · TL;DR

  • 一位持怀疑态度的设计师发现，通过清晰描述方向，AI 带来的控制力远超预期。
    A skeptical designer finds AI offers more control than expected by describing clear direction.
  • 真正的差异在于品味和判断力；AI 只会执行明确指令，不会自行设计。
    Real differentiators are taste and judgment; AI only follows clear direction, not design by itself.
  • 直接用 AI 开始构建，仅在最后 20%的打磨阶段使用 Figma，例如阴影和渐变。
    Start building directly with AI; use Figma only for final 20% polish like shadows and gradients.

核心观点 · Key points

  • 如果设计师清楚知道自己要什么并能清晰描述，就能对 AI 输出拥有几乎完全的控制力。
    A designer can have almost total control over AI output if they know what they want and describe it clearly.
  • 真正的差异来自品味与判断力；AI 擅长接受指示，而不是自己进行设计。
    The real differentiators are taste and judgment; AI is good at taking direction, not at design by itself.
  • Figma 的使用比预期少得多，主要用于最后 20% 的打磨，比如阴影和渐变。
    Figma was used far less than expected, mostly for the final 20% of polish like shadows and gradients.
  • 用 AI 构建意味着要花上百小时处理边缘情况和反复迭代，而不是靠一次神奇的提示词。
    Building with AI meant hundreds of hours of edge cases and iterations, not a single miraculous prompt.
  • AI 工具让视觉设计师得以扩展到声音设计、动画、原型制作和图标设计。
    AI tools let visual designers expand into sound design, animation, prototyping, and icons.

反共识 · Contrarian takes

  • 在尝试之前，他以为 AI 意味着零控制；现在他发现它能带来超预期的控制力。
    Before trying it, he believed AI meant zero control; now he finds it gives more control than expected.
  • 他并没有从 Figma 开始，而是直接进入 Claude，只在最后 20% 才借助设计工具。
    Instead of starting in Figma, he jumped straight into Claude and only reached for design tools in the final 20%.
  • AI 依然不擅长设计，它只是执行来自有品味之人的明确指示。
    AI is still not good at design; it just executes clear direction from people with taste.
  • 你不必记住终端命令或理解 GitHub；你可以把每个操作都描述给 Claude。
    You don't need to memorize terminal commands or understand GitHub; you can describe every action to Claude.
  • 在网上高谈阔论 AI 工具的大多不是设计师，而有品味的设计师反而很少获得关注。
    Most people loudly talking about AI tools are not designers, while designers with taste are rarely spotlighted.
  • 一比一地复刻一个现有工具远比从零开始容易，因为原作者已经解决了各种边缘情况。
    Copying an existing tool is far easier than starting from scratch because the original creator already solved edge cases.
