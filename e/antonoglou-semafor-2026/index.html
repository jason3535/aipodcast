Reflection AI CTO Ioannis Antonoglou discusses the launch of the open-weight model Beam, token efficiency, and why deploying intelligence is becoming the real bottleneck.

  • 真正的瓶颈在于部署智能，而非创造智能。

Well, I mean, I guess AI means artificial intelligence, right? So it's kind of like the fact that it's not human intelligence. That's what it means. 学习与上下文 Learning and Context

Ioannis

嗯，我的意思是，既是也不是，因为那在某种意义上是同时成立的。这是因为有上下文，然后你有相同的模型但不同的上下文，对吧？你其实可以说人类也是如此——他们在不同的上下文之间不记得；你只是恰好有一个上下文，你的一生，所以你的一生就像一个大上下文、一个大会话、一个大交互。所以如果你真的把一个智能体的生命定义为它的上下文，那么一切都在那个上下文中发生。然后你只是让它知道不同生命之间的事情，这有点像编码在权重中的东西。所以你可以说我们有构成人类的结构部分，这有点像代际共享的，那就是我们的模型，然后我们有我们的寿命，就像我们的上下文，然后我们在这个上下文中学习行为，对吧？所以这有点像定义的问题。

Well, I mean, yes and no, in the sense that that's true at the same time. This is because there is the context and then you have the same model just with different contexts, right? And you can actually say that humans also—they don't remember between different contexts; you just happen to have one context, your life, so your whole life is like one big context, one big session, one big interaction. So if you actually define the life of an agent as that of its context, then everything happens in that context. And then you just ask it to know things between lives, which is kind of like what's encoded in the weights. So you can say that we have the structural part of what makes us human, which is kind of like shared between generations, and that's kind of our model, and then we have our lifetimes, which are like our context, and then we just learn behaviors in this context, right? So it's kind of like a matter of definition.

Ioannis

目前，因为上下文——即使是最长的上下文——也相当小。你可能有大约一百万个 token 之类的。所以那算长，但不足以让一个智能体运行数天或数年之类的。但如果你真的拥有那个，在某种程度上你可以说一切都在上下文中发生。所以上下文学习就是学习。

Currently, because the context is—even for the longest context—it's pretty small. You can have maybe one million tokens or something like that. So that's long, but it's not long enough to have an agent running for days or years or whatever it is. But if you actually had that, in a way you can say that then everything happens within context.
