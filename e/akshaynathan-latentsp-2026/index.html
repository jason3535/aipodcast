From No-Code to ChatGPT Work: The Era of Bottoms-Up Ambition

本期速览 · Overview

来自 OpenAI 的 Akshay 探讨了想法和品味如何成为新的瓶颈，以及 ChatGPT Work 如何体现将代码魔力带给每个人的使命。

Akshay from OpenAI discusses how ideas and taste are the new bottlenecks, and how ChatGPT Work embodies the mission of bringing code's magic to everyone.

要点 · TL;DR

  • 有了 AI，构建变得容易；真正的分水岭是品味和想法。
    With AI, building is easy; the differentiators are taste and ideas.
  • 简化 AI 选择：默认设置应适合大多数人，而不是提供无数选项。
    Simplify AI choices: default settings should fit most users, not force endless options.
  • 衡量明确目标，而非代理指标，才能避免把动作当成进展。
    Measure prescriptive goals, not proxy metrics, to avoid mistaking motion for progress.

核心观点 · Key points

  • OpenAI 的使命始终不变：构建 AGI（通用人工智能）并将前沿智能带给所有人，尽管路径非线性、产品不断演进。
    OpenAI's mission stays constant: build AGI and bring frontier intelligence to everyone, even though the path is nonlinear and products evolve.
  • OpenAI 内部非开发者对 Codex 的采用表明智能体能力不只属于程序员，这也促使 ChatGPT Work 将这种能力带给每个人。
    Non-developer adoption of Codex inside OpenAI showed agent power isn't just for coders, prompting ChatGPT Work to bring it to everyone.
  • ChatGPT Work 与 Codex 共用同一底层运行框架；差异只体现在界面、沙箱默认设置和展示方式，而非底层能力。
    ChatGPT Work and Codex share the same harness; only UX, sandbox defaults, and display choices differ, not underlying capabilities.
  • 站点和产物正成为知识工作协作的主流格式，以更灵活、更高带宽的输出取代幻灯片和电子表格。
    Sites and artifacts are becoming the canonical collaboration format for knowledge work, replacing slide decks and spreadsheets with more flexible, higher-bandwidth output.
  • 当人人都能用 AI 构建时，瓶颈转向创意与品味；能闭环用户反馈的通用型人才依然不可替代。
    When anyone can build with AI, the bottleneck shifts to ideas and taste; grounded generalists who close feedback loops remain essential.
  • 衡量生产力应看目标进展和高质量的“打席”，而非提交数、故事点或 token 等代理指标。
    Productivity should be measured by progress toward goals and quality at-bats, not proxies like commits, story points, or tokens.

反共识 · Contrarian takes

  • 尽管团队已扩展到数千人，OpenAI 依然比以往更像创业公司；最大的变化其实是没什么变化。
    Despite growth to thousands of people, OpenAI still feels more startup-like than ever; the biggest change is how little has changed.
  • 更多用户应选择 Terra 而非 Soul：除了成本更低，Soul 还会耗尽容量，轻量模型反而更应成为实际默认选项。
    More users should choose Terra over Soul; besides cost, Soul keeps exhausting capacity, making lighter models the practical default.
  • 模型能综合 Slack、代码和上下文，写出比我更好的绩效评估；但礼仪要求绝不能把纯 AI 生成的文本当作自己的输出。
    Models can draft better performance reviews than I can by pulling Slack, code, and context; etiquette: never present AI-only text as yours.
  • 连企业财务团队现在也用站点而非幻灯片或电子表格制作月报；灵活的产物正在取代传统办公套件。
    Even corporate finance teams now build monthly reports as sites instead of slide decks or spreadsheets; flexible artifacts are replacing office suites.
  • AI 不是替代人，而是让每个人都变成 T 型人才——既通又专——因此能做成的事几乎不受限制。
    Instead of replacing people, AI enables everyone to become T-shaped—both broad generalist and deep specialist—so what you can accomplish becomes nearly limitless.
  • 按角色划分的产品线正在消亡：代码、文档和战略策略融入同一个运行框架，把用户锁进不同应用反而有害。
    Role-based product lines are dead: code, docs, and strategy merge into one harness; boxing users into separate apps becomes harmful.
