本期速览 · Overview

库克在最后一次财报电话会上宣布，苹果六月季度营收 1094 亿美元，同比增长 16%，iPhone 和 Mac 均创纪录。

In his 90th and final earnings call, CEO Tim Cook reported a June-quarter record $109.4 billion revenue, up 16%, with iPhone and Mac hitting records despite supply constraints.

要点 · TL;DR

  • 苹果公布六月季度营收创纪录达 1094 亿美元，同比增 16%，每股收益 2.02 美元。
    Apple reported record June quarter revenue of $109.4B, up 16% YoY, with EPS $2.02.
  • 供给受限源于需求远超预期，内存成本飙升引发 iPad 和 Mac 价格上涨。
    Supply constraints are demand-driven; memory cost surge leading to price increases on iPad and Mac.
  • 苹果与博通合作投资超 300 亿美元布局美国芯片，并推进混合云 AI。
    Apple invests over $30B with Broadcom for U.S. silicon, ramping AI via hybrid cloud.

核心观点 · Key points

  • 六月季度营收 1094 亿美元，同比增长 16%，创纪录；每股收益 2.02 美元，同比增长 29%。
    June quarter revenue $109.4B, up 16% YoY, a record; EPS $2.02, up 29%.
  • iPhone 营收 543 亿美元，增长 22%；Mac 营收 104 亿美元，增长 29%，均创六月季度纪录。
    iPhone revenue $54.3B, up 22%; Mac $10.4B, up 29%, both June quarter records.
  • 服务营收 307 亿美元，增长 12%，创六月季度纪录；付费订阅超 15 亿。
    Services revenue $30.7B, up 12%, a June quarter record; paid subs surpass 1.5B.
  • 九月季度营收指引增长 9%-11%，受汇率和显著加剧的供应限制影响。
    September quarter revenue guided 9-11% growth, impacted by FX and significantly higher supply constraints.
  • Siri AI 推出获得压倒性积极反馈；AI 采用混合云与端侧模型。
    Siri AI rollout met with overwhelming positive feedback; hybrid cloud and on-device model for AI.

反共识 · Contrarian takes

  • 供应限制源于需求远超预期，而非供应商问题。
    Supply constraints are driven by demand far exceeding expectations, not supplier failures.
  • 内存成本呈指数级飙升，导致 iPad 和 Mac 史无前例地提价。
    Memory costs surging exponentially, causing unprecedented price increases on iPad and Mac.
  • 关税退款提振毛利率约 2 个百分点；调整后毛利率环比持平。
    Tariff refunds boosted gross margin by ~2 points; adjusted margin flat sequentially.
  • 尽管供应受限，Mac 仍增长 29%；教育需求正取代 Windows 和 Chromebook。
    Mac grew 29% despite supply constraints; education demand displacing Windows and Chromebooks.
  • 苹果与博通达成超 300 亿美元合作，扩展美国芯片供应链；亚利桑那工厂关键。
    Apple invests over $30B with Broadcom, expanding U.S. silicon supply chain; Arizona fab key.
