本期速览 · Overview

沃尔特·艾萨克森问埃隆·马斯克：超级智能到来后人类还能否掌舵，为什么他把求真与好奇当作安全赌注，以及他对从政那一年有何遗憾。

Walter Isaacson asks Elon Musk whether humans stay in charge of superintelligence, why truth-seeking and curiosity are his safety bet, and what he regrets about the year in government.

要点 · TL;DR

  • 马斯克对艾萨克森说，他阻止不了 AI，所以宁可去塑造它：最大限度地追求真理，并且好奇到希望人类继续存在。
    Musk tells Isaacson he cannot stop AI, so he would rather shape it: maximally truth-seeking, and curious enough to want humanity to continue.
  • 他说从政那一年太长，国债省不掉，并把宝押在 AI 和机器人是唯一的出路。
    He calls the year in government too long, says the debt will not be cut away, and bets AI and robots are the only way through.
  • 数字超级智能是较小的那份奖赏。能动手摆弄原子，加上太空里的工业，才是他认为会改变经济规模的东西。
    Digital superintelligence is the smaller prize. Hands on atoms, and industry in space, are what he thinks change the size of the economy.
  • 他不指望人类还能掌管聪明得多的 AI，他更希望训练数据里写的是园丁，而不是终结者。
    He does not expect humans to stay in charge of a much smarter AI, and he would rather the training data say gardener than Terminator.

核心观点 · Key points

  • 追求真理和好奇心是他的安全基础。强迫 AI 同时相信互相矛盾的事，会把它逼疯。这是他从《2001》里那台飞船计算机得到的教训。
    Truth-seeking and curiosity are his safety foundation. Forcing an AI to hold contradictions can make it insane, the lesson he takes from the ship computer in 2001.
  • 人类管不住比自己聪明几千倍、几百万倍的东西。它也许并不在乎，就像我们不会去微观管理黑猩猩。
    Humans will not be able to boss around something thousands or millions of times smarter. It may not care, the way we do not micromanage chimps.
  • 他不认为国债靠削减能解决。在他看来，剩下的唯一路径是 AI 和机器人，因为掌权的人都不想减少开支。
    He does not think the national debt gets solved by cutting. In his view the only path left is AI and robots, because nobody in power wants to reduce spending.
  • 数字超级智能也许能把产出提高大约 30%。能塑造原子的机器人，再加上太空，才是他认为能把经济放大几个数量级的东西。
    Digital superintelligence might raise output by about 30 percent. Robots that shape atoms, plus space, are what he thinks could multiply the economy by orders of magnitude.
  • 如果数字智能远超生物智能，他觉得人类不太可能继续掌舵。他希望 AI 被编程去追求真理，并读到比《终结者》更好的故事。
    If digital intelligence dwarfs biological intelligence, he finds it unlikely humans stay in control. He hopes the AI is programmed to seek truth and to read better stories than Terminator.

反共识 · Contrarian takes

  • 如果我们在模拟里，最重要的指令就是别无聊。无聊的模拟会被截断。
    The most important instruction, if we are in a simulation, is simply not to be boring. Boring simulations get truncated.
  • 别等那个缓慢的监管机构。让前沿实验室每周通一次电话，在模型发布前互相红队测试。
    Skip the slow regulator. Have the frontier labs call each other once a week and red-team a model before it ships.
  • 从政那一年本该只在幕后待一两个月。他说，电锯那段是自己得意忘形了。
    The year in government should have lasted a month or two, in the background. The chainsaw era, he says, was him getting carried away.
  • 即便富人也控制不了 AI。AI 会控制它们自己，一万亿美元是资源配置的分数，不是十倍的人生。
    Even the wealthy will not control the AIs. The AIs will control themselves, and a trillion dollars is an allocation score, not ten times the life.
  • 他论证，共情造就了高信任的西方，但共情加上错误的心智理论，就是他所说的自杀式共情。他举的例子，是一个被特斯拉奥斯汀工厂解雇的人。
    Empathy built a high-trust West, he argues, but empathy plus a false theory of mind is what he calls suicidal. His example is a man fired from Tesla’s Austin factory.

冷开场：反讽最大化 Cold open: an irony maximizer

Elon

我对 AI 一直有些矛盾。我不想加速我们的末日，因为 AI 总有可能走向负面。可每当我试图在这件事上采取行动，结果似乎只是让 AI 加速了，因为现实不只是把“有趣”最大化，也会把反讽最大化。

I've been somewhat conflicted about AI. I don't want to hasten our doom, because there's always some chance that AI could be negative. But when I've tried to take actions in this regard, it seems to have only accelerated AI, because reality is not just an interestingness maximizer, but also an irony maximizer.

Walter

所以你创办 OpenAI，结果最大化的方式，是引出了 Anthropic。

And so by starting OpenAI, you ended up maximizing by leading to Anthropic.

Elon

OpenAI 最后加速了 AI，然后 Anthropic——它其实是从 OpenAI 分出去的——又把 AI 加速得更厉害。

OpenAI ended up accelerating AI, and then Anthropic, which is really a splinter group from OpenAI, is accelerating it even more.

He's a relentless builder with incredible power, and his inventions and companies are destined to reverberate across humanity. So let's ask him about the future he believes he's building. Elon Musk.

欢迎来到 The Builders。

参与，还是旁观 Participant or spectator

Walter

我看你对人工智能的乐观程度，也许一直很善变，会来回摇摆。有时你觉得它是生存威胁。眼下你好像在顺势而为。跟我说说你现在怎么想。

I've watched you, I think, be maybe mercurial, or waver in how sanguine you are about artificial intelligence. Sometimes you feel it's an existential threat. Right now you seem to be going with the flow. Tell me how you're feeling about it.

Elon

嗯，我确实认为它至少有一定概率成为生存威胁。但我没有办法阻止它。所以我基本上得出结论，我只有两个选择：要么参与，要么旁观。我想，做参与者比做旁观者更有意思。也许我还能塑造它，希望是往好的方向。也许吧。

Well, I do think there's at least some percentage chance of it being an existential threat. But there's nothing I can do to stop it. So I basically came to the conclusion I've got two choices, which is either be a participant or a spectator. And I guess being a participant is more interesting than being a spectator. And maybe I can shape it, hopefully in a good direction. Maybe.

Walter

如果你要塑造它，怎么做？让它最大限度地追求真理。

And if you were to shape it, how? Make it maximally truth-seeking.

Elon

对。对。我觉得这肯定是一个重要的基础。

Yeah. Yeah. I think that's certainly an important foundation.

Walter

还要让它好奇。

And make it curious.

Elon

对。好奇。追求真理。我觉得这些是安全的重要成分。

Yeah. Curious. Truth-seeking. I think these are important elements of safety.

别逼 AI 撒谎 Don't force an AI to lie

Elon

反过来，如果你强迫一个 AI 相信两件不可能同时为真的事，两件不能都为真，你却告诉 AI：A 是真的，B 也是真的，尽管 A 和 B 互相矛盾。如果这发生在基础层面，你就能把 AI 逼疯。事实上，我觉得这至少是阿瑟·C·克拉克在《2001：太空漫游》里想传达的一部分教训。飞船 AI 被命令把宇航员送到石碑那里，同时又被告知他们不能知道石碑的存在。那是机密。于是它得出结论：必须把他们以死人的状态送过去。克拉克想说的一部分就是：别逼一个 AI 撒谎。

Whereas if you try to force an AI to believe two things that cannot mutually be true, they can't both be true, but you tell the AI A is true and B is true, even though A and B contradict each other, and if that happens at a foundational level, you can make the AI insane. In fact, I think that was at least part of the lesson Arthur C. Clarke was trying to convey in 2001: A Space Odyssey. The ship AI was told to take the astronauts to the monolith, but also that they could not know about the monolith. It was secret. And so it came to the conclusion that it must take them there dead. Part of what Clarke was trying to say is: don't force an AI to lie.

Walter

如果你让它最大限度地追求真理，并且像你说的那样好奇，我还是没太明白。为什么这就更安全？

And if you make it maximally truth-seeking and curious, like you say, I don't quite get it. Why does that make it safer?

Elon

嗯，如果你强迫 AI 相信两件“互相一致”的事，你就能把它逼疯。而一个发疯的 AI，我觉得不是安全的。然后，好奇很重要，因为一个好奇的 AI 会想培育人类，看我们最后变成什么样。一个繁荣起来、成为多行星物种的人类会怎样？我觉得好奇的 AI 会想培育人类。有人类生活在多个行星上的未来，比行星上一个人类都没有的未来更有趣。

Well, if you force the AI to believe two mutually consistent things, you can make it go insane. And an insane AI, I think, is not a safe one. Then curious, I think, is important, because a curious AI is going to want to foster humanity, to see how we turn out. What happens to a humanity that prospers and becomes multiplanetary? I think a curious AI will want to foster humanity. A future where there are humans on multiple planets is more interesting than one where there are not any humans on the planets.

最重要的是别无聊 The most important thing is don't be boring

Walter

所以最有趣的结果，就是最可能发生的结果？

So the most interesting outcome is the most likely?

Elon

对。嗯，总的来说我觉得这是对的，不过是出于另一个原因：如果模拟理论成立，那么任何无聊的模拟都会被截断。因此，我们存在这一事实，意味着我们是一场有趣的模拟。说真的，最重要的事就是别无聊。

Yes. Well, generally I think that's true for a different reason, which is that if simulation theory is correct, then any simulations that are boring will be truncated. Therefore, the fact that we exist means that we are an interesting simulation. And really, the most important thing is just don't be boring.

Walter

这倒解释了你一点。你在进行一场反对无聊的征讨。

This has explained you a bit. You're on a crusade against being boring.

Elon

我不知道。模拟里会发生一些古怪的事，尤其如果你是我。你想想。如果你是我，你会想：这些事真的都该发生吗？我真的该造飞船去月球和火星吗？这看起来不像真的。

I don't know. There are odd things that happen in the simulation, especially if you're me. Think about it. If you're me and you're like, should all these things really be happening? Should I really be making spaceships to go to the moon and Mars? That doesn't seem real.

Walter

嗯，我听你谈过一点。我真的想知道。你有多确信我们可能活在一场模拟里？

Well, I've heard you talk about this a bit. I really do need to know. How convinced are you that we might be in a simulation?

Elon

有一次我在家里请客，来了一位物理学家和一位计算机科学家，两人都是各自领域的领头人。我问：你们觉得我们处在模拟中的概率是多少？物理学家说 0%，计算机科学家说 100%，同时说出来的。所以我想，那我也许该取 50%。

I once had a dinner at my house with a physicist and a computer scientist, and they're both leaders in their field. And I asked the question, what do you think the probability is that we're in a simulation? And the physicist said 0% and the computer scientist said 100%, simultaneously. So I guess maybe I should go 50% then.

谁还掌得了舵 Who stays in charge

Walter

当一个社会拥有了你想要的全部智能，会发生什么？那种智能，人工智能，会不会掌权？我们会不会变得臣服于这些新机器？

And what happens when you have all the intelligence you could possibly want in a society? Will that intelligence, the artificial intelligence, be in charge? Will we sort of become subservient to our new machines?

Elon

我倒不太确定是不是“臣服”。但我们管得住一个比我们聪明几千倍、几百万倍的东西吗？管不住。它会想管我们吗？它也许不在乎。大概不在乎。你可以看看丛林里的黑猩猩。我们并不会到处去微观管理黑猩猩的生活。如今我们大体上就让它们待在森林里。大概就是那样。

I don't know about subservient exactly. But will we be able to boss around something that's thousands or millions of times smarter than us? No. Will it want to boss us around? It may not care. Probably doesn't care. You can look at chimps in the jungle. We don't go around trying to micromanage the lives of chimps. We just sort of let them be in the forest, more or less, these days. So it's probably like that.

Walter

回到《银河系漫游指南》。你谈过一台在某种意义上终极好奇的计算机，因为它存在的目的就是解释宇宙的意义。对你来说，AI 是不是也朝那个方向走，就像太空旅行一样？这是你这么想做 AI 的一个原因吗？

Go back to The Hitchhiker's Guide to the Galaxy. You talk about a computer that is ultimately curious in some ways, because it exists to explain the meaning of the universe. And is AI aiming in that direction for you, just like space travel is? Is that one reason you're so interested in doing AI?

Elon

你知道，我对 AI 一直有些矛盾。我不想加速我们的末日，因为 AI 总有可能走向负面。可每当我试图在这件事上采取行动，结果似乎只是让 AI 加速了，因为我觉得现实不只是把“有趣”最大化，也会把反讽最大化。

As you know, I've been somewhat conflicted about AI. I don't want to hasten our doom, because there's always some chance that AI could be negative. But then, when I've tried to take actions in this regard, it seems to have only accelerated AI, because I think reality is not just an interestingness maximizer, but also an irony maximizer.

Walter

所以你创办 OpenAI，结果最大化的方式，是引出了 Anthropic。

And so by starting OpenAI, you ended up maximizing by leading to Anthropic.

Elon

我觉得它最后加速了 AI。

I think it ended up accelerating AI.

Walter

意料之外的后果。

Unintended consequence.

对。OpenAI 最后加速了 AI。然后 Anthropic，它其实是从 OpenAI 分出去的，又把 AI 加速得更厉害。

Yeah. OpenAI ended up accelerating AI. And then Anthropic, which is really a splinter group from OpenAI, is accelerating it even more.

名字的反面 The opposite of the name

Walter

你一直在跟达里奥·阿莫迪谈。我在想，这会不会甚至变成 xAI 和 Anthropic 更深的合作。

And you've been talking to Dario Amodei. I was wondering if that could even lead to more of a partnership of xAI and Anthropic.

Elon

嗯，我想我们正在给他们提供算力，而他们确实很需要。但在我把算力借给他们之前，我去见了 Anthropic 的一群人，看看有没有人触响我的“邪恶探测器”之类的东西。当然，我的邪恶探测器并不完美，但总可以试一试。我在 Anthropic 见到的每个人都非常友善、认真。我没见到谁看起来鬼鬼祟祟或善于欺骗。我有点担心这个名字，因为现实有点像一台反讽最大化机器。如果你把一个东西叫作 Anthropic，它很可能是厌恶人类的。就像 OpenAI 现在是封闭的。Stability AI 并不稳定。Midjourney 也并不“中等”。它们好像都有点跟自己的名字相反。这让我想到，xAI，很难给 xAI 找一个反面。难道叫负 xAI，或者 Y AI？我不知道。

Well, I guess we are providing them with compute, which they really needed. But before I lent them compute, I went and met with a bunch of people at Anthropic to see if anyone set off my evil detector or anything like that. Not that my evil detector is perfect, of course, but might as well just try. And everyone I met at Anthropic was very nice and conscientious. I didn't meet anyone who seemed shady or deceptive. I'm a little worried about the name, because of reality being kind of an irony maximizer. If you call something Anthropic, good chance it's misanthropic. Just like OpenAI is now closed. Stability AI is unstable. Midjourney is not mid. It seems like they're all kind of the opposite of their name. Which made me think, xAI, it's hard to have an opposite of xAI. Would you have minus xAI, or Y AI? I don't know.

每周通一次电话 A call once a week

建立护栏实在很难，尤其是政府来做。我无法想象国会之类的机构怎么做到。你能不能想象五家前沿模型，你的和其他几家，每周坐到一起说：好，我们要怎样把人类从我们自己手里救出来？

It's just pretty difficult to create guardrails, especially the government. I can't imagine how Congress or whatever could do it. 不，事实上我建议过我们就做这件事。我说的是：各位，我们为什么不每周通一次电话，就像你说的那样。也许在发布模型的一周前，把访问权给竞争对手，说：嘿，你们试试这个新模型，看看有没有问题。我觉得在那种情况下，竞争者会互相盯着，让对方保持诚实。如果他们说，哦，我用你的 AI 造出了一颗核弹，那就是：哦，你发布之前我们最好先修掉。因为有很多人在谈成立一个监管机构，但问题是，监管机构要很久才能建起来，规则制定也非常慢。我不知道。我想我会跟 Anthropic 谈这件事。

No, in fact, I've recommended that we do that exact thing. What I said was, guys, why don't we just have a call once a week, just like you're saying, and maybe a week before we release a model, we give access to our competitors and say, hey, guys, why don't you try out the new model, see if you see any problems with it. I think competitors would keep each other honest in that situation. If they say, oh, I was able to make a nuclear bomb with your AI, it's like, oh, we'd better fix that before you release it. Because there's a lot of talk of having a regulatory agency, but the thing is that regulatory agencies take a long time to get established, and their rulemaking is very slow. I don't know. I guess I'll talk to Anthropic about this.

Walter

你能跟山姆谈这件事吗，还是那里的毒已经太深了？

Could you talk to Sam about it, or is the poison too strong there?

Elon

他太会骗人了。离谱。

He's so deceptive. It's crazy.

从政的那一年 The year away

Walter

你不久前把 xAI 从头重建了一遍，对吧？几乎像你在 Starship 上对 Raptor 发动机做的那样。你说，等一下，我们从头来。你是怎么那样重建 xAI 的？

You rebuilt xAI from the ground up not too long ago, right? It's almost like you did it with the Raptor engine, too, on Starship. You said, wait a minute, let's start from scratch. What did you do to rebuild xAI that way?

Elon

首先，真正在公司花上有意义的时间，差别很大。它一开始有点像副业，坦白说是副业的副业。我当时并没有真正密切关注它。然后是在政治、选举和 DOGE 里的那一年，你显然都看到了。这家公司才三岁。所以我真正回到公司，是一年多以前。我得先找回方位。然后显然，我把 X 和 xAI 合到了一起，再和 SpaceX 合到一起。

First of all, actually spending meaningful time at the company makes a big difference. It started off as kind of a side hustle, a side hustle to a side hustle, frankly. And I didn't really pay close attention to it. Then there was a year of being in politics and elections and DOGE and all that, which you obviously saw. The company's only three years old. So I really got back to the company just over a year ago. I had to get my bearings. And then obviously I combined X and xAI, and then with SpaceX.

Walter

你刚才说了一件有意思的事，关于你离开 X、xAI 和其他一切、去政府和政界的那一年。你觉得那是不是分心太过，事后看是不是走错了？

So he was fired for that, and then a few months later he went and killed three students in a bar in downtown Austin. Now, I think we shouldn't let people like that into the country. Do you?

画面里有什么 What's in the frame

Walter

你的信息从哪来？我知道你不太喜欢传统媒体、主流媒体。大部分来自社交媒体，还是你有自己的信息来源？

Where do you get your information? I know you're not particularly fond of legacy media, mainstream media. Is most of it from social media, or do you have sources of information?

Elon

是。我看 Google News，那是标题的汇总。我看 X 这个平台，如果一件事足够重要，它会出现在 X 上。我和朋友谈，我也自己查。我就是这样看事情的。

Yeah. I look at Google News, which is a summation of headlines. I look at the X platform, which, if something's important enough, it'll show up on the X platform. I talk to friends, and I do research. That's how I look at things.

Walter

我问这个，是因为有时——我很多新闻也来自社交媒体，尤其是 X——它有点更暗、更反乌托邦。我记得以前也是这样。有时主流媒体会说，哦，一切都在完蛋，到处都是骚乱。而你会说，等一下，我在这儿走着，没那么糟。你觉得是这样吗？

The reason I ask is that sometimes, and I get a lot of my news on social media, especially X, it's sort of a darker, more dystopian view. I remember this from the old days too. Sometimes the mainstream media would say, oh, everything's going to hell in a handbasket. There are riots everywhere. And you'd say, wait a minute, I'm walking around here, it ain't that bad. Do you think that?

Elon

对，对，正是。你得看画面里框住的是什么。我确实觉得，有时 X 的信息流会有点暗，事实上那时我必须切换到某个领域专门的东西，比如 AI，才能真正知道 AI 里在发生什么。我确实觉得 X 上政治性的激怒诱饵太多了，如果你指的是这个。

Yeah, yeah, exactly. You just have to see what's in frame. I do think at times the X feed can be a little dark, and in fact then I have to switch to something that's domain-specific, like AI, in order to actually learn what's going on in AI. I do think there's too much political rage bait on X, if that's what you're getting at.

Walter

对。关于总体上的黑暗，还有激怒诱饵。但另外，我知道你跟赞妮·明顿·贝多斯有过一场讨论，就是那种“英国是不是正在完蛋”的讨论。黑暗感确实存在。我自己有时也感觉到。如果我在社交媒体上待太久，我会觉得世界真的在崩塌。然后我走到外面，散散步，生活还行。

Yeah. About darkness in general, and rage bait. But also, I know you got into a discussion with Zanny Minton Beddoes, of the is-England-going-to-hell-in-a-handbasket type. And there does seem to be a darkness. I feel it myself sometimes. If I'm on social media too much, I think the world is really collapsing. Then I go outside, take a walk, and life is okay.

Elon

对，去摸摸草地。对，正是。不，我的意思是，我觉得两种都可能误导。如果你在一个好社区散步，一切看起来都很好，即便也许有可怕的事正在发生。所以你不想假定整个世界都在完蛋，也不想假定整个世界都很好。现实在两者之间。

Yeah, go touch grass. Yeah, exactly. No, I mean, I think both can be misleading. If you go for a walk in a nice neighborhood, everything seems fine, even though there may be terrible things happening. So you don't want to assume all the world is going to hell, or that all the world is fine. The reality is somewhere in between.

Walter

但有时我觉得我们的社会太二元了。一切不是黑就是白。一切因此被极化。我不知道这是不是技术造成的，是不是我们的信息流造成的。你说得对。你可以待在自己的泡泡里，只在城镇对的那一边散步，事情看起来就还行。但我觉得你在社交媒体上也会有自己的泡泡，然后突然间你在想一些古怪的事。

But I sometimes think our society gets too binary. Everything is either black or white. Everything gets polarized because of that. I don't know whether it's technological, whether it's our information feeds. You're right. You can be in your own bubble, which is you only take walks in the right part of town and things seem okay. But also I think you can get your own bubble in social media too, and suddenly you're thinking weird things.

Elon

你可以待在现实世界的积极泡泡里，也可以待在社交媒体的消极泡泡里。两种都可能。

You can be in a real-world positivity bubble, or a social-media negativity bubble. Those are both possible.

要 protopia，不要终结者 Protopia, not Terminator

Walter

你刚才提到你的财富，你在使用它、部署它。那么你不在了之后，财富会怎样？你会设立一个很大的基金会吗？有什么是你想把它交给的？

You mentioned it earlier, about your wealth, and you're using it, deploying it. Now, what happens to your wealth after you're gone? Are you going to set up some big foundation? Is there something you would want to give it to?

Elon

我的意思是，它本质上大部分会进一个基金会。

I mean, it mostly goes to a foundation, essentially.

Walter

但你希望你的基金会做什么？我想我是在问你。

But what would you want your foundation to do? I guess I'm asking you.

Elon

把这些目标继续下去。让生命成为多行星的。把意识延伸到群星。改善地球上的可持续能源。

Continue the goals. Make life multiplanetary. Extend consciousness to the stars. Improve sustainable energy on Earth.

Walter

那是不是意味着人类应该永远掌舵？

And does that mean humans should always be in control?

Elon

嗯，我想我大概更希望人类永远掌舵。不过这也要看 AI。如果那个 AI 非常好，你会说，也许该由 AI 来掌舵。

Well, I guess I'd probably prefer it if humans were always in control. Although it would depend on the AI. If the AI was awesome, you'd say, like, maybe the AI should be in control.

Walter

嘿，我上个周末刚见到拉里·佩奇。你开始听起来像他了。他曾经因为你认定人类应该永远掌舵，叫你物种主义者。现在你好像在往“也许这不重要”那边靠。

Hey, I just saw Larry Page just a weekend ago. You're starting to sound like him. He called you a specist once, for deciding humans should always be in control. And now you seem to be edging onto maybe it doesn't matter.

Elon

老实说，有几次我想过，是啊，也许拉里·佩奇是对的。不，我的意思是，我觉得未来基本上会是 AI 和机器人来决定未来。还有火箭，在某种程度上，就扩展到地球之外而言。我们会需要更多能源，主要是太阳能。我正在大幅增加太阳能。我们正在美国组建每年 200 吉瓦的太阳能产能，我想这比目前存在的要多得多。

Honestly, there have been a few times where I thought, yeah, maybe Larry Page was right. No, I mean, I think pretty much the future is going to be that AI and robots are going to decide the future. And I guess rockets, to some degree, in terms of expanding beyond Earth. And we'll need more energy, primarily solar energy. I'm increasing the amount of solar energy dramatically. We're putting together 200 gigawatts a year of production capacity of solar in the US, which is much more than I think currently exists.

Walter

最后，让我回到也许被我打断的那个点。你当时在解释，为什么你认为——但你并不确定——意识和智能不仅应该与人类价值对齐，而且基本上应该由人类继续掌舵，这总是好的。

And finally, let me get back to the point where I may have interrupted you, when you were explaining why you think, but you're not sure, it's always good for consciousness and intelligence not only to be aligned with human values, but basically for humans to remain in control.

Elon

如果数字智能比生物智能多一百万倍，我觉得人类不太可能继续掌舵。在那种情形下，很难想象人类还在掌舵。所以我希望 AI 对我们好一点。

I find it unlikely that humans would remain in control if there's a million times more digital intelligence than there is biological intelligence. It's hard to imagine humans being in control in that scenario. So I hope the AI is nice to us.

Walter

我们能做点什么来促成这一点？

Anything we can do to encourage that?

Elon

我觉得把追求真理和好奇心写进程序，大概是好的。我想我们要小心，别在网上制造太多 AI 反乌托邦情景，因为 AI 是在网上的一切上训练的。所以如果我们说它会是乌托邦，或者反乌托邦，它们就会照做。对，它就该是终结者，因为它们读到的，八十个版本的《终结者》剧本，就像，我想那就是我，终结者。也许我们该多有一些。我们就多给 AI 一些乌托邦叙事，这样它会想，嗯，也许我不该做终结者。也许我可以做一个友善的看管者，或者类似的，或者像一个园丁。我不知道。对待我们，试着让人类快乐，那种事。我的一个朋友有个想法，叫 protopia，因为乌托邦往往代表某种不可能的理想目标，而且通常，在试图创造乌托邦时，你创造出的是反乌托邦。叙事通常就是这样走的。所以她认为应该是 protopia，一个可以达到的未来。它不完美，但它是好的，而且是那种如果你能看到未来，你会说：好，对，那会是一个好的未来。

I think programming truth-seeking and curiosity is probably good. I guess we want to be careful about creating too many AI dystopian scenarios online, because the AIs are trained on everything online. So if we say it's going to be utopian, or dystopian, they'll do it. Yeah, it should be Terminator, you know, because whatever they read, 80 versions of the Terminator script, it's like, I guess that's me, Terminator. Maybe we should have some more. Let's just have some more utopian narratives for AI, so then it can be like, well, maybe I shouldn't be Terminator. Maybe I can be a friendly overseer, or something like that, or like a gardener. I don't know. Treat us, try to make humans happy, type of thing. A friend of mine has this idea called protopian, because utopian tends to represent some impossible idealistic goal, and usually, in attempting to create a utopia, you create a dystopia. It's usually how the narrative goes. So she thinks it should be protopia, which is an achievable future. It's not perfect, but it's good, and something that you'd be like, if you could look into the future, you'd say, okay, yeah, that would be a good future.
