本期速览 · Overview

Typeface 与 Jev 创始人 Diogo Almeida 解析为何可靠性而非能力是 AI 最大瓶颈，以及为何数据比算力更重要。

Typeface and Jev founder Diogo Almeida explains why reliability, not raw capability, is AI's biggest bottleneck and why data matters more than compute.

要点 · TL;DR

  • AI 最大的瓶颈是判断的可靠性而非正常运行时间；模型方差很大。
    AI's biggest bottleneck is reliability of judgment, not uptime; models are high-variance.
  • 数据质量和任务选择比算力更重要；基准测试有误导性。
    Data quality and task choice matter more than compute; benchmarks mislead.
  • AI 存在泡沫；过度承诺与交付不足将导致需求侧破裂。
    AI is a bubble; expect a demand-side burst from overpromise and underdelivery.

核心观点 · Key points

  • 智能的可靠性，而非正常运行时间，是AI自动化最大的瓶颈。
    Reliability of intelligence, not uptime, is the biggest bottleneck to AI automation.
  • 数据比算力更重要；正确的任务是最重要的。
    Data matters more than compute; the right task is the most important thing.
  • 当前模型方差很大：95%的时间是爱因斯坦，5%是憨豆先生。
    Current models are high variance: Einstein 95% of the time, Mr. Bean 5%.
  • 基准测试具有误导性；决策中的真正可靠性才是关键。
    Benchmarks are misleading; real reliability in decision-making is what matters.
  • AI一直存在泡沫；由于过度承诺和交付不足，需为需求侧破裂做准备。
    AI has been a bubble; plan for a demand-side burst due to overpromise and underdelivery.
  • 软件研究——结合AI与软件创造新界面——被严重低估，这很可悲。
    Software research—combining AI and software for new interfaces—is tragically underhyped.

反共识 · Contrarian takes

  • 大多数新实验室极其浪费；他们的商业模式是招聘、获取GPU、然后被收购。
    Most neolabs are incredibly wasteful; their business model is hire, get GPUs, get acquired.
  • AI同事是我们时代的机械马；自动化不应模仿人类。
    AI coworkers are the mechanical horses of our era; automation should not mimic humans.
  • 通过专有模型拥有自己的智能往往是错误的；软件才是护城河。
    Owning your own intelligence via proprietary models is often a mistake; software is the moat.
  • 由于伦理约束，美国开源模型可能无法赶上中国模型。
    American open source models likely won't catch up to Chinese ones due to ethical constraints.
  • 炒作的重要性远低于我的想象；少承诺多交付才是赢家。
    Hype matters much less than I thought; underpromise and overdeliver wins.
  • 机器人是最被高估的领域；它离可靠性最远。
    Robotics is the most overhyped category; it's furthest from reliability.
