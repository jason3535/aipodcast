本期速览 · Overview

ChatGPT 核心研究者、Typesafe AI CEO Diogo Almeida 剖析大模型为何难以实现自动化，并介绍系统一模型 Jev 如何重新定义可靠 AI 决策的路径。

Diogo Almeida, co-creator of ChatGPT and CEO of Typesafe AI, explains why LLMs fail at automation and how Jev, a system-one model, rethinks the path to reliable AI decisions.

要点 · TL;DR

  • 在 AI 成功中，任务选择比数据、算力和算法都更重要。
    Task choice beats data, compute, and algorithms in AI success.
  • 大模型擅长辅助却难以自动化；真正的自动化需要结构化类型。
    LLMs assist but fail at automation; structured types needed for real automation.
  • 基准测试不可靠；软件的金色时代可能才刚开始，而非结束。
    Benchmarks are broken; software's golden age may be ahead, not over.

核心观点 · Key points

  • 做对任务比数据更重要，数据比算力更重要，算力比算法更重要。
    Doing the right task matters more than data, which matters more than compute, which matters more than algorithms.
  • 大语言模型是为辅助而非自动化优化的，因此在真正的自动化上基本无用。
    LLMs are optimized for assistance, not automation, so they are basically useless at real automation.
  • 前沿是参差不齐的：模型能解难题数学，却搞不定免下车点餐和会计。
    The frontier is jagged: models solve hard math but fail at drive-throughs and accounting.
  • 字符串生成对软件极低效；自动化需要结构化语义类型来驱动条件分支。
    String generation is inefficient for software; automation needs structured semantic types feeding if statements.
  • AI 主要让软件写得更便宜更快，但软件本身基本没变。
    AI mostly makes software cheaper and faster to write, but software itself is basically unchanged.
  • 真正的赢家将是那些懂得哪些任务值得自动化的人。
    The real winners will be those who understand which tasks are worth automating.

反共识 · Contrarian takes

  • 基准测试很糟糕，只利于基准制定者和有钱的实验室；永远别信它们。
    Benchmarks are horrid and favor benchmark makers and well-funded labs; never trust them.
  • 软件已死的叙事本身已经死了；我们可能正进入软件黄金时代。
    The narrative that software is over is itself over; we may be entering a golden age of software.
  • 参差不齐可能源于强迫系统一模型在数学上做系统二思考。
    Jaggedness may stem from forcing system-one models to be system-two thinkers in math.
  • 人类在环是优化的产物，而非 AI 运作方式的内在属性。
    The human in the loop is an artifact of optimization, not intrinsic to how AI works.
  • ChatGPT 过度承诺、交付不足；它并未真正自动化多少知识工作。
    ChatGPT is overpromise and underdeliver; it has not really automated much knowledge work.
  • 不该强迫大多数人使用 Jev；理解工具空间本身更重要。
    Most people should not be forced to use Jev; understanding the tool space matters more.
