Replit CEO Amjad Masad 认为，年轻人的真正价值在于质疑根深蒂固的观念、成为异端，大学应培养第一性原理思维，而非规定学习内容。

Replit CEO Amjad Masad argues that the true value of young people is to question deeply held beliefs and become heretics, and that college should foster first-principles thinking rather than prescribe what to learn.
