Elon Musk discusses record Q2 deliveries, surging FSD demand, energy business growth, and cautious Robo Taxi scaling.

要点 · TL;DR

  • 特斯拉无人出租车车队完成 38 万英里无监督行驶，零事故，验证了纯视觉方案。
    Tesla's robotaxi fleet achieved 380k unsupervised miles with zero incidents, validating camera-only autonomy.
  • 擎天柱量产因无现成供应链而极其困难，需完全自建。

核心观点 · Key points

  • 特斯拉无人驾驶出租车车队已行驶38万英里，零重大事故，验证了纯视觉自动驾驶方案。
    Tesla's robotaxi fleet has driven 380,000 unsupervised miles with zero notable incidents, validating camera-only autonomy.
  • Optimus的量产因无现有供应链而极其困难，需要完全自建。
    Optimus production scaling is extremely difficult due to no existing supply chain, requiring full in-house buildout.
  • 特斯拉正处于大规模资本支出周期，2026年超250亿美元，未来2-3年将持续增长。
    Tesla is in a massive capex cycle, spending over $25 billion in 2026, with capex growing for next 2-3 years.
  • FSD是主要需求驱动力；北美55%的交付车辆在交付时已启用FSD订阅。
    FSD is a major demand driver; 55% of North American deliveries had FSD subscription enabled at delivery.
  • 能源存储业务长期毛利率将稳定在20%中低水平，数据中心和电气化带来强劲订单积压。
    Energy storage margins normalized to mid-to-low 20% long-term, with robust order backlog from data centers and electrification.
  • 无人驾驶出租车增长呈指数级，周里程两位数增长，将扩展至全州范围。
    Robotaxi growth is exponential, with week-over-week double-digit mileage growth, expanding to entire states.

反共识 · Contrarian takes

  • 特斯拉无人驾驶出租车安全记录（38万英里零事故）反驳了专家认为需要激光雷达和高精地图的观点。
    Tesla's robotaxi safety record (380k miles, zero incidents) contradicts expert claims that LiDAR and HD maps are necessary.
  • Optimus将是量产难度最大的产品，甚至超过电动汽车，因为完全没有供应链。
    Optimus will be the hardest product to scale manufacturing, even harder than electric cars, due to zero supply chain.
  • 特斯拉计划自建AI芯片半导体工厂，这是一项高风险赌注，可能重塑芯片供应格局。
    Tesla plans to build its own semiconductor fab for AI chips, a high-risk bet that could reshape chip supply.
  • 星链集成到车辆中对无人驾驶出租车连接至关重要，因为蜂窝网络覆盖常常不可靠。
    Starlink integration in vehicles is critical for robotaxi connectivity, as cellular coverage is often unreliable.
  • 特斯拉的资本支出效率“高得离谱”，尽管尽可能快速支出，但优先考虑速度而非资本效率。
    Tesla's capex efficiency is 'off the charts' despite spending as fast as possible, prioritizing speed over capital efficiency.
  • 仅靠电池就能通过平滑峰值需求使美国能源容量翻倍，挑战新建发电厂的必要性。
    Batteries alone could double US energy capacity by smoothing peak demand, challenging the need for new power plants.
