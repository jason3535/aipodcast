本期速览 · Overview

StarCloud 的 Philip Johnston 与 Odyssey 的 Oliver Cameron 畅谈以极致速度创业，从太空数据中心到基础世界模型。

Philip Johnston of StarCloud and Oliver Cameron of Odyssey discuss building companies at extreme speed, from space-based data centers to foundation world models.

要点 · TL;DR

  • 公司几乎不会死于他杀，而是死于自杀；内部失败是主因。

  • AI 发展速度是以往的 10 倍；相信直觉比等待完美数据更有效。
    AI development is 10x faster than ever; trusting intuition beats waiting for perfect data.
  • 最大的 AI 风险是 AI 控制 AI；广泛探索是构建世界模型的关键。
    Biggest AI risk is AIs controlling AIs; broad exploration is key for world models.

核心观点 · Key points

  • 公司几乎从不因外部谋杀而死，而是死于自杀；内部失败是主因。
    Companies almost never die by murder, but by suicide; internal failures are the main cause.
  • 创始人必须有意识地塑造公司文化和沟通方式，以影响结果。
    Founders must be intentional about company culture and communication to shape outcomes.
  • 相信直觉并坚定行动，往往胜过等待完美数据。
    Trusting intuition and acting with conviction often beats waiting for perfect data.
  • AI 发展和竞争的速度比以往任何时候都快 10 倍。
    The pace of AI development and competition is 10x faster than ever before.
  • 初创公司通过质疑假设和第一性原理思考取得成功。
    Startups succeed by questioning assumptions and thinking from first principles.
  • 世界模型需要跨行业的广泛探索，而非狭窄聚焦。
    World models require broad exploration across industries, not narrow focus.

反共识 · Contrarian takes

  • AI 的最大风险是 AI 控制其他 AI，而非人类作恶。
    The biggest AI risk is AIs controlling other AIs, not rogue humans.
  • 费米悖论意味着智能生命短暂；灾难很可能在等着我们。
    The Fermi paradox implies intelligent life is short-lived; a calamity likely awaits us.
  • 不聚焦——广泛探索——是基础世界模型的刻意策略。
    Not focusing—exploring broadly—is a deliberate strategy for foundation world models.
  • 尽管拥有核武器，人类在避免自我毁灭方面表现得出奇地好。
    Humans have been remarkably good at not killing ourselves entirely, despite nuclear weapons.
  • 昨天的专家不是明天的专家；新鲜视角往往能赢。
    Yesterday's experts are not tomorrow's experts; fresh perspectives often win.
  • 以 1000-1500 万美元发送航天器到半人马座阿尔法星是可行的，且资金不足。
    Sending a spacecraft to Alpha Centauri for $10-15M is feasible and underfunded.

我的观点是，风险比几乎任何人认为的都高得多。这是因为费米悖论的想法。我们看不到智能生命，因此智能生命在我们银河系中可能至少是短暂的。但话虽如此，这并不意味着你真的能对此做太多。这似乎是我们之前所有文明不可避免的命运，也可能将是我们之后所有文明不可避免的命运。所以，你知道，有一种论点说：‘好吧，那我们就好好度过接下来几百年，看看之后会怎样。’是的，这可能有点虚无主义，但……

So my opinion is that the risk is way higher than almost anybody else. And it's because of this Fermi paradox idea. We don't see intelligent life and therefore probably intelligent life is short-lived in our galaxy at least. So but having said that, that doesn't mean there's really too much you could do about it. It seems like the inevitable fate of all civilizations before us and probably will be the inevitable fate of all civilizations after us. So, you know, there's an argument to be made that says, 'Okay, well, let's just have a good next couple of hundred years maybe and let's see where we end up after that.' Yeah, it's a bit nihilistic maybe, but...

Philip

我认为这个问题的答案是否定的，但我不认为其他人不这么想。我认为我们会大致调整安全投资的规模。不过实际上，我经常听到的讨论是，人类本身才是我们不应该信任高级 AI 的原因。如果我们拥有高级 AI，人类实际上将是这一切终结的原因。就像某个 rogue 人类会拿走一堆 AI，在世界上做出可怕的事情，这就是为什么它危险。实际上我认为作为人类，我们一直很了不起——一方面，是的，我们造成了很多破坏、很多战争、很多可怕的事情。另一方面，我们似乎找到了完全停止自我毁灭的方法，对吧？人类这个物种。我们拥有核武器已经很长时间了，但我们仍然在这里。

I think that the answer to the question is no, but I don't think that others don't think that. I think we'll sort of rightsize the amount of investment in safety. I actually though I think the discourse I hear a lot is that humans themselves are the thing that we shouldn't trust with advanced AI. That if we have advanced AI, humans are actually going to be the reason that all of this ends. Like some rogue human is going to take a bunch of AIs and is gonna do something horrible in the world and that's why it's dangerous. I actually I think as humans we have been remarkable about—on one hand yes we cause lots of damage, lots of war, lots of horrible things. On the other hand, we seem to find a way to stop killing ourselves like entirely, right? The human species. We've had nukes for a long time and yet we're still here.

AI风险：AI控制AI AI Risk: AIs Controlling AIs

Philip

所以其实我并不认为人类能接触到 AI 就一定是最大的风险。我认为 AI 拥有 AI 才是更大的风险。先别管人类——光是 AI 能够控制其他 AI，以及 AI 向物理世界和虚拟世界扩散，我认为风险就在这里。所以是的，在我看来那才是大得多的挑战，我们应该停止过度执迷于某个失控的人类造成这一切，而应该把注意力放在 AI 自身造成难以估量的破坏上。

And so I actually don't think humans having access to AIs is necessarily the biggest risk. I think just AIs having AIs is the bigger risk. Just forget humans — just AIs being able to control other AIs, and the proliferation of AIs into the physical world and virtual world. I think that's where the risk is. And so yeah, that would seem to me to be the dramatically bigger challenge, and we should stop over-obsessing about a rogue human causing all this and instead just focus on the AIs causing untold damage by themselves.

最大的冒险 Biggest Risk Taken

Philip

我们冒的最大的风险，是我们在 Odyssey 刻意选择不聚焦。创业的时候，所有人都告诉你聚焦、聚焦、聚焦，只做一件事，把它做到极致。过去 12 个月我们非常刻意地说不——世界模型需要探索。你需要铺得很广，需要进入很多不同的行业，去证明这是一个基础模型。不这么做是非常诱人的，因为那样痛苦得多，但我很高兴我们这么做了。你呢？

The biggest risk that we've taken is that we are intentional at Odyssey about not focusing. So building a company, everyone tells you focus, focus, focus. Do one thing, do it really well. We've been very intentional the last 12 months to say no — world models need exploration. You need to go very broad. You need to go to lots of different industries. Prove that this is a foundation model. And it's been very tempting to not do that because it's dramatically more painful, but I'm very glad we did. What about you?
