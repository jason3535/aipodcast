引言与单人创业可能性 Introduction and Possibility of Solo Entrepreneurship

Host

在 AI 时代，任何人都能成为亿万富翁企业家，独自创办一家公司。你觉得现在可能吗？

In the AI age, anyone can become a hundred million dollar entrepreneur, build a company solo. Do you think it is possible now?

Mike

我认为非常可能。就拿我在 Instagram 的经历来说，最初很多工作只有我和凯文两个人做，我们两个人就能完成很多事。当只有一两个人专注做某件事时，会有一种专注力和能量。我觉得理想状态其实是两个人，因为在经历起起落落时有个伙伴很有帮助。但我学到的是，随着公司成长，每增加一个团队成员，就多一个人带来自己的想法和能量，这很棒。但如果你需要调整公司方向，你也需要让这个人跟上。所以现在让我很兴奋的是，一两个人或三个人的团队可以自我扩展，比以前能做更多事，可能还更快，并且保持那种我所说的“概念完整性”。就像你把公司所有重要的东西都装在自己脑子里，或者两个人脑子里，一起协作，而不是试图驾驭一艘大船。

I think it's very possible. Just even watching the journey I went on with Instagram where we did a lot of the initial work with just me and Kevin and we were able to do a lot with just the two of us. There's a focus and an energy when you have just one or two people working on something. I think the ideal is actually two because it's helpful having a partner when going through the ups and downs. But what I've learned is as you grow, every person you add to the team is another person that can bring their own ideas and bring their energy, which is great. But it's also another person that you need to get on board if you need to shift where the company is going. So what I think is very exciting now is that one, two, three person team can scale themselves up, do a lot more than they would have been able to do before, maybe do it faster, and preserve that kind of conceptual integrity. Like you have all of what matters about that company in your head or in two heads basically working together versus trying to steer a huge ship.

技术技能的重要性 Importance of Technical Skills

I think with increasing things like Claude Code and products online, you're getting to the point where you're able to at least get to a version of your idea where you can see if there's something there. So what I think is exciting is even if the tools aren't there to get you from your first 10 users to your first million users in terms of the system scaling up, they'll at least get you in front of those first 10 people. Get you to the prototype or even that initial version. And that seems very possible even for people who aren't technical. And that's maybe where you might want to step back and say there's probably energy better spent somewhere else. Like the way it's not scientific, but when you feel like one unit of input you're getting 10 units of output, that's a good feeling. When it feels like 10 units of input, you're getting one of output, it's time to pivot to something else.
