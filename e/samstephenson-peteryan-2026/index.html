Sam, co-founder of Granola, shares how he uses AI meeting briefs, recipes, and agents to stay calm and prepared between back-to-back meetings.

要点 · TL;DR

  • Granola 是一款冷静的会议助手，隐藏转录并优先提供会前简报。
    Granola is a calm meeting assistant that hides transcripts and prioritizes pre-meeting briefs.

    Meetings hold the most valuable company context, so Granola evolves into a contextual platform.

    Work-life balance and attention to detail after shipping improve quality, not just speed.
