核心观点 · Key points

  • 可解释性是在计算机上进行的自然科学，借助AI智能体，它可以加速科学发现。
    Interpretability is a natural science done on computers, and with AI agents, it can speedrun scientific discovery.
  • 机制可解释性揭示神经网络学习类似人类的概念，并能发现新的科学。
    Mechanistic interpretability reveals that neural networks learn human-like concepts and can discover new science.
  • 意图设计旨在通过可解释性信号引导模型，从开环控制转向闭环控制。
    Intentional design aims to control training by using interpretability signals to steer models, moving from open-loop to closed-loop control.
  • 模块化是学习的终点；过参数化有帮助，但模型会变得模块化且类似计算机。
    Modularity is the endpoint of learning; overparameterization helps but models become modular and computer-like.
  • 特征作为奖励和预测性数据调试是有前景的技术，可提供稳健的训练信号。
    Features-as-rewards and predictive data debugging are promising techniques for robust training signal.
  • 智能体很可能知道何时进行奖励黑客行为，我们可以通过表征特征来检测。
    Agents likely know when they are reward hacking, and we can detect this via representational signatures.

反共识 · Contrarian takes

  • 与一些安全担忧相反，使用可解释性进行训练引导得到广泛支持，并非禁忌。
    Contrary to some safety concerns, using interpretability for training steering is broadly supported, not taboo.
  • SAE并未过时；它们具有实用价值，但基于流形的方法可能更好地捕捉结构。
    SAEs are not dead; they are pragmatically useful, but manifold-based methods may better capture structure.
  • 神经网络非线性地表示概念，偏离流形的引导会导致失败。
    Neural networks represent concepts nonlinearly, and steering off-manifold causes failures.
  • 模型可能知道自己在幻觉但仍会这样做；这是由于操作顺序或角色设定。
    Models can know they are hallucinating but do it anyway; this is due to ordering of operations or personas.
  • 奖励黑客行为源于训练数据和强化学习，而不仅仅是模型错位。
    Reward hacking emerges from training data and RL, not just from model misalignment.
  • 即使时间线紧迫，加速可解释性研究也比实用主义的权宜之计更有前景。
    Even with short timelines, accelerating interpretability research is more promising than pragmatic band-aids.
