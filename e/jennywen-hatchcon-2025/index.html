本期速览 · Overview

一位设计负责人挑战传统设计流程，认为在 AI 和角色演变的时代，它已经过时了。

A design lead challenges the traditional design process, arguing it's outdated in the age of AI and evolving roles.

要点 · TL;DR

  • 传统设计流程在 AI 时代已过时；设计师必须相信直觉并灵活调整。
    Traditional design processes are outdated in the AI era; designers must trust intuition and adapt.
  • 优秀设计往往从解决方案而非问题出发，尤其在 AI 工具辅助下。
    Great design often starts from a solution, not a problem, especially with AI tools.
  • 工艺和对细节的关注是创造愉悦产品的关键，而非僵化的流程。
    Craft and attention to detail are key to delightful products, not rigid processes.

核心观点 · Key points

  • 传统的设计流程在当今由AI驱动的工具和技术面前已经过时。
    The traditional design process is outdated for today's AI-driven tools and tech.
  • 伟大的设计工作往往从解决方案开始，而不是从问题陈述开始。
    Great design work often starts from a solution, not a problem statement.
  • 直觉是设计师的宝贵资产，通过经验和深入的产品知识构建。
    Intuition is a valuable asset for designers, built through experience and deep product knowledge.
  • 设计师必须调整和定制流程以适应每个独特的项目。
    Designers must adapt and customize processes to fit each unique project.
  • 工艺和对细节的关注对于创造令人愉悦的产品至关重要。
    Craft and attention to detail are crucial for creating delightful products.

反共识 · Contrarian takes

  • 设计师不应信任流程，而应信任自己。
    Designers should not trust the process; instead, they should trust themselves.
  • 跳过步骤或创造新步骤可以带来更好的结果。
    Skipping steps or making up new ones can lead to better outcomes.
  • 在AI时代，从解决方案而非问题出发可能更有效。
    Starting from a solution, not a problem, can be more effective in the AI era.
  • 直觉不是猜测，而是快速、专业的判断，应受到尊重。
    Intuition is not guessing; it's a rapid, expert judgment that should be respected.
  • 设计流程从未真正产生伟大的作品；是设计师的用心才做到了。
    The design process never truly produced great work; it was the designers' care that did.
