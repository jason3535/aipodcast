核心观点 · Key points

  • 让想法变得真实是设计师的核心机会，可在影视、代码或简单工具中任选。
    Making ideas feel real is the designer's core opportunity, choosing film, code, or simple tools as needed.
  • 保持一份俏皮、夸张的想法清单并频繁画草图，能激发源源不断的创意。
    Keeping a running list of playful, exaggerated ideas and sketching frequently fuels creative output.
  • 原型制作是检验想法、说服领导层和开展研究的高效方式。
    Prototyping is effective for testing ideas, persuading leadership, and running research studies.
  • 公开发布作品风险低、回报高；即使失败也能学到东西。
    Sharing work publicly has low downside and high upside; you always learn something.
  • 可见度是很有价值的货币，无论在社交媒体还是在组织内部。
    Visibility is a powerful currency, both on social media and inside an organization.
  • 努力保真度比视觉保真度更重要；只需把核心面向做真实。
    Effort fidelity matters more than visual fidelity; only make the essential aspects feel real.

反共识 · Contrarian takes

  • 空间设计不一定要用重型工具；手机拍的视频加手绘覆盖层就够。
    Spatial design doesn't require heavy tools; low-fi phone videos with drawn overlays can work.
  • 工具应当与想法相互影响；让媒介反过来塑造结果。
    Tools should inform ideas as much as ideas inform tools; let the medium shape the outcome.
  • 早期就给出高保真原型，能比低保真概念得到更明确的反馈。
    High-fidelity prototypes early can yield clearer feedback than low-fidelity concepts.
  • 反向使用“疯狂八格法”——画隐喻与观察，而不是解决方案。
    Use the crazy eights method backwards—sketch metaphors and observations, not solutions.
  • 粗头笔草图有意避免细节，迫使你聚焦核心交互。
    Fat-marker sketches intentionally prevent detail, forcing focus on core interaction.
  • 一个俏皮、非功能性的想法，即使不实用也能有力传达。
    A playful, non-functional idea can communicate powerfully without being practical.
