Spatial Design Experiments: Making Ideas Feel Real

本期速览 · Overview

微软设计师 Ryan Steven 分享如何利用 After Effects 和 Blender 等工具打造令人惊艳的空间设计实验，将未来概念变为现实。

Microsoft designer Ryan Steven shares how he creates striking spatial design experiments using tools like After Effects and Blender to bring futuristic ideas to life.

要点 · TL;DR

  • 让想法变得真实是设计师的核心机会，可根据需要选用电影、代码等工具。
    Making ideas feel real is the core opportunity for designers, using whatever tools from film to code.
  • 投入的保真度比视觉保真度更重要，只需让关键部分显得真实。
    Effort fidelity matters more than visual fidelity; only make essential aspects feel real.
  • 公开分享作品利大于弊；可见性是一种强大的货币。
    Sharing work publicly has low downside and high upside; visibility is powerful currency.

核心观点 · Key points

  • 设计师的核心工作是让想法变得真实，使用任何必要的工具——从电影制作到代码——去表达它们。
    A designer's core job is to make ideas feel real, using whatever tools—from filmmaking to code—are needed to express them.
  • 工具与想法相互塑造；可以从宏大的构想出发，但让工具指引你找到可行且最小化的表达。
    Tools and ideas shape each other; start grand but let the tool guide you to the minimum idea that works.
  • 在好玩、夸张的概念与务实的产品工作之间来回切换，能让两个领域都产出最好的成果。
    Zigzagging between playful, exaggerated concepts and practical product work creates the best work in both worlds.
  • 在网上分享实验能积累社群和可见度；最坏的结果只是没人在意，而潜在收益巨大。
    Sharing experiments online builds community and visibility; the worst case is nothing happens, while upside is huge.
  • 努力保真度比视觉保真度更重要；要判断想法的哪一部分真正需要被做得真实。
    Effort fidelity matters more than visual fidelity; decide which part of the idea actually needs to feel real.
  • 要根据受众定制原型和故事；一个可用的原型可以取代幻灯片，让人们直接评估作品本身。
    Tailor prototypes and stories to your audience; a working prototype can replace slides and let people evaluate the thing itself.

反共识 · Contrarian takes

  • 空间视频并不一定需要重型 3D 工具；用手机拍一段素材，再在 Figma 里直接画上去可能就足够了。
    Spatial videos don't require heavy 3D tools; filming on a phone and drawing over it in Figma can be enough.
  • 最快或最简单的原型制作方法往往优于“正确”的方法；你可以伪造缓动曲线，依然能让观众沉浸其中。
    The fastest or easiest prototype method is often better than the 'correct' one; you can fake ease curves and still suspend disbelief.
  • 为了对齐而刻意保持低保真被高估了；尽早展示一个精致的原型，得到的是具体反馈，而不是泛泛的点头赞同。
    Keeping fidelity low for alignment is overrated; showing a slick prototype early gives specific feedback instead of vague agreement.
  • “疯狂八格”可以反向使用：画出日常中有趣的隐喻和夸张的观察，而不是用来解决一个明确问题。
    Crazy Eights works backwards: sketch funny everyday metaphors and exaggerated observations rather than solving a defined problem.
  • 并非每个想法都应该被落地为产品；好玩、不严肃的概念依然能磨炼品味，并打开意想不到的方向。
    Not every idea should be shipped; playful, unserious concepts can still sharpen your taste and open unexpected directions.
  • “氛围编码”工具让设计师能够融合代码与设计；你不需要很深的编程背景，也能构建出真实感的交互。
    Vibe-coded tools let designers blend code and design; you don't need a deep programming background to build real-feeling interactions.
