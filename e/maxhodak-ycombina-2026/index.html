Startup Infrastructure: Lessons from Deep Tech

本期速览 · Overview

Max Hodak 分享了在深度科技初创公司中管理基础设施的见解，从采购设备到预算控制。

Max Hodak shares insights on managing infrastructure in deep tech startups, from buying equipment to budgeting.

要点 · TL;DR

  • 速度由基础设施决定，更快的迭代循环能克服许多挑战。
    Speed is determined by infrastructure, and faster iteration loops overcome many challenges.
  • 招聘应利用人脉，采用公司全员投票的结构化流程，以避免瓶颈。
    Hiring should leverage networks and use structured processes with company-wide voting to avoid bottlenecks.
  • 绩效评估可通过基于特征向量中心性的连续图反馈来改进。
    Performance reviews can be improved with continuous, graph-based feedback using eigenvector centrality.

核心观点 · Key points

  • 速度决定成败，而速度由基础设施决定。
    Speed determines success or failure, and speed is determined by infrastructure.
  • 迭代速度区分成功与失败；更快的迭代循环能克服许多其他挑战。
    Rate of iteration separates success from failure; faster iteration loops overcome many other challenges.
  • 招聘应利用人脉网络，并通过公司范围内的投票等结构化流程来避免瓶颈。
    Hiring should leverage networks and use structured processes with company-wide voting to avoid bottlenecks.
  • 通过基于图论的特征向量中心性进行持续反馈，可以改进绩效评估。
    Performance reviews can be improved with continuous, graph-based feedback using eigenvector centrality.
  • 初创公司应比通常预期更早关注盈利能力，以减少对融资的依赖。
    Startups should focus on profitability sooner than often expected to reduce reliance on fundraising.
  • AI 是团队的倍增器，而非替代品，尤其在编码、法规和数据管理方面。
    AI is a multiplier for teams, not a replacement, especially in coding, regulations, and data management.

反共识 · Contrarian takes

  • 为采购和人力资源构建定制内部软件比购买商业 ERP 系统更有效。
    Building custom internal software for purchasing and HR is more effective than buying commercial ERP systems.
  • 绩效评估应该是持续且基于图表的，而不是年度且由人力资源驱动的。
    Performance reviews should be continuous and graph-based, not annual and HR-driven.
  • 短期内，脑机接口更多是长寿故事，而非 AI 故事。
    BCIs are more a longevity story than an AI story in the near term.
  • 在深度科技领域，失败更多是由于组织问题，而非技术不工作。
    In deep tech, failure is more often due to organizational issues than technology not working.
  • 行动产生信息；当陷入困境时，必须注入行动以创造熵并解锁。
    Action produces information; when stuck, you must inject action to create entropy and unblock.
  • 作为 CEO，你不能委托你的判断；即使孤立无援，也必须做出对自己有意义的决定。
    You cannot delegate your judgment as CEO; you must make decisions that make sense to you even if alone.
