核心观点 · Key points

  • 尽管探索了更环境化的设计，聊天式界面仍然是人与智能体交互的正确形态。
    The chat-based interface remains the right form factor for interacting with agents, despite explorations into more ambient designs.
  • 入门引导应着重于前置传达价值，而非减少步骤或添加跳过按钮。
    Onboarding should focus on communicating value upfront, not on reducing steps or adding skip buttons.
  • Figma 仍然是有价值的草图工具，尤其是当智能体能处理布局和素材放置等重复性任务时。
    Figma remains a valuable sketching tool, especially when agents can handle repetitive tasks like layout and asset placement.
  • Cursor 的设计是高保真的，每个想法都有可运行的原型支撑，而非静态稿。
    Design at Cursor is high-fidelity, with every idea backed by a working prototype rather than static mockups.
  • 用户研究和数据指导决策，但深思熟虑的超级用户提供了宝贵的定性反馈。
    User research and data inform decisions, but thoughtful power users provide invaluable qualitative feedback.

反共识 · Contrarian takes

  • Grok Bot 的初始版本由工程师在房间里构建，而非设计师，并且它成功了。
    The initial version of Grok Bot was built by engineers in a room, not by designers, and it succeeded.
  • 设计师应该接受丢弃大部分概念；只有少数种子能进入最终产品。
    Designers should be okay with throwing away most of their concepts; only a few seeds make it to the final product.
  • 尽管受到批评，三栏式布局仍然是人与智能体舰队交互的最佳方式。
    The three-column layout for agents is still the best way to interact with a fleet of agents, despite criticism.
  • 入门引导不应有跳过按钮，因为无指导地进入 AI 工具会导致困惑。
    Onboarding should not have a skip button because jumping into an AI tool without guidance leads to confusion.
  • 吉祥物角色，最初是六边形光标形状，成为产品身份和用户共鸣的核心。
    The mascot character, initially a hexagonal cursor shape, became central to the product's identity and user resonance.
