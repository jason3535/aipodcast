本期速览 · Overview

Fireworks AI 创始人 Lin Qiao 讲述为何基于专有用户数据打造的定制模型将超越基础模型，以及她在 Meta 开发 PyTorch 的经历如何塑造了这一判断。

Fireworks AI founder Lin Qiao explains why custom models built on proprietary user data will outlast foundation models, and how her PyTorch work at Meta shaped that vision.

要点 · TL;DR

  • 推理质量是 AI 产品的首要因素，前沿质量是门槛。
    Inference quality is the top priority for AI products, and frontier quality is the bar.
  • 私有数据是解锁下一代智能的关键，而前沿实验室缺乏私有数据。
    Private data unlocks the next frontier of intelligence, which frontier labs lack.
  • 未来是定制模型主导，而非少数基础模型；拥有自己的模型至关重要。
    Customized models will dominate, not a few foundation models; owning your model is critical.

  • 应用商品化意味着应用层没有深护城河；数据才有。

训练与推理解析 Training vs. inference explained

Host

对我们一些不太懂技术的听众，你能不能解释一下训练和推理的区别？训练可能有些人比较熟悉，比如训练 GPT 或 Claude 这样的大模型；而推理正是 Fireworks 主要专注的方向。

For some of our less technical listeners, can you explain the difference between training, which a few people might be familiar with in terms of training something big like GPT or Claude, versus inference, which is a lot of what Fireworks focuses on?

Lin

训练是模型学习的一种方式。从根本上说——目前我们主要谈的是神经网络——神经网络学习的方式基本上是：先做一次前向传播，然后产生一个损失，再从损失出发用反向传播往回推，沿途改变权重，然后再前向传播一次，再往回推一次。所以这种来回往复就是训练的本质，它天生就非常依赖数据，因为要从零开始学习就需要海量数据。但训练也有不同的阶段：预训练、中期训练、后训练，使用不同的算法。所以这就是它拿一个大数据集，观察这些数据，找出其中的联系，并从中学习以达成某个目标，对吧？

So training is a way for a model to learn. Fundamentally — currently we're mostly talking about neural networks — the way for neural networks to learn is basically: it has a forward pass and then generates a loss, and then from the loss it uses the backward pass to push back and change the weights along the way, and then go forward, pass again, and then push back again. So this back-and-forth is the essence of training, and it's by nature very data-heavy, because to learn from scratch it needs a huge amount of data. But also training has different phases: pre-training to mid-training to post-training, using different kinds of algorithms. So this is where it takes a big dataset, looks at that data, finds connections between it, and learns from that to achieve something, right?

Lin

所以首先，在训练之前你需要设计一个有很多层的模型架构，而学习过程就是把权重填充进这个模型架构。这些权重是某种数值。最终结果是模型架构加上各种不同的权重，然后你把它部署到生产环境。接着你的产品可以输入信息——提示词就是输入——然后前向传播生成预测结果，再返回给产品。这就是推理。所以推理只有前向传播，而训练是多次前向加反向，两者在设计上是根本不同的系统。我会说训练复杂得多，但它有一条共同的路径。其实训练的一部分就是推理——你可以这么理解——正因如此，拥有一个让训练和推理完全对齐的平台极其重要。对齐直接影响质量，而不对齐会导致质量下降和精度损失。所以 Fireworks 把训练和推理作为一个平台提供，来支撑各种应用。这是比较高层面的说法，但我们也经历了算法设计的各个阶段。

So first of all, before you train you need to design a model architecture with many layers, and the learning process is to populate weights into this model architecture. Those weights are kind of numerical values. And the end result is a model architecture combined with various different weights, and then you deploy that into production. And then your product can pump in information — the prompt is the input — and then go forward to generate the projected result and then push back to the product. So that's inference. So inference is forward pass only, and training is forward and backward multiple times, and it's a fundamentally different system to design. Training is much more complicated, I would say, but it has a common path. Actually, part of training is inference — you can think that way — and that's where having a platform that runs both training and inference fully aligned is extremely important. Alignment directly leads into quality, and misalignment leads to lower quality and loss of precision. So that's where Fireworks provides both training and inference as one platform to power applications. That's kind of high level, but we've also gone through phases of algorithm design.

Yeah, I think passing on cost is one thing, but also not building a moat is a bigger problem, because if you're building on top of a foundation that you're just easily replaceable on, it's not a good position to be in. It's not a durable business to be in. The most important role of building a product is to build a durable business. And I do think the intelligence derived from the model is a critical part of a product dependency that it should be returned to the hands of the product team.
