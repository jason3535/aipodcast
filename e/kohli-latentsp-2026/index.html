本期速览 · Overview

Pushmeet Kohli 与 Sal Candido 探讨蛋白质折叠为何尚未真正解决、数据的“苦涩教训”，以及开放社区驱动的数据如何推动生物科学突破。

Pushmeet Kohli and Sal Candido discuss why protein folding isn't truly solved, the bitter lesson for data, and how open community-driven data can unlock bioscience.

要点 · TL;DR

  • 蛋白质折叠并未解决，动力学、功能和设计仍是开放且资金不足的领域。
    Protein folding isn't solved; dynamics, function, and design remain open and underfunded.
  • 缩放定律并非普适，成功来自找到合适的数据-计算机制。
    Scaling laws aren't universal; success comes from finding the right data-compute regime.
  • 低质量宏基因组数据和生物物理归纳偏置提升了模型性能和数据效率。
    Low-quality metagenomic data and biophysical inductive bias boost model performance and data efficiency.

核心观点 · Key points

  • 先定义问题，在建模、数据与算力之间灵活取舍，而非教条地只选其一。
    Define the problem first; be flexible across modeling, data, and compute rather than religiously picking one.
  • 缩放定律并非无处不在；大量工作在于找到更多算力与数据确实有效的区间。
    Scaling laws are not everywhere; much of the work is finding the regime where more compute and data help.
  • 优质数据整理本身就是艺术；数据的覆盖度比单纯的数量更重要。
    Good data curation is an art; coverage of data matters more than sheer volume.
  • 蛋白质折叠并未解决；动力学、功能与设计仍是开放问题，值得持续投入。
    Protein folding is not solved; dynamics, function, and design remain wide-open problems deserving continued funding.
  • 部署前必须对模型做行为刻画，明确其能力边界与局限。
    Behavioral characterization of models—their strengths and limits—is essential before deployment.
  • AI 已渗透药物发现各环节；更大的 10 倍加速有待更好的生物学模型。
    AI already permeates drug discovery; the larger 10x acceleration awaits better biological models.

反共识 · Contrarian takes

  • 低质量宏基因组数据——甚至不完整的蛋白质——反而提升了蛋白质语言模型的表现。
    Low-quality metagenomic data, even incomplete proteins, improved protein language model performance.
  • AlphaFold 复现了 PDB 结构，却未揭示蛋白质真正的基态或结构分布。
    AlphaFold replicated PDB structures but did not reveal proteins' true ground state or structural distribution.
  • 可解释性因人而异；更大的模型或许比人类更能解释 AlphaFold。
    Interpretability is in the eyes of the beholder; a larger model may interpret AlphaFold better than humans.
  • 来自生物物理的归纳偏置赋予模型不公平优势，使其数据效率大幅提升。
    Inductive bias from biophysics gives models an unfair advantage and makes them far more data efficient.
  • 瞄准 10 倍而非 10% 的改进，能拓宽视野，发现原本会错过的解法。
    Aiming for 10x improvement, not 10%, broadens the view and reveals solutions you otherwise miss.
  • 模型蕴含的信息远超我们认知；可解释性可释放功能与运动方面的知识。
    Models contain far more information than we realize; interpretability can unlock function and motion knowledge.
