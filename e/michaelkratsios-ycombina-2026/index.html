本期速览 · Overview

迈克尔·克拉齐奥斯讨论他在塑造美国 AI 政策中的角色，以及他选择公共服务的原因。

Michael Kratsios discusses his role in shaping US AI policy and why he chose public service.

要点 · TL;DR

  • 美国必须在开源和闭源 AI 之间取得平衡以保持全球领先，并建立统一的国家监管框架。
    The US must balance open and closed source AI to lead globally, with a single national regulatory framework.
  • AI 监管应避免设定硬性红线，因为它们很快就会过时；应关注输出而非训练。
    AI regulation should avoid firm red lines as they quickly become outdated; focus on outputs not training.
  • 继 AI 之后，量子计算是华盛顿下一个前沿技术优先事项。
    Quantum computing is the next frontier tech priority for Washington, following AI.

核心观点 · Key points

  • 美国必须维持一个充满活力的开源和闭源AI生态系统，才能在全球保持领先。
    The US must maintain a vibrant open and closed source AI ecosystem to lead globally.
  • 清晰的监管和统一的全国性AI框架对于支持初创企业至关重要。
    Regulatory clarity and a single national framework for AI are essential to support startups.
  • 政府应避免在AI监管中设定过于僵化的红线，因为它们很快就会过时。
    The government should avoid setting firm red lines in AI regulation because they quickly become outdated.
  • AI将改变科学发现的方式，政府必须相应调整其研发资助策略。
    AI will transform scientific discovery, and the government must adapt its R&D funding accordingly.
  • 量子计算是华盛顿将优先考虑的下一个前沿技术。
    Quantum computing is the next frontier technology that Washington will prioritize.
  • 在政府工作提供了对国家产生重大影响的独特机会。
    Working in government offers a unique opportunity to have a large impact on the country.

反共识 · Contrarian takes

  • 欧盟AI法案在ChatGPT出现之前就已制定，如今已经过时，这显示了过早监管的风险。
    The EU AI Act was written before ChatGPT and is already outdated, showing the danger of early regulation.
  • AI带来的生物风险被夸大了；尽管有警告，但三年来并未成为现实。
    Biological risk from AI is overblown; it hasn't materialized in three years despite warnings.
  • 政府不应直接指导发现，而应塑造创新的环境。
    The government should not direct discovery but shape the arena for innovation.
  • 优先于州AI法律是支持初创企业的政策，因为大科技可以应对零散的法规，而初创企业不能。
    Preemption of state AI laws is a pro-startup policy because big tech can handle patchwork regulations, startups cannot.
  • 美国政府在研发资助中的角色已经反转；私营部门现在占主导，需要新的方法。
    The US government's role in R&D funding has inverted; private sector now dominates, requiring a new approach.
  • AI监管应关注模型输出，如防止输出米老鼠，而不是过度监管训练过程。
    AI regulation should focus on model outputs, like preventing Mickey Mouse outputs, rather than over-regulating training.
