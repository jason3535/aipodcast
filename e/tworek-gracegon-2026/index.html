本期速览 · Overview

Core Automation CEO Jerry Tworek 解析为何 Transformer 并非终点，以及模型如何随用户数据实时学习。

Core Automation CEO Jerry Tworek explains why transformers aren't the end of the road and how models can learn from user data on the fly.

要点 · TL;DR

  • Transformer 并非终点，更好的架构将带来更智能、更稳健的学习系统。
    Transformers aren't the end; better architectures will enable smarter, more robust learning.
  • 微调效率低下，模型应实时从用户数据中学习。
    Fine-tuning is inefficient; models should learn from user data on the fly.
  • 未来的公司将由 AI 运营，人类负责设定目标和引导。

核心观点 · Key points

  • Transformer 并非终点；更好的架构能带来更智能、更鲁棒的学习系统。
    Transformers are not the end of the road; better architectures can yield smarter, more robust learning systems.
  • 微调效率低且不可靠；模型应即时从用户数据中学习。
    Fine-tuning is inefficient and unreliable; models should learn from user data on the fly.
  • 未来的公司将在很大程度上由 AI 运营，人类负责设定目标并掌舵。
    The company of the future will be largely AI-operated, with humans setting objectives and steering.
  • 我们正走向后劳动社会，工作成为可选，学习成为核心。
    We are heading toward a post-labor society where work is optional and learning is central.
  • AI 驱动的效率将让大公司行动更快，使初创公司更难追赶。
    AI-driven efficiency will let big companies move faster, making it harder for startups to catch up.
  • 优秀研究者避免群体思维，理解系统与 GPU，并避免陷入停滞。
    Good researchers avoid groupthink, understand systems and GPUs, and avoid getting stuck.

反共识 · Contrarian takes

  • 当前智能体不学习新技能，仅通过上下文窗口个性化，这有根本局限。
    Current agents don't learn new skills; they only personalize via context, which is fundamentally limited.
  • 工作替代将同时全面发生，而非按职业类型逐渐推进。
    Job replacement will happen everywhere at once, not gradually by job type.
  • 评估大多具有误导性且被精心挑选；对于持续学习的模型，评估是过时的。
    Evals are mostly misleading and cherry-picked; they are an anachronism for models that learn continuously.
  • 对企业而言，按机架收费搭配本地硬件比按 token 收费更合理。
    Charging per rack with on-prem hardware makes more sense than per-token pricing for enterprise.
  • 神经网络架构研究被严重低估，并将迎来回归。
    Neural network architecture research is severely undervalued and will make a comeback.
  • 人才并非被大实验室垄断；随着公司变得官僚化，人才会分散。
    Talent is not monopolized by big labs; it disperses as companies become bureaucratic.
