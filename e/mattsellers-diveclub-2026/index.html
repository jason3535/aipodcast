本期速览 · Overview

Matt Sers 分享他如何通过减法、专注工艺和设计沉浸式体验来重建作品集。

Matt Sers shares how he rebuilt his portfolio by subtracting work, focusing on craft, and designing it as an immersive experience.

要点 · TL;DR

  • 作品集是一种沉浸式体验，激进策展与匠心比数量更重要。
    A portfolio is an immersive experience where radical curation and craft speak louder than quantity.
  • 为特定的资深受众而非泛泛的招聘者而设计。
    Design for a specific experienced viewer, not a generic recruiter.
  • 对科技招聘受众，桌面优先体验可能比移动优先更优。
    Desktop-first experiences can outperform mobile-first for tech hiring audiences.

核心观点 · Key points

  • 作品集应更看重作品的平均质量而非总量；果断做减法能提高水准。
    A portfolio should prioritize the average quality of work over the sum; ruthless subtraction raises the bar.
  • 把作品集当作一种沉浸式体验，让工艺与用心比流程更能打动人。
    Treat the portfolio as an immersive experience, letting craft and care speak louder than process.
  • 微型文案能传递共鸣与品味，因此开篇段落是至关重要的设计元素。
    Microcopy can signal shared experience and taste, making the intro paragraph a critical design element.
  • 为设计招聘经理这样的特定受众而设计，而非面向泛泛的招聘人员或所有人。
    Design for a specific audience like design hiring managers, not generic recruiters or everyone.
  • 借创建作品集的机会突破工具边界，并深度学习新技能。
    Use portfolio creation as a chance to push tool boundaries and learn new skills deeply.

反共识 · Contrarian takes

  • 对设计作品集而言，移动优先被高估了；桌面优先更能向招聘经理传递有效信号。
    Mobile-first is overrated for design portfolios; desktop-first delivers better signal to hiring managers.
  • 过多展示流程可能产生反效果；不带指标的纯粹工艺反而更有说服力。
    Showing too much process can create negative signal; pure craft without metrics can be more convincing.
  • 删掉 75%–87% 的作品；项目越多，越容易被挑毛病而失去机会。
    Cut 75–87% of your work; more projects create more opportunities for disqualification.
  • 首页不要放行动号召按钮；让访客自由探索，而不是被引向案例研究。
    Avoid CTAs on the homepage; let visitors explore instead of steering them to case studies.
  • 如果你的工艺尚弱，别用冗长的流程说明来掩盖；应展现审美与潜力。
    If your craft is weak, don't overcompensate with process fluff; show taste and potential instead.
