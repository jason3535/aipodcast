Crafting a Portfolio That Gets You Hired at Top Startups

本期速览 · Overview

Matt Sers 分享他如何通过减法、专注工艺和设计沉浸式体验来重建作品集。

Matt Sers shares how he rebuilt his portfolio by subtracting work, focusing on craft, and designing it as an immersive experience.

要点 · TL;DR

  • 作品集是一种沉浸式体验，激进策展与匠心比数量更重要。
    A portfolio is an immersive experience where radical curation and craft speak louder than quantity.
  • 为特定的资深受众而非泛泛的招聘者而设计。
    Design for a specific experienced viewer, not a generic recruiter.
  • 对科技招聘受众，桌面优先体验可能比移动优先更优。
    Desktop-first experiences can outperform mobile-first for tech hiring audiences.

核心观点 · Key points

  • 作品集应是一种沉浸式体验，传递用心与工艺，而不仅仅是项目的功能列表。
    A portfolio should be an immersive experience that conveys care and craft, not just a functional list of projects.
  • 激进的取舍至关重要：所展示作品的平均质量远比数量重要。
    Aggressive curation is essential: the average quality of work shown matters more than the total quantity.
  • 只展示你最引以为傲的作品，即使这意味着砍掉你能呈现的75%到90%。
    Show only the work you are most proud of, even if it means cutting 75-90% of what you could present.
  • 微文案和细节能建立共鸣，并向有眼光的设计负责人传递你的品味信号。
    Microcopy and small details create shared connections and signal taste to discerning design leaders.
  • 为特定的理想受众而设计——资深设计师和招聘经理，而非泛泛的HR或招聘人员。
    Design for a specific ideal viewer—experienced designers and hiring managers—rather than a generic recruiter or HR.

反共识 · Contrarian takes

  • 彻底省略过程说明和指标；如果作品本身的工艺立不住，任何文字都救不了它。
    Omit process explanations and metrics entirely; if the craft does not stand alone, no words can save it.
  • 对科技受众来说，移动优先被高估了；桌面优先的体验可能是作品集的正确选择。
    Mobile-first design is overrated for tech audiences; desktop-first experiences can be the right choice for portfolios.
  • 不要复制优秀作品，而是逆向工程其决策；在建立技能之前先建立品味。
    Instead of copying great work, reverse engineer its decisions; build your taste before you build your skills.
  • 不要用冗长的过程包装来伪装工艺；诚实展现成长比虚张声势的平庸更受尊重。
    Don't fake craft with verbose process dressing; honest growth signals are respected more than inflated mediocrity.
  • 作品集可以刻意省略明显的案例研究导航；探索本身就是一个特色。
    A portfolio may intentionally leave out obvious case study navigation; exploration itself is a feature.
