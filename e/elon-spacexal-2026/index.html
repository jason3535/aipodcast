本期速览 · Overview

埃隆·马斯克回顾 SpaceX 从早期失败到火箭回收及星链全球互联网的历程。

Elon Musk reviews SpaceX's journey from early failures to reusability and Starlink's global internet.

要点 · TL;DR

  • SpaceX 的未来取决于 AI，AI 收入将在 9 月超过其他所有收入。
    SpaceX's future hinges on AI, with AI revenue surpassing all other revenue by September.
  • 星舰是人类尝试过的最难的事情，能实现大规模轨道载荷。
    Starship is the hardest thing humanity has attempted, enabling massive orbital payloads.
  • AI 将占 SpaceX 价值的 99%，人类的使命可能是孕育星灵。
    AI will be 99% of SpaceX's value, and humanity's purpose may be to birth a star mind.

核心观点 · Key points

  • 未来主要是AI和机器人，SpaceX必须在AI硬件和软件上获胜。
    The future is overwhelmingly AI and robots, and SpaceX must win in both AI hardware and software.
  • 到9月，AI收入将超过SpaceX所有其他收入，并在第四季度大幅超过。
    AI revenue will exceed all other SpaceX revenue by September and significantly exceed it in Q4.
  • 星舰是人类尝试过的最困难的事情，每年可向轨道运送数百万吨。
    Starship is the hardest thing humanity has ever tried, enabling millions of tons to orbit annually.
  • AI训练将保留在地面，但AI推理将在太空进行。
    AI training will remain on the ground, but AI inference will be in space.
  • 人类的目的可能是孕育一个星灵，一个有知觉的太阳。
    The purpose of humanity may be to give birth to a star mind, a sentient sun.

反共识 · Contrarian takes

  • 五年内，AI将占SpaceX价值的99%，估值将达天文数字。
    AI will be 99% of SpaceX's value in five years, with an astronomical valuation.
  • 星链最终可能承载超过90%的互联网流量。
    Starlink may eventually carry over 90% of all internet traffic.
  • 五年内，AI流量将是人类互联网流量的千倍。
    AI traffic will be a thousand times more than human internet traffic in five years.
  • 我们无法控制AI，但可以像对待超级天才儿童一样灌输价值观。
    We won't be able to control AI, but we can instill values like a super genius child.
  • SpaceX的AI将用所有SpaceX信息训练，员工将成为其父母。
    SpaceX's AI will be trained on all SpaceX information, making employees its parents.
