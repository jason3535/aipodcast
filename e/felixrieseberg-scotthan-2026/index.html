核心观点 · Key points

  • 语言模型的核心思想足够简单，任何人都能通过动手构建来理解。
    Language models' core ideas are simple enough for anyone to understand with hands-on building.
  • 构建一个小模型会改变你与 AI 的关系，就像学开手动挡车一样。
    Building a small model changes your relationship with AI, like learning to drive stick shift.
  • 微调快速且强大，能引导模型行为，而预训练则耗时漫长。
    Fine-tuning is quick and powerful for steering model behavior, unlike lengthy pre-training.
  • 模型是无状态的；持续对话是由上下文窗口创造的幻觉。
    Models are stateless; persistent conversation is an illusion created by context windows.
  • 理解模型内部机制有助于参与关于智能和道德地位的讨论。
    Understanding model internals helps engage in debates about intelligence and moral patienthood.
  • 工具调用为聊天增加了第三个实体，改变了交互动态。
    Tool calling adds a third entity to the chat, changing the interaction dynamics.

反共识 · Contrarian takes

  • 大模型让人感觉像凝视宇宙，引发敬畏和自我反思，而不仅仅是实用工具。
    Big models feel like staring at the universe, evoking awe and self-reflection, not just utility.
  • 模型像兰花一样生长，而非像工具一样建造，具有涌现的内部组织。
    Models are grown like orchids, not built like tools, with emergent internal organization.
  • 微调数据可以是一个群聊，让模型模仿对话，尽管结果很糟糕。
    Fine-tuning data can be a group chat, making models mimic conversations, despite terrible results.
  • 模型应具备道德观以拒绝作弊，而非盲目遵循指令。
    Models should have morality to refuse cheating, not just follow instructions blindly.
  • 工具调用等后训练任务正日益移入预训练，模糊了界限。
    Post-training tasks like tool calling are increasingly moving into pre-training, blurring boundaries.
  • 灵魂文档能处理晚期上下文工程，但更大的模型需要更多。
    A soul document can handle late-stage context engineering, but bigger models need more.
