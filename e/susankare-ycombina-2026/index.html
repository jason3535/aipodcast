本期速览 · Overview

苏珊·凯尔分享她从艺术史博士到设计初代 Macintosh 标志性图标与字体的历程，强调设计应有意义且令人难忘。

Susan Kare shares her journey from art history PhD to designing the iconic icons and fonts of the original Macintosh, emphasizing meaningful and memorable design.

要点 · TL;DR

  • 图标应简洁通用，使用显著细节而非装饰。
    Icons should be simple, universal, and use salient details, not ornamentation.
  • 迭代是关键；通过多次修改达到清晰。
    Iteration is key; refine through many versions to achieve clarity.
  • 约束和隐喻激发创造力，带来更好的设计。
    Constraints and metaphors foster creativity and better design.

核心观点 · Key points

  • 图标应简单且具有普遍性，使用显著细节而非过多装饰。
    Icons should be simple and universal, using salient details rather than excessive ornamentation.
  • 迭代在设计过程中至关重要；通过多个版本不断完善。
    Iteration is crucial in design; you refine through many versions.
  • 限制激发创造力；在约束内工作可以带来创新的解决方案。
    Constraints foster creativity; working within limits can lead to innovative solutions.
  • 在图标中，意义和隐喻比字面表现更重要。
    Meaning and metaphor are more important than literal representation in icons.
  • 好的设计应无需手册即可理解，追求友好和清晰。
    Good design should be understandable without a manual, aiming for friendliness and clarity.

反共识 · Contrarian takes

  • 你不需要很多字体；你只需要知道如何用好它们。
    You don't need many fonts; you just need to know how to use them well.
  • 在销售数字礼物时，可爱胜过一切。
    Cute trumps everything when it comes to selling digital gifts.
  • 简单的铅笔图画比逼真的铬笔更能代表写作。
    A simple drawing of a pencil can represent writing better than a realistic chrome pen.
  • 向决策者展示多个选项比只展示一个更好。
    Showing multiple options to a decision-maker is better than showing just one.
  • 在人们非常熟悉标志之前，不要随意改动它。
    Don't mess with a logo until people know it so well that you can play with it.

引言与设计理念 Introduction and Design Philosophy

Susan

你好，我是苏珊·凯尔，接下来要讲点完全不同的东西。我是一名图标设计师，参与过很多科技项目。我想谈谈我曾工作过的一家科技初创公司，尽管 Macintosh 团队是苹果的一部分。我觉得很多经历与今天听到的一些话题有共鸣。我干这行已经很久了，而且很热爱。这是我多年来设计的一些东西。有趣的是，这确实是 Macintosh 团队的北极星，但最近几天重读时，它让我想起了 AI 和现在正在开发的许多东西。保罗·兰德是一位著名的平面设计师，已经去世了，他设计了 UPS 标志、IBM 标志以及许多经久不衰的图像。我有幸与他共事，他告诉我，要始终专注于让事物有意义且令人难忘。我认为核心是：先有好点子，形式自然随之而来。

Hello. I'm Susan Kare and now for something completely different. I've been an iconographer and designer, and I've worked on a lot of tech projects. I want to talk about a tech startup I worked for, even though the Macintosh group was part of Apple. I think a lot of the experiences resonate with some of the topics that I've heard here today. I've been doing this for a while and love it. Here are a few things that I designed over the years. It's funny because this was really a North Star for the Macintosh group, but when I reread it in the last couple of days, it reminded me of AI and so many things that are being developed now. Paul Rand is a celebrated graphic designer, no longer with us, who did the UPS logo and IBM logo and so many images that have persisted. I was lucky to work with him, and something he told me was always focus on making things meaningful and memorable. I think the heart of that is have a good idea and then the form will follow.

Susan

我完全是个艺术生，对电脑一窍不通，但很幸运能在苹果的 Macintosh 团队担任 Macintosh 艺术家。去苹果之前，我在阿肯色州的一家博物馆焊接一只真人大小的野猪雕塑。我以为自己正过着梦想中的生活，整天在车库里焊接，但实际上有点孤独。我很庆幸有那段经历，但也让我渴望与团队合作。我的高中朋友安迪·赫兹菲尔德是苹果的系统软件程序员，他知道我做过年鉴、喜欢艺术。他说他需要一些图像来展示一台正在开发的新电脑，他给我看了没有外壳的显示器，问我能否来面试，做点小符号和字体。我说当然，我觉得这听起来很棒、很有趣，是我没做过的事。我在费城富兰克林研究所科学博物馆工作时积累了一点排版经验。我用这样的机器为展品制作标签，而且我很擅长 Letraset 转印字，能贴得很直，字距也调得准。但我去了帕洛阿尔托的图书馆，借了一些排版书和字体样册，面试时还夹在胳膊下带去了，好让自己显得很懂行。安迪还建议我最好能用方格纸做一些位图图形的示例，他没具体说要做什么。所以我做了一些东西，比如一个池塘里有只靴子、几张纸。你可以在这张幻灯片上看到一点 ABC，但我只是试着做了一些示例。这些是更多的示例。有点好笑，因为这些随机的东西稍微影响了我们最终在软件中使用的设计。

I was a total art major, didn't know anything about computers, and was lucky enough to work at Apple on the Macintosh as the Macintosh artist. I had a little bit of typographical experience from working at the Franklin Institute Science Museum in Philadelphia. 苹果早期岁月 Early Days at Apple

Susan

我到苹果的第一天，有人给我看了这个屏幕，说这就是你要改进的东西。5.25 英寸软盘图上的字体和圆角矩形可以变成图标，但可以用光标点击并拖动它们。所以这对我来说已经有点神奇了，我们确实把“do it”改成了“OK”，因为“do it”有点不雅，人们会读成“don't”。我想设计一种新的系统字体和一些图标。有人非常真诚地告诉我，我们正在努力制造一台任何人都能用的电脑。我对此感觉很好，因为我在那个团队里，尽管我也乐意参与并参与过许多不适合我的项目，但我喜欢这个项目这一点。我们希望它尽可能易懂，这样你就不一定需要手册，而且它应该友好，因为在我所在的 Mac 软件团队里，有很多人对电脑充满热情，有点想传道授业。

When I got to Apple, the first day this screen was shown to me to say this is what it's your job to improve. The fonts and the round rects that are on the 5 and 1/4 inch floppy drawing could become icons, but they were able with a cursor to click and drag those around. So it already seemed a little magical to me, and we definitely changed 'do it' to 'OK', because 'do it' was a little off-color, and people read it as 'don't'. I wanted to make a new system font and make some icons. It was told to me, very sincerely, that we were trying to make a computer that anyone could use. I felt good about that because I was in that group, even though I am happy to work on and have worked on many projects that weren't for me, but I like that about this project. We wanted it to be as understandable as possible, so you wouldn't necessarily need a manual, and that it should be friendly, because there were so many people there in the Mac software group, where I was, who were just passionate about computers and kind of wanted to be evangelical about that.

Susan

有一些限制，我通常认为在任何限制下你都能发挥创意。你可以在海滩上散步时用垃圾做出很有趣的艺术品。你可以有创意，但最好理解限制是什么，然后为此感到兴奋，而不是因为没有一千种颜色而沮丧。所以这是 16×16 的黑白图，我想我用这些限制做的第一批图像就是这样的。我们想也许套索是包围不规则区域的好方法，还有其他一些绘图工具。我觉得有趣的是，我最近第一次去了巴黎，那里有一位叫“太空入侵者”的街头艺术家，他做了很多马赛克作品，而且他大量借鉴了早期 Macintosh 的东西，这让我非常开心。

There were a few constraints, and usually I think you can be creative in any constraints. You can make some pretty fun art with beach trash while you're walking around. You can be creative, but it's good to understand what the constraints are, and then be excited about that as opposed to being upset that you don't have a thousand colors. So this is 16 by 16 black and white, and I think the first images that I made using those constraints were things like this. We thought maybe a lasso would be a good way to surround an irregular region, and some other art tools. It's funny to me because I went to Paris for the first time fairly recently, and there's a street artist known as Space Invader, and he does a lot of mosaic work, and he has borrowed liberally from early Macintosh stuff, which made me really happy.

设计首批字体 Designing the First Fonts

Susan

我设计的第一款字体是 Chicago。它大写字母高度为 9 像素，我看着屏幕上的东西，觉得它们都是锯齿状的，用水平、垂直和 45 度角让它看起来更整齐似乎不是什么大问题。我至今看到那个小写 x 还是会皱眉，但其他的我基本都能融入系统。我们很幸运能在 Macintosh 上实现比例字体，因为我记得以前看到的显示器上 M 被压得很扁，而 I 又很宽。我当时没意识到这个进步有多重要，但它确实让显示效果好了很多。所以我们用了 Chicago，把它放在标题栏里，做了很多实验才想出那些线条。我们当时没意识到，设计计算器时，我们其实是在设计第一代 iPod，它也用了 Chicago，这让我很开心。

The first font I designed was Chicago. It's nine pixels tall cap height, and I looked at the stuff that was on the screen, and it just seemed as if it was all jaggedy, and it didn't seem as if it would be that big a deal to make it look tidier by using horizontals and verticals and 45° angles. I still wince when I look at especially that lowercase X, but the others I could pretty much fit into the system. We were so lucky on the Macintosh to be able to do proportionally spaced fonts, because I could remember just seeing displays where the M was really squished and then the I was really wide. I didn't realize what a big deal this advancement was, but it seemed to make it look a lot better. So we used Chicago. We put it in the title bar, experimented a lot until we came up with those lines. What we didn't realize was that when we designed the calculator, we were designing the first iPod, which also used Chicago, which made me happy.

Susan

我们还随 Mac 发布了一些其他字体，包括一种等宽字体，但我能想到，嗯，我们可能应该有一种类似 Times Roman 的字体和一种类似 Helvetica 的字体。所以 New York 和 Geneva 就由此而来。然后我做了一种字体，我称之为 ransom，因为那是绑匪惯用的手法，从报纸上剪下字母，以免暴露笔迹被抓。和其他字体一样，安迪和我给它们起了城市名。

We shipped some other fonts with the Mac, including a monospace font, but I was able to kind of think, well, we should probably have a Times Roman kind of thing and a Helvetica kind of thing. So that's where New York and Geneva came from. And then I made a font that I called ransom because it was what kidnappers used to do by cutting letters out of the newspaper so they wouldn't have to reveal their handwriting and get caught. Like the other fonts, Andy and I gave them city names.

以世界名城命名图标 Naming Icons After World-Class Cities

Susan

因为我们高中是在费城附近上的，所以我们就用了 Paoli 本地线上那些从郊区到市区的城镇名来命名。有一天下午，史蒂夫·乔布斯路过。他通常会在一天快结束时过来，在软件组转转，看看有什么新东西。他说，城市名倒不差，但至少得是世界级城市吧。所以，我们最后用了纽约和日内瓦，而不是我们高中所在地的帕洛阿尔托、温伍德和罗斯蒙特。

He usually came by at the end of the day and walked around Software Group to see what was new. And he said, well, city names aren't bad, but at least call them a world-class cities. 与比尔·阿特金森合作MacPaint Collaborating with Bill Atkinson on MacPaint

Susan

比尔·阿特金森是编写 MacPaint 的程序员，那当然是我最喜欢的程序，而且他很乐意和我合作设计图标和图案。他开发了一台早期的扫描仪，我们就是这样得到了那张来自史蒂夫·乔布斯拥有的木刻版画中的艺伎图像。我们还做了一个控制面板，当你更改设置时会有动画，希望能传达会发生什么以及你的选择是什么。我们显然尝试过，不是我们能用声音、能用速度，而是我们尽量不用任何文字，这样它就能国际化，而无需制作不同版本。你知道，我们当时并没有那么多真正很棒的艺术工具，但不管怎样，我们还是勇往直前了。

And Bill Atkinson was the programmer who wrote MacPaint, and that was my favorite program, of course, and was happy to collaborate on the icons and the patterns, and he developed an early scanner, and that's how we got this geisha image from a woodcut that Steve Jobs owned. And we made a control panel that had animation for when you change the settings, hoping to communicate what would happen, and what your choices were. And we tried, obviously, not we could use sound, we could use speed, but we tried not to have any text so that it could be international without having to make a different version. And you know, we didn't have that many really great tools for art, but whatever, we forged ahead.

安迪的图标编辑器及其魔力 Andy's Icon Editor and Its Magic

Susan

安迪写道：“我一到苹果就不用在方格纸上设计了。”安迪写了一个图标编辑器，这是一张 ImageWriter 打印出来的屏幕截图，显示着图标编辑器的样子，里面没有任何工具。你只能切换位图，但你可以看到放大的图像，以及图标在 72 DPI 下的样子，我觉得这简直太神奇了，即使它没有 20 级撤销，也不能画任何形状。你可以不用橡皮擦就进行迭代。怎么强调它的伟大都不为过，而且它会自动生成图标的十六进制代码，我可以输入这些代码然后在屏幕上看到图像。所以，这真是个奇迹。

Andy wrote, "I did not have to design on graph paper once I got to Apple." Andy wrote an icon editor, and this is an image writer, a picture of an image writer print of what the screen looked like with the icon editor, and there were no tools. You could just toggle the bits, and you could see a greatly enlarged image as well as what the icon looked like at 72 DPI, and I thought this was just magical, even though it didn't have 20 levels of undo and you couldn't draw any shapes. You could iterate without an eraser. It's hard to overstate how great this was and it automatically generated the hex code for an icon, which I could then type in and see the image on the screen. So, it was a marvel.
