本期速览 · Overview

探索谷歌设计团队如何打造体现公司个性与价值观的独特硬件美学。

A look into how Google's design team created a unique hardware aesthetic that embodies the company's personality and values.

要点 · TL;DR

  • 谷歌的硬件设计以人性化、乐观和大胆为原则，将品牌 DNA 转化为实体产品。
    Google's hardware design is guided by human, optimistic, and daring principles, translating brand DNA into physical products.
  • 设计应满足未言明的需求，平衡直觉与数据，以获得更深层次的情感理解。
    Design should address unarticulated needs, balancing intuition with data for deeper emotional understanding.
  • 团队信任和促进创造力的物理空间对于实现大胆的设计成果至关重要。
    Team trust and physical spaces that foster creativity are essential for achieving audacious design outcomes.

核心观点 · Key points

  • Google 的硬件设计语言建立在人性化、乐观和大胆的原则之上，将品牌 DNA 转化为实体产品。
    Google's hardware design language is built on human, optimistic, and daring principles, translating brand DNA into physical products.
  • 设计应关注人们的需求，而非仅听他们所说，需要更深层次的情感理解。
    Design should focus on what people need, not just what they say, requiring deeper emotional understanding.
  • 直觉是一种强大的智能形式，在设计决策中应与数据平衡使用。
    Intuition is a powerful form of intelligence that should be balanced with data in design decisions.
  • 团队成员之间建立信任和联系对于实现大胆的目标至关重要。
    Creating trust and connection among team members is essential for achieving audacious outcomes.
  • 技术应无缝融入家庭环境，模糊工作与休闲的界限。
    Technology should blend into home environments seamlessly, softening lines between work and leisure.

反共识 · Contrarian takes

  • 数据可能不是最高形式的智能；直觉往往知道意识思维所不知道的东西。
    Data may not be the highest form of intelligence; intuition often knows what the conscious mind does not.
  • 仅根据人们所说的需求进行设计是不够的；真正以人为本的设计需要针对未言明的需求进行设计。
    Designing for what people say they want is insufficient; true human-centered design requires designing for unarticulated needs.
  • 在数字化的公司中，实体书籍和材料库仍然有价值，因为触觉体验不可替代。
    Physical books and materials libraries remain valuable in a digitized company, as tactile experiences are irreplaceable.
  • 自然和感官体验可以放慢时间，增强团队创造力，这与快节奏的工作环境相反。
    Nature and sensory experiences can slow down time and enhance team creativity, contrary to fast-paced work environments.
  • 设计感受，而不仅仅是设计思维，至关重要；认知负荷下的情感负荷揭示了更深层的真相。
    Design feeling, not just design thinking, is crucial; emotional load under cognitive load reveals deeper truths.

设计演变与家庭展示 Design Evolution and Home Display

Ivy

去年某个时候，我们开始规划我们设计原则中哪些是真正不变的灵魂，哪些地方允许自己随着时间演变。于是我们开始着手今年的发布产品，第一个是为家庭创造新的形态，这里指的是显示屏。我们非常谨慎，知道人们在家居装饰上花了很多心思。所以我们问自己，在从功能到纯装饰的光谱上，我们应该定位在哪里？我们考虑了各种屏幕尺寸、方向、扬声器音量，寻找合适的形态。最终我们发现，将显示屏悬浮并抬起，将扬声器置于后方并留出足够空间让声音传出，产生了这种视觉上的轻盈感。选择 7 英寸显示屏和扬声器尺寸是为了在家庭中实现最大的多功能性和摆放灵活性。结果是一款独特而轻盈的产品，既与产品家族的其他成员相契合，也融入你的家中，因为屏幕亮度会根据你家的光照条件自动调整。

So sometimes last year, we started to chart out what parts of our design principle was really our soul that would never change, and where would we give ourselves permission to evolve over time. And we wanted to be very mindful, knowing that people take a lot of care in decorating their homes. So we asked ourselves, how far on the spectrum should we position ourselves from function to pure decoration? And we considered various screen sizes, orientations, speaker volume, searching for the right form factor. And we ended up finding out that floating and lifting the display and recessing the speaker behind with enough room for the sound to come through produce this visual lightness. And the 7-inch display speaker size was chosen for the maximum versatility and placement around the home. And the result is a unique light product that fits in with the rest of the product family as well as into your home, because the screen brightness adjusts based on your home's lighting conditions.

Do you test colors with consumers?

我们确实会去找消费者测试颜色，当我们需要调整时，但我们总是以颜色系列的方式进行。你知道，消费者通常第一印象是看到同一产品的多种颜色展示，而这一组产品的初始印象会创造情感。然后人们会被特定颜色吸引，选择适合自己的。所以我们确保在系列中以及单独测试颜色。我们还致力于开发一种一致的语言，不仅在形式和颜色上，还在声音、触觉和灯光等交互中。对于我们的灯光模式，我们寻找人类交流的线索，并尽可能匹配，例如使用多种灯光模式来紧密模仿我们的人类信号，这里映射到连续循环，而充电完成时则是稳定的持续亮灯。

We do go out to consumers and test colors when we need to edit them, but we always do so in families of color. You know, consumers usually their first impression is they see multiple colors of the same product exhibited, and that initial impression of a group of products creates the emotion. And then one gets drawn to a specific color, choosing what is right for them. So we make sure that we test colors in families as well as individually. And we also work on how to develop a consistent language not only in form and color, but in interactions such as sound, touch, and lights. When it comes to our lighting patterns, we look for clues as to how we humans communicate and try to match that as much as possible, such as using a variety of light patterns that closely mimic our human signals, mapping here to a continuous loop versus a solid done holding steady when charging is complete.

设计感受vs设计思维 Design Feeling vs Design Thinking

Ivy

这让我想到设计感受与设计思维。以人为本的设计不是为人们所说或认为他们想要的东西而设计，而是为人们的需求而设计。为人们的需求而设计，正是我们不能仅仅依赖访谈和研究中的调查数据的原因。这类信息只触及表面，因为我们只能从人们那里得到他们基于已知所能表达的东西，而不是我们作为设计师可能想象到的东西，再加上我们的直觉告诉我们的东西。

And that brings me to design feeling versus design thinking. Human-centered design is not about designing for what people say or think they want. It's about designing for what people need. And designing for what people need is why we cannot rely solely on interviews and survey data from research. This type of information only scratches the surface, because we can only get out of people what they're able to articulate based on what they already know, as opposed to what we as designers might be able to imagine, as combined with what our intuition tells us.

Ivy

这就是为什么，在为尚不存在的未来设计时，我们需要更深入地理解人们，他们如何思考和感受，以真正提供他们在五到十年内可能需要的技术。这带你进入设计感受。除了解决问题或实现任务，真正重要的是产品给我们留下的感觉。因此，我们必须理解认知负荷之下层层叠加的情感负荷，这揭示了事物背后更深层的动态和真相。技术变化快，但人类真相不变，行为变化缓慢。

This is why, when designing for a future that does not yet exist, we need to gain a deeper understanding of people, how they think and feel, to truly provide what they might need from technology in five to ten years. Which brings you to design feeling. Beyond solving a problem or enabling a task, what's truly important is how a product leaves us feeling. So it becomes imperative that we understand the emotional load layered under the cognitive load, that reveals the deeper dynamics and truths behind things. Tech changes fast, but human truths don't, and behavior does but slowly.
