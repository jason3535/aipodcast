本期速览 · Overview

谷歌首席设计官艾维·罗斯探讨设计中艺术与科学的交汇，以及惊奇与想象在创造有意义产品中的重要性。

Ivy Ross, Chief Design Officer at Google, discusses the intersection of art and science in design, and the importance of wonder and imagination in creating meaningful products.

要点 · TL;DR

  • 设计解决问题，可持续性是一大挑战。
    Design solves problems, with sustainability as a key challenge.
  • 自然是最好的设计指南，从中寻找灵感。
    Nature is the best design guide; look to it for inspiration.
  • 我们是会思考的情感生物，情感驱动认知。
    We are feeling creatures that think; emotions drive cognition.

核心观点 · Key points

  • 设计关乎解决问题，而可持续性是我们时代最重大的问题之一。
    Design is about solving problems, and sustainability is one of the greatest problems of our time.
  • 自然是最好的设计师；遇到瓶颈时，从自然中寻找灵感。
    Nature is the best designer; when stuck, look for cues from nature.
  • 我们是会思考的感受体，而非会感受的思想体。
    We are feeling creatures that think, not thinking creatures that feel.
  • 不带评判地进行艺术创作，对我们的健康和幸福至关重要。
    Art making, without judgment, is crucial for our health and well-being.
  • 技术应当增强我们的人性，而非使我们脱离人性。
    Technology should amplify our humanity, not take us out of it.
  • 关注那些吸引你注意力的东西，并沿着那条路走下去。
    Pay attention to what gets your attention; follow that path.

反共识 · Contrarian takes

  • 机器有传感器，但没有感官系统。
    Machines have sensors but they don't have sensory systems.
  • 听讲座时涂鸦实际上能提高记忆力。
    Doodling during a lecture actually improves memory retention.
  • 医生应该开博物馆参观处方；艺术是一种良药。
    Doctors should prescribe museum visits; art is a form of medicine.
  • 我们有52种感官，而不仅仅是五种；传统的五种只是开始。
    We have 52 senses, not just five; the traditional five are just the beginning.
  • 约束可以激发创造力；过多的自由可能让人无所适从。
    Constraints can boost creativity; too much freedom can be paralyzing.
  • 未来的职业是多元化的；单一职业路径已经过时。
    The future is multi-diverse occupations; swim lanes are over.
