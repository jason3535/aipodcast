本期速览 · Overview

高通 CEO 克里斯蒂亚诺畅谈未来五年公司重点研发方向，涵盖 6G、汽车、个人 AI 设备与机器人等领域。

Qualcomm CEO Cristiano discusses the company's big R&D bets over the next five years, from 6G and automotive to personal AI devices and robotics.

要点 · TL;DR

  • 高通从 5 瓦耳机芯片到 500 瓦数据中心芯片全覆盖，押注解耦的 AI 基础设施。
    Qualcomm spans 5W earbuds to 500W data center chips, betting on disaggregated AI infrastructure.
  • 6G 将于 2028-29 年到来，高通作为标准制定者提前多年布局，而非跟随。
    6G arrives 2028-29; Qualcomm invests early as standards creator, not just follower.
  • 美国能在芯片制造前沿竞争；来自中国的竞争让高通更强，而非更弱。
    US can lead-edge manufacture chips; competition from China made Qualcomm stronger, not weaker.

核心观点 · Key points

  • 高通的多元化覆盖从5瓦的耳机芯片到500瓦的数据中心芯片。
    Qualcomm's diversification spans from earbud chips at 5 watts to 500-watt data center chips.
  • 6G将在2028-2029年左右推出，高通作为标准制定者提前数年投资。
    6G will launch around 2028-2029, and Qualcomm is investing years ahead as a standards creator.
  • 个人AI设备将通过AI对视觉、语音和声音的理解，从根本上改变人机交互。
    Personal AI devices will fundamentally change human-computer interaction through AI understanding of sight, speech, and sound.
  • 数据中心将分解计算，需要不同的架构来处理预填充和解码推理。
    Data centers will disaggregate compute, requiring different architectures for prefill and decode inference.
  • 《芯片法案》正在发挥作用但需要时间；建立半导体生态系统需要长期投资。
    The CHIPS Act is working but takes time; building a semiconductor ecosystem requires long-term investment.
  • 移动领域的中国竞争教会了高通如何保持全球竞争力并避免自满。
    Competition from China in mobile taught Qualcomm to be globally competitive and avoid complacency.

反共识 · Contrarian takes

  • GPU并非AI的唯一解决方案；针对推理优化的NPU将在数据中心获得份额。
    The GPU is not the only solution for AI; NPUs optimized for inference will gain share in data centers.
  • 电力正成为数据中心的有限资源，迫使类似智能手机的计算分解。
    Power is becoming a finite resource in data centers, forcing disaggregation similar to smartphones.
  • 美国能在芯片制造前沿竞争；认为美国不行的前提是错误的。
    The US can compete at the leading edge of chip manufacturing; the premise that it can't is wrong.
  • 向中国出售先进芯片不一定有害；竞争使美国公司更强大。
    Selling advanced chips to China is not necessarily bad; competition makes US firms stronger.
  • 特定任务的机器人已近在眼前，但通用家用机器人还需数年。
    Robots for specific tasks are right in front of us, but general-purpose home robots are years away.
