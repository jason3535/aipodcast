AI 时代的设计领导力

Design Leadership in the Age of AI

本期速览 · Overview

Hannah Hearth 探讨 AI 工具如何重塑设计实践，以及这对设计领导者的意义，并分享了她在设计领导岗位面试中接受实践作业的经历。

Hannah Hearth discusses how AI tools are reshaping design practice and what this means for design leaders, sharing her experience with a take-home assignment in a design leadership interview.

要点 · TL;DR

  • AI 时代的设计领导者必须亲力亲为，交付成果，而不是仅仅管理团队。
    AI-era design leaders must be hands-on, shipping work rather than just overseeing teams.
  • 作品集应侧重展示使用 AI 工具和副项目的实验，而非精致的案例研究。
    Portfolios should highlight experimentation with AI tools and side projects over polished case studies.
  • 新的设计领导者必须快速交付成果，摒弃传统的 90 天倾听之旅。
    New design leaders must deliver results quickly, abandoning the traditional 90-day listening tour.

核心观点 · Key points

  • 出色的设计领导者如今要深度参与执行和交付上线，而不是招到聪明人之后就放手不管。
    Great design leaders today are deeply involved in execution and shipping, not hiring smart people and getting out of their way.
  • 市场回调是一次长期的方向修正；设计团队必须借助 AI 工具以更少人力做更多事，同时避免倦怠。
    The market correction is a permanent course correction, so design teams must do more with less through AI tools while avoiding burnout.
  • 强制要求每天使用 AI 可能显得残酷，但这是企业抢在别人之前自我颠覆的必要之恶。
    Requiring daily AI use can feel ruthless, but it is a necessary evil for companies to disrupt themselves first.
  • 设计冲刺已从五整天关在房间里，演变为每天简短同步加上独立原型制作，而且往往第一天就做出原型。
    The design sprint has evolved from five days in a room to short daily syncs plus independent prototyping, often by day one.
  • 设计师应该尽早、频繁地分享作品；等到每周评审会才拿反馈是错误时机。
    Designers should share work early and often; waiting for weekly critique meetings is the wrong time to get feedback.
  • 作品集应该展示对新工具和副项目的尝试，而不只是精致的流程堆砌式案例研究。
    Portfolios should show experimentation with new tools and side projects, not just polished process-heavy case studies.

反共识 · Contrarian takes

  • 越来越多资深设计领导者回归 IC（独立贡献者）岗位，因为缺少亲自动手的工具经验几乎无法领导 AI 时代的设计。
    Senior design leaders are increasingly returning to IC work because leading AI-era design without hands-on tool experience is nearly impossible.
  • 传统的 30-60-90 天倾听之旅已行不通；新领导者必须在一个月内拿出成果并调整团队。
    The old 30-60-90-day listening tour no longer works; new leaders must deliver outcomes and restructure teams within a month.
  • 设计师应该重新拥抱编码；在专业化时代之前，每位设计师都是前端开发，而这能显著提升设计影响力。
    Designers should embrace coding again; before the specialization era, every designer was a front-end developer, and that materially increases design impact.
  • 如果你的强项是产品思维而非打磨细节，不妨考虑转向产品管理，而不是硬做设计作品集。
    If your strength is product thinking rather than craft, consider pivoting to product management instead of forcing a design portfolio.
  • 设计系统在 AI 时代不是更没价值，而是更有价值，因为完善的文档能训练大语言模型更快地产出更好的界面。
    Design systems are more valuable with AI, not less, because robust documentation trains LLMs to produce faster, better UI.
