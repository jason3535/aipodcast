本期速览 · Overview

Hannah Hearth 探讨 AI 工具如何重塑设计实践，以及这对设计领导者的意义，并分享了她在设计领导岗位面试中接受实践作业的经历。

Hannah Hearth discusses how AI tools are reshaping design practice and what this means for design leaders, sharing her experience with a take-home assignment in a design leadership interview.

要点 · TL;DR

  • AI 时代的设计领导者必须亲力亲为，交付成果，而不是仅仅管理团队。
    AI-era design leaders must be hands-on, shipping work rather than just overseeing teams.
  • 作品集应侧重展示使用 AI 工具和副项目的实验，而非精致的案例研究。
    Portfolios should highlight experimentation with AI tools and side projects over polished case studies.
  • 新的设计领导者必须快速交付成果，摒弃传统的 90 天倾听之旅。
    New design leaders must deliver results quickly, abandoning the traditional 90-day listening tour.

核心观点 · Key points

  • 设计领导者必须是深度参与的伙伴，帮助资深设计师应对组织政治和模糊性，而不是雇完人就撒手的经理。
    Design leaders today must be deeply involved partners, helping senior designers navigate politics and ambiguity, not managers who hire and step aside.
  • AI 工具正在重塑设计行业，团队必须积极采用它们，即使是被强制使用，因为颠覆就在眼前。
    AI tools are reshaping design, so teams must actively adopt them—even through forced usage—because disruption is imminent.
  • 尽早并频繁分享工作成果；等到每周评审会再获取反馈是错误的时机。
    Share work early and often; waiting for weekly critiques is the wrong time to get feedback.
  • 设计领导者越来越以实际发布并交付客户的产品来衡量，而不仅仅是精美的产出物。
    Design leaders are increasingly judged by what actually ships and lands with customers, not just polished deliverables.
  • 作品集应展示实验和个人项目；用新工具持续学习如今已成为基本门槛。
    Portfolios should showcase experiments and side projects; demonstrating continuous learning with new tools is now table stakes.
  • 叙事和获取认同是永恒技能，随着角色演变，它们会持续推动设计师前进。
    Storytelling and gaining buy-in are forever skills that carry designers forward as their roles evolve.

反共识 · Contrarian takes

  • “招到聪明人就别挡路”已经过时；实际上资深设计师比初级设计师需要更多支持。
    “Hire smart people and get out of their way” is outdated; senior designers actually need more support than juniors.
  • 市场修正不是一次性调整，而是永久性的方向修正，因此团队将长期保持精简。
    The market correction is not a one-time fix but a permanent course correction, so teams will stay lean.
  • 五天的设计冲刺正在压缩为一天或仅一小时同步的形式，原型制作从第一天就开始了。
    Five-day design sprints are collapsing into one-day or one-hour-sync formats, with prototyping starting on day one.
  • 与做出正确的解决方案相比，工艺的牺牲风险反而较小；削减对齐时间才是最糟糕的取舍。
    Craft is less at risk than solving the right problem; cutting alignment time is the worst thing to sacrifice.
  • 给设计领导者的家庭作业其实很有价值，尽管业内反对，但应被欢迎。
    Take-home assignments for design leaders are actually valuable and should be welcomed, despite industry backlash.
  • 偏重产品而非工艺的设计师，应认真考虑转向产品管理岗位。
    Designers who lean product rather than craft should seriously consider switching to product management.
