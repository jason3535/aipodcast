本期速览 · Overview

前 OpenAI 研究员 Diogo 探讨大语言模型过度承诺与交付不足之间的巨大鸿沟，追问为何 AI 在某些任务上表现出色却在其他任务上失灵。

Former OpenAI researcher Diogo explores the massive divide between LLM over-promise and under-delivery, asking why AI excels at some tasks yet fails at others.

要点 · TL;DR

  • 大语言模型被优化用于人类在环的辅助，而非可靠的自动化。
    LLMs are optimized for human-in-the-loop assistance, not reliable automation.
  • RLHF 导致模式崩溃，使模型输出看似合理但常常错误。
    RLHF causes mode-drop, making models produce plausible but often incorrect outputs.
  • 对 LLM 进步而言，做对任务比算力或数据更重要。
    Doing the right task matters more than compute or data for LLM progress.

核心观点 · Key points

  • 大多数 LLM 针对人类在环的辅助任务优化，而非可靠自动化。
    Most LLMs are optimized for human-in-the-loop assistance, not reliable automation.
  • 基于人类反馈的强化学习（RLHF）导致模型模式坍缩，输出看似合理却常出错。
    RLHF makes models mode-drop, producing plausible but often incorrect outputs.
  • 对 LLM 进步而言，做对任务比算力或数据更重要。
    Doing the right task matters more than compute or data for LLM progress.
  • 不要让 AI 做有风险的决定，必须保持人类在环。
    Don't let AI make decisions with stakes; keep humans in the loop.
  • 该领域能超越 RLHF，自动化是不可避免的未来。
    The field can do better than RLHF; automation is the inevitable future.
  • 缩放定律有效是因为数据可得且任务正确，而非仅靠算力。
    Scaling laws work because data is available and task is right, not just compute.

反共识 · Contrarian takes

  • 苦涩教训是错的，数据和任务比算力更重要。
    The bitter lesson is wrong; data and task matter more than compute.
  • 编码智能体是辅助而非自动化，尽管普遍认为相反。
    Coding agents are assistance, not automation, despite common belief.
  • 精度需求上升时 LLM 效用下降，与传统机器学习相反。
    As precision needs rise, LLM utility drops—opposite of traditional ML.
  • ChatGPT 没完蛋，世界才完蛋，因为对成瘾的优化不可避免。
    ChatGPT is not cooked; the world is, due to inevitable optimization for addiction.
  • 无法同时兼顾辅助与自动化，同时优化会撕裂优化空间。
    You can't have both assistance and automation; optimizing both shatters the space.
  • AI 经济革命未发生，因为我们优化了错误的任务。
    The AI economic revolution hasn't happened because we optimized for the wrong task.
