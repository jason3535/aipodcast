本期速览 · Overview

Isomorphic Labs 总裁 Max Jaderberg 讲述 AlphaFold 的概念验证如何推动用 AI 设计药物、攻克所有疾病的使命。

Max Jaderberg, President of Isomorphic Labs, explains how AlphaFold's proof of concept is driving a mission to solve all diseases with AI-designed medicines.

要点 · TL;DR

  • AI 的下一个万亿美元市场是药物发现而非软件，由 AlphaFold 式的突破驱动。
    AI's next trillion-dollar market is drug discovery, not software, driven by AlphaFold-like breakthroughs.
  • 在 AI 驱动的药物发现中，真正的瓶颈是数据，而非算法或算力。
    Data, not algorithms or compute, is the real bottleneck in AI-driven drug discovery.
  • AI 让研究者定义设计空间、由智能体运行实验，从而实现个性化医疗。
    AI shifts researchers to define design spaces while agents run experiments, enabling personalized medicine.

核心观点 · Key points

  • AlphaFold 证明了 AI 模型能在生物学和化学领域泛化，为攻克所有疾病的使命提供了依据。
    AlphaFold proved AI models can generalize in biology and chemistry, justifying the mission to solve all diseases.
  • 制药需要解决十几个难题；我们需要将六个以上类似 AlphaFold 的突破结合起来。
    Making medicines requires a dozen hard problems; we need half a dozen AlphaFold-like breakthroughs combined.
  • AI 在两方面提供帮助：理解疾病生物学和设计分子来调控它。
    AI helps on two fronts: understanding disease biology and designing molecules to modulate it.
  • 将宏大的定性使命转化为定量的排行榜，以聚焦团队并跟踪进展。
    Translate big qualitative missions into quantitative leaderboards to focus teams and track progress.
  • 招聘不同领域的顶尖专家，并鼓励他们学习彼此的语言。
    Hire top specialists from different disciplines and encourage them to learn each other's language.
  • AI 改变了研究人员的角色：他们定义设计空间和目标，而智能体负责运行实验。
    AI changes researchers' roles: they define design spaces and goals, while agents run experiments.

反共识 · Contrarian takes

  • 在药物发现中，你不需要完美的预测精度就能创造真实价值；显著的提升就足够了。
    In drug discovery, you don't need perfect predictive accuracy to create real value; a significant delta suffices.
  • 我们几乎拥有解决任何问题的算法和算力；真正的瓶颈是数据，而不是模型。
    We have algorithms and compute for almost any problem; data is the real bottleneck, not models.
  • 与其让实验室在环，不如通过世界模型将真实世界搬到计算机上，以减少实验。
    Instead of lab-in-the-loop, bring the real world onto a computer via world models to reduce experiments.
  • 招聘没有生物学背景的 AI 科学家能带来第一性原理思维，有利于药物设计。
    Hiring AI scientists with no biology background brings first-principles thinking that benefits drug design.
  • 即使模型达到 AGI，人类注意力、时间和物理空间仍是最终瓶颈。
    Even as models reach AGI, human attention, time, and physical space remain the ultimate bottlenecks.
  • AI 将实现针对个体患者的个性化医疗，而非一刀切的药物。
    AI will enable personalized medicine tailored to individual patients, not one-size-fits-all drugs.
