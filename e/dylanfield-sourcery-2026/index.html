本期速览 · Overview

Figma CEO Dylan Field 探讨 Config 大会、AI 时代设计的价值，以及为什么设计远未消亡。

Figma CEO Dylan Field discusses the Config conference, the value of design in the age of AI, and why design is far from dead.

要点 · TL;DR

  • 设计比以往任何时候都更重要，用于构建可扩展的真实系统，而非已死。
    Design is more important than ever for building scalable, real systems, not dead.
  • 没有人的引导和观点，AI 工具只能产生平庸的结果。
    AI tools produce average results without human steering and a point of view.
  • 品牌的信任是通过一致性和创造比获取更多的价值来建立的。
    Trust in brands is built through consistency and creating more value than you capture.

核心观点 · Key points

  • 设计没有死；对于构建可扩展的真实系统，它比以往任何时候都更重要。
    Design is not dead; it's more important than ever for building scalable, real systems.
  • 没有人类的引导和观点，AI 工具只会产生平庸的结果。
    AI tools produce average results without human steering and a point of view.
  • 品牌信任是通过长期的稳定性和创造比获取更多的价值来建立的。
    Trust in brands is built through consistency over time and creating more value than you capture.
  • AI 模型的越狱研究不足，需要更多激励来进行压力测试。
    Jailbreaking AI models is understudied and needs more incentives for battle testing.
  • 与模型交互的未来将超越提示，出现新的界面。
    The future of interaction with models will evolve beyond prompting, with new interfaces.

反共识 · Contrarian takes

  • 永远独自工作的想法是孤独的，且不可扩展。
    The idea that you can work alone on everything forever is lonely and not scalable.
  • 设计和判断力与给模型增加智商点无关。
    Design and judgment are not correlated with adding IQ points to models.
  • 无聊的软件被低估了；许多大问题缺乏软件解决方案。
    Boring software is undercounted; many huge problems lack software solutions.
  • 平台的定义是在平台之外创造比平台本身更多的价值。
    The definition of a platform is creating more value outside than from the platform itself.
  • 我们仍处于提示时代，但还有其他探索潜在空间的方法。
    We are still in a prompting age, but there are other ways to explore latent space.
