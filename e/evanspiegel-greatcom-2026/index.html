Evan Spiegel on Building Snapchat and Battling Meta

本期速览 · Overview

Snapchat 创始人埃文·斯皮格尔回顾早期保密策略、Meta 的“Poke”抄袭事件，以及他作为领导者的成长。

Snapchat founder Evan Spiegel reflects on early secrecy, Meta's 'Poke' ripoff, and his evolution as a leader.

要点 · TL;DR

  • Snap 早期保密是巨大错误；透明能塑造文化并汇集最佳创意。
    Snap's early secrecy was a mistake; transparency builds culture and surfaces best ideas.
  • 让计算更人性化意味着优先连接而非竞争性互动。
    Making computing more human means prioritizing connection over competitive engagement.
  • 对创始人而言，韧性与快速纠错比任何银弹都重要。
    Resilience and quick error correction matter more than any silver bullet for founders.

核心观点 · Key points

  • 早期 Snapchat 的保密是个错误；内部透明与沟通对文化和愿景至关重要。
    Early secrecy at Snapchat was a mistake; internal transparency and communication became essential for culture and vision.
  • 计算的目标应是更人性化，融入我们自然生活、工作与连接的方式。
    The goal of computing should be to make it more human, fitting into natural ways we live, work, and connect.
  • 社交媒体应优化人际连接与关系，而非竞争和表现指标。
    Social media should optimize for human connection and relationships rather than competition and performance metrics.
  • 创始人的成功在于坚韧和不放弃，而非特殊天赋或策略。
    Success in founders comes from resilience and not giving up, more than any special talent or strategy.
  • 奖励快速认错能让组织更快学习和成长。
    Rewarding the speed of admitting when you are wrong lets an organization learn and grow faster.
  • 共享体验和当下共处应塑造未来技术设计。
    Shared experiences and being present together in the moment should shape future technology design.

反共识 · Contrarian takes

  • IPO 令人清醒，是更艰难阶段的开始，而非庆祝终点。
    An IPO felt sobering and marked the beginning of a harder phase, not a celebratory end.
  • 由于 Snapchat 以消息为导向，其增长实际比广播型社交媒体更慢，而非爆发式。
    Because Snapchat is messaging-oriented, its growth was actually slower than broadcast social media, not explosive.
  • 童年被禁止看电视是馈赠，鼓励了阅读和想象，尽管有社交劣势。
    Being allowed no television as a child was a gift that encouraged reading and imagination, despite social downsides.
  • 在估值 8 亿美元时提前套现 1000 万美元减轻压力，促成更大长期押注。
    At a $800 million valuation, selling $10 million of stock early reduced stress and enabled bigger long-term bets.
  • 在 AR 眼镜中，物理物体可由光替代，提升可及性、环保性和经济性。
    Physical objects can be replaced by light in augmented reality glasses, improving accessibility, environment, and economics.
  • 承担风险的最佳条件是下行空间极小，因此从斯坦福退学近乎完美境况。
    The best way to take risk is when downside is minimal, so dropping out of Stanford was almost the perfect circumstance.
