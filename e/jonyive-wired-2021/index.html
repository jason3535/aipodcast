本期速览 · Overview

在这次访谈中，Jony Ive 回顾了 iPod 发布 20 周年，分享了设计 Apple Watch 等可穿戴设备的挑战，并展望了个人设备的未来。

In this interview, Jony Ive reflects on the 20th anniversary of the iPod, the challenges of designing wearables like the Apple Watch, and the future of personal devices.

要点 · TL;DR

  • iPod 标志着苹果转向个人化、特定用途的设备，塑造了其在可穿戴设备领域的未来。
    The iPod marked Apple's pivot to personal, specific devices, shaping its future in wearables.
  • 可穿戴设备将变得更加私密，有些最终会消失在皮肤之下。
    Wearables will become more intimate, some eventually disappearing beneath the skin.
  • 史蒂夫·乔布斯真正的遗产是他对创造过程的好奇与敬畏，而不仅仅是他的成就。

    Wearables will become more intimate, with some eventually disappearing beneath the skin.
