本期速览 · Overview

帕特里克·科利森探讨了从第一性原理学习的价值、为何仍亲自写作，以及他两次辍学创业的经历。

Patrick Collison discusses the value of learning from first principles, why he still writes himself, and his experience dropping out of college twice to start companies.

要点 · TL;DR

  • 即使有先进的 AI，认知 L1 缓存知识在解决问题时仍然更快、更有价值。

  • 写作和人际沟通是模型仍缺乏的基本技能，因此亲自去做很重要。

核心观点 · Key points

  • 即便有了先进的AI，认知一级缓存中的知识在解决问题时仍然更快、更有价值。
    Even with advanced AI, cognitive L1 cache knowledge remains faster and more valuable for problem-solving.
  • 写作和人际沟通是模型仍欠缺的基本技能，因此亲自去做很重要。
    Writing and interpersonal communication are fundamental skills that models still lack, so doing them yourself matters.
  • 创办一家基于具体、真实客户问题的公司对成功至关重要。
    Starting a company grounded in a concrete, real customer problem is crucial for success.
  • 从一开始就拥有生产用户，即使在公开发布之前，也能提供必要的依据和反馈。
    Having production users from the very beginning provides essential grounding and feedback, even before a public launch.
  • 在AI时代，初创公司应考虑更宏大、更发散的起点，而不是狭窄的利基市场。
    In the AI era, startups should consider more ambitious and divergent starting points rather than narrow niches.
  • Stripe的数据显示，现在是创办企业的最佳时机，更多初创公司正更快地取得成功。
    Stripe data shows it's a better time than ever to start a business, with more startups succeeding faster.

反共识 · Contrarian takes

  • 辍学创业并不冒险；成本微乎其微，之后也没人在意。
    Dropping out of college to start a company is not risky; the costs are minimal and nobody cares later.
  • 如果你现在不创业就会被困在永久下层阶级的担忧被夸大了；机会是充足的。
    The fear of being trapped in a permanent underclass if you don't start a company now is overblown; opportunities are abundant.
  • 等待两年才发布Stripe是正确的选择，与“尽早发布”的信条相反，因为他们很早就有了生产用户。
    Waiting two years to launch Stripe was the right call, contrary to the 'launch early' mantra, because they had production users early.
  • 精益创业方法正变得不那么有效；在AI时代，反精益策略更为成功。
    The lean startup approach is becoming less effective; anti-lean strategies are more successful in the AI era.
  • AI不会导致霸权集中化；相反，它将创造一个更加去中心化、多赢家的世界。
    AI will not lead to hegemonic centralization; instead, it will create a more decentralized world with many winners.
  • 即使有强大的AI，自己写文章仍然更胜一筹；模型生成的文章并不引人入胜。
    Even with powerful AI, writing essays yourself is still superior; model-generated writing is not compelling.
