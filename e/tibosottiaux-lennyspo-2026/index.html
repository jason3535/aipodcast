本期速览 · Overview

OpenAI 的 ChatGPT 负责人蒂博·索蒂奥解释为何互联网上的大多数操作将很快由 AI 代理完成，以及我们将如何摆脱对循环的繁琐调试。

OpenAI's head of ChatGPT, Tibo Sottiaux, explains why most internet actions will soon be taken by AI agents and how we'll break free from fiddling with loops.

要点 · TL;DR

  • 未来大多数互联网操作将由 AI 代理完成，因此应为这一未来而构建。
    Most internet actions will soon be taken by AI agents, so build for that future.
  • 一个持续学习、持久存在的代理将取代手动循环和图结构。
    A single persistent learning agent will replace manual loops and graphs.
  • 人类的品味、创造力以及知道什么是好的能力比以往任何时候都更有价值。
    Human taste, creativity, and knowing what good looks like are more valuable than ever.

核心观点 · Key points

  • 互联网上的大多数操作将由智能体完成，因此要为这一未来构建产品。
    Most internet actions will be taken by agents, so build for that future.
  • 一个持久、持续学习的智能体将取代手动设置的循环和图。
    A single persistent, learning agent will replace manual loops and graphs.
  • AI 应成为永久活跃的智能，可在任何设备上使用。
    AI should be a permanent active intelligence, available on any device.
  • 人类的品味、创造力和知道什么是好的能力比以往更有价值。
    Human taste, creativity, and knowing what good looks like are more valuable than ever.
  • 控制前沿意味着在 Alignment（对齐）、安全和保障上大力投入。
    Pacing the frontier means investing heavily in alignment, safety, and security.
  • 开放生态系统和共享经济将推动下一波 AI 应用浪潮。
    Open ecosystems with shared economics will drive the next wave of AI applications.

反共识 · Contrarian takes

  • 摆弄循环和图不是未来；一个能学习的系统才是。
    Fiddling with loops and graphs is not the future; a learning system is.
  • 快速打字是一项正在贬值的技能；品味和用户同理心正在升值。
    Typing fast is a declining skill; taste and user empathy are rising.
  • 应届毕业生有优势，因为他们可以全身心投入 AI，没有旧习惯的束缚。
    New grads have an advantage because they can go all-in on AI without old habits.
  • 语音和听写对于许多任务来说，出乎意料地比打字更好。
    Voice and dictation are unexpectedly better than typing for many tasks.
  • 模型选择器和配置疲劳应该被消除，而不是被推崇。
    Model pickers and configuration fatigue should be eliminated, not celebrated.
  • AI 应该减少噪音和会议，而不是逼你同时运行 30 个智能体。
    AI should reduce noise and meetings, not push you to run 30 agents at once.
