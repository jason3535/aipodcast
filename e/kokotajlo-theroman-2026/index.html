实证对齐研究的局限 Limitations of Empirical Alignment Research

Daniel

But that doesn't mean that there isn't some new situation that we never thought of that could change their goals. The example I would give for this is with humans. So a similar thing could happen with these types of AIs where even after we've done step one, step two, and step three, there could just be some future situation that we didn't anticipate that has an unexpected effect on the personality traits and goals and so forth of our AIs.
