本期速览 · Overview

SpaceX 公布第三季度营收 146 亿美元、净利润 44 亿美元，Starship 第 14 次飞行入轨，超级智能成为增长引擎。

SpaceX reports $14.6 billion in Q3 revenue and $4.4 billion in net income, with Starship Flight 14 reaching orbit and superintelligence as the growth driver.

要点 · TL;DR

  • 第三季度营收达到 146 亿美元，增长 161%，净利润 44 亿美元。
    Q3 revenue hit $14.6 billion, up 161%, and net income was $4.4 billion.
  • Starship 第 14 次飞行入轨；下个月的第 15 次将尝试接住助推器和飞船。
    Starship Flight 14 reached orbit; Flight 15 next month will try to catch the booster and the ship.
  • 超级智能是增长引擎，他说到 2027 年底年度经常性收入大概在 1000 亿到 1 万亿美元之间。
    Superintelligence is the growth driver, with a stated path from about $100 billion to $1 trillion of ARR by the end of 2027.

核心观点 · Key points

  • 第三季度营收 146 亿美元，增长 161%，净利润 44 亿美元，调整后 EBITDA 为 88 亿美元。
    Q3 revenue was $14.6 billion, up 161%, with $4.4 billion of net income and $8.8 billion of adjusted EBITDA.
  • Starship 第 14 次飞行入轨并部署了投入运营的 Starlink V3 卫星；第 15 次将尝试捕获两级。
    Starship Flight 14 reached orbit and deployed operational Starlink V3 satellites; Flight 15 will try to catch both stages.
  • 埃隆对 2027 年再增加 10 吉瓦算力有信心，并瞄准再增加 20 吉瓦电力与冷却。
    Elon is confident of 10 more gigawatts of compute in 2027 and aims for 20 more gigawatts of power and cooling.
  • Grok Bot 是他见过增长最快的产品，付费周活 80 万，一个月内翻了一倍多。
    Grok Bot is the fastest product growth he has seen, with 800,000 paid weekly users, more than doubled in a month.
  • Starlink 本季度新增 160 万消费者。格温说略低于 1400 万，布雷特说 1360 万。
    Starlink added 1.6 million consumers in the quarter. Gwynne said just under 14 million; Bret said 13.6 million.

反共识 · Contrarian takes

  • 在他看来，SpaceX 与 Tesla 合并是个好主意，但不是定局。Tesla 需要全部股份的 50%，而他不在特别委员会的房间里。
    A SpaceX–Tesla combination is a good idea in his view, not a certainty. Tesla would need 50% of all shares, and he is not in the special-committee room.
  • 他说每瓦算力的价值会上升，所以 SpaceX 按现货价格出租。即便 H100，现在的租金也高于全新时。
    He says the value of compute per watt will rise, so SpaceX rents at spot rates. Even H100s now rent for more than when they were new.
  • 明年在线的超级智能算力超过 20 吉瓦，可能性小，但已经不再是零。
    There is a small but no-longer-zero chance of more than 20 gigawatts of superintelligence compute online next year.
  • 他看到连接收入达到每年数千亿美元的路径，但不会在几分钟里讲清楚。
    He sees a path to several hundred billion dollars a year in connectivity revenue, and will not spell it out in a few minutes.
  • 明年算力六个月回收是可能的，不是承诺。到别处去租 GB300、GB200、TPU、Trainium 或 H100，像一家没有奶酪的奶酪店。
    A six-month payback on compute next year is possible, not promised. Trying to rent GB300s, GB200s, TPUs, Trainium, or H100s elsewhere is a cheese shop with no cheese.
