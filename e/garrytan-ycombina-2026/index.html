本期速览 · Overview

一场关于个人 AGI 的演讲，它如同斯宾诺莎的上帝，弥散于基础设施之中，而非单一事件，并阐述为何你应该构建自己的 AGI。

A talk on how personal AGI, like Spinoza's God, is diffused through infrastructure, not a singular event, and why you should build your own.

要点 · TL;DR

  • 个人 AGI 是运行在你基础设施上的智能体，读取你拥有的记忆，每日复利增长。
    Personal AGI is an agent on your infrastructure, reading from your memory, compounding daily.
  • 技能文件将纯英语转化为代码，让任何人都能通过 AI 智能体成为程序员。
    Skill files turn plain English into code, making anyone a programmer with AI agents.
  • 拥有你的技能文件，否则你的工作就会变成别人拥有的技能文件。
    Own your skill files; otherwise your job becomes a skill file owned by someone else.

核心观点 · Key points

  • 个人AGI是运行在你自己的基础设施上、读取你拥有的记忆并每日复利的智能体。
    Personal AGI is an agent running on your infrastructure, reading from memory you own, and compounding daily.
  • AI智能体带来的倍增效应适用于所有知识工作，而不仅仅是编码，可实现100倍的产出。
    The multiplier from AI agents applies to all knowledge work, not just coding, enabling 100x output.
  • 技能文件是用普通英语编写的指令，将智能体变成员工，使Markdown成为一种代码形式。
    Skill files are plain English instructions that turn agents into employees, making markdown a form of code.
  • 随着模型改进，构建个人上下文库是关键差异化因素；你的库就是你的护城河。
    Building a personal library of context is the key differentiator as models improve; the library is your moat.
  • 从一个Markdown文件文件夹开始，为讨厌的任务编写技能，并每日复利。
    Start small with a folder of markdown files, write skills for hated tasks, and compound daily.
  • 拥有你的技能文件；否则，你的工作就会变成别人拥有的技能文件。
    Own your skill files; if you don't, your job becomes a skill file owned by someone else.

反共识 · Contrarian takes

  • AGI并非作为单一事件到来，而是以个人智能体的形式扩散，已经在我们身边。
    AGI is not arriving as a singular event but diffused as personal agents, already in the room.
  • 最优秀的创始人将AI视为劳动力，而非自动补全，从而实现了前所未有的人均收入。
    The best founders treat AI as a workforce, not autocomplete, leading to unprecedented revenue per person.
  • Markdown就是代码；任何能用清晰英语写作的人都可以用AI智能体编程。
    Markdown is code; anyone who can write clear English can program with AI agents.
  • 你生活数据的默认状态并非隐私；将其整合到自己的系统中是保管，而非风险。
    The default state of your life data is not privacy; consolidating it into your own system is custody, not risk.
  • 你受到的嘲讽和批评只是采用曲线在宣告自身。
    The dunks and criticism you receive are just the adoption curve announcing itself.
  • 一个人脑中只能记住七件事的限制已经失效；你现在可以飞翔了。
    The constraint of one person holding seven things in their head has expired; you can fly now.
