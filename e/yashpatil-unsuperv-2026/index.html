Applied Compute CEO Yash explains why owning your own intelligence is about flexibility and control, and how post-training is becoming the key to winning inference.

要点 · TL;DR

  • 后训练推动帕累托前沿，在推理规模上取胜。
    Post-training pushes the Pareto frontier, winning inference at scale.
  • 强化学习是爬山，定义山才是难点。
    RL is hill-climbing; defining the hill is the hard part.
  • 多数公司需要优化工具链，而非预训练自己的模型。
    Most firms need harness optimization, not pre-training their own models.

核心观点 · Key points

  • 拥有自己的智能意味着对模型部署的灵活性和控制权，而不仅仅是避免实验室的恶意行为。
    Owning your intelligence means flexibility and control over model deployment, not just avoiding malicious labs.
  • 后训练可以推动帕累托曲线外移，实现更好的能力和成本效率。
    Post-training can push the Pareto curve out, enabling better capabilities and cost efficiency.
  • 未来是多模型的：公司希望在开源和专有模型之间拥有选择和灵活性。
    The future is multi-model: companies want choice and flexibility across open and proprietary models.
  • 强化学习是一台爬山机；最难的部分是定义要爬的山。
    RL is a hill-climbing machine; the hardest part is defining the hill to climb.
  • 专有数据和专家判断轨迹是分布外后训练的关键。
    Proprietary data and expert judgment traces are key for out-of-distribution post-training.
  • 推理是大部分价值捕获的地方；后训练在规模上赢得推理。
    Inference is where most value is captured; post-training wins inference at scale.

反共识 · Contrarian takes

  • 强化学习的泛化能力不如预训练；跨领域迁移有限。
    RL does not generalize as well as pre-training; transfer across domains is limited.
  • 对于大多数开源模型用例，成本优化比能力是更大的驱动力。
    Cost optimization is a bigger driver than capabilities for most open model use cases.
  • 大多数公司不需要预训练自己的模型；工具链优化应优先。
    Most companies don't need to pre-train their own models; harness optimization comes first.
  • 最好的工程师在AI出现之前就学会了编程；系统设计比语法更重要。
    The best engineers learned to code before AI; system design matters more than syntax.
  • 杰文斯悖论是真实的：更便宜的模型导致更多使用，而不是更少。
    Jevons paradox is real: cheaper models lead to more usage, not less.
  • 你可以通过让模型更token高效来削减token账单，而不仅仅是推理优化。
    You can cut token bills by making models more token-efficient, not just inference optimization.
