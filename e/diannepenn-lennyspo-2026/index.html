本期速览 · Overview

Anthropic 研究实验室产品主管分享公司早期挣扎、Opus 3 与 Claude Code 的突破，以及 AI 时代产品管理角色的演变。

Anthropic's Head of Product for Research and Labs shares the story of the company's early struggles, the breakthrough with Opus 3 and Claude Code, and the changing role of product management in the age of AI.

要点 · TL;DR

  • Claude 的编码能力，尤其是 Claude Code 和 Opus 45，推动了其采用，并使 Anthropic 成为领导者。
    Claude's coding capabilities, especially Claude Code and Opus 45, drove its adoption and positioned Anthropic as a leader.
  • Anthropic 的自力更生文化和小团队实验培养了快速创新和适应性。
    Anthropic's bootstrapped culture and small-team experimentation foster rapid innovation and adaptability.
  • 评估取代了传统的产品需求文档，成为核心产品管理工具，实现数据驱动决策。
    Evals replace traditional PRDs as core product management tools, enabling data-driven decisions.

核心观点 · Key points

  • 编码成为关键差异化因素；Claude Code 和 Opus 45 推动了采用率。
    Coding became a key differentiator; Claude Code and Opus 45 drove adoption.
  • 自举式文化和小团队实验有助于创新。
    Bootstrapped culture and small team experimentation foster innovation.
  • 评估取代了传统产品需求文档，成为核心产品管理工具。
    Evals replace traditional PRDs as core product management tools.
  • 模型的指数级增长要求适应性和第一性原理思考。
    Exponential model growth demands adaptability and first-principles thinking.
  • 用户反馈需转化为可执行的具体细节，供研究人员使用。
    User feedback must be translated into actionable specifics for researchers.

反共识 · Contrarian takes

  • 安全和对齐使Claude能够提出异议，从而更有用。
    Safety and alignment make Claude more useful by enabling pushback.
  • AI写作目前生硬，但优先级低于智能体能力。
    AI writing is currently stiff but deprioritized over agentic capabilities.
  • 最大化令牌消耗就是活在未来并获取超额收益。
    Maximizing token spend is living in the future and capturing alpha.
  • 产品经理必须像写测试驱动开发那样写评估，而不仅仅是文档。
    PMs must write evals like test-driven development, not just documents.
  • 管理者必须保持动手并交付产品，才能有效领导。
    Managers must stay hands-on and ship products to lead effectively.
