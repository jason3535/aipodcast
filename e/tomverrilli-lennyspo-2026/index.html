我们为何后悔产品管理存在

Why We Regret Product Management Exists

本期速览 · Overview

Whatnot 首席产品官 Tom Verrilli 解释为何他的团队避免招聘产品经理，而是赋能工程师和设计师做决策。

Tom Verrilli, CPO of Whatnot, explains why his team avoids hiring PMs and instead empowers engineers and designers to make decisions.

要点 · TL;DR

  • 产品管理应是专门角色，而非每个团队的默认配置；应赋能工程师和设计师。
    Product management should be a specialized role, not a default on every team; empower engineers and designers instead.
  • 资深产品经理应专注于个人贡献者工作，利用 AI 直接理解数据和代码。
    Senior PMs should focus on individual contributor work, leveraging AI to understand data and code directly.
  • 核心产品技能如客户理解和技术深度比政治协调更有价值。
    Core product skills like customer understanding and technical depth are more valuable than political alignment.

核心观点 · Key points

  • 产品管理不应成为每个团队的默认角色；只在有特定需求的地方招聘产品经理。
    Product management should not be a default role on every team; hire PMs only where there is a specific need.
  • 让工程师和设计师承担产品管理工作，能锻炼他们的决策能力，从而打造更好的产品。
    Empowering engineers and designers to do PM work builds their decision-making muscles and leads to better products.
  • 资深产品经理应将大部分时间花在个人贡献者工作上，而不仅仅是管理人。
    Senior PMs should spend most of their time on individual contributor work, not just managing people.
  • AI 工具使产品经理能够直接进行数据科学和了解代码库，提升他们的杠杆作用和速度。
    AI tools enable PMs to do data science and understand codebases directly, increasing their leverage and speed.
  • 核心产品技能，如理解客户、业务和技术，正变得比政治技能更有价值。
    Core product skills like understanding customers, business, and tech are becoming more valuable than political skills.
  • 当领导者深入细节并与团队一起寻求真相时，自上而下的领导是有效的。
    Top-down leadership works when leaders are deeply involved in details and seek truth alongside their teams.

反共识 · Contrarian takes

  • 雇佣过多的产品经理会使工程师和设计师变得幼稚化，他们本有能力做出好的决策。
    Hiring many PMs infantilizes engineers and designers, who are capable of making good decisions if given the chance.
  • 最好的产品经理不是那些专注于对齐和利益相关者管理的人，而是那些做出决策并交付的人。
    The best PMs are not those who focus on alignment and stakeholder management, but those who make decisions and ship.
  • 将顶尖产品经理晋升为总监职位会使他们脱离实际工作，适得其反；应让他们保持个人贡献者角色。
    Promoting top PMs to director roles removes them from the work, which is counterproductive; keep them as ICs.
  • AI 使产品经理更容易亲力亲为，但摆脱产品经理密集型组织的转变不仅是技术性的，也是文化性的。
    AI makes it easier for PMs to be hands-on, but the shift away from PM-heavy orgs is also cultural, not just technological.
  • 平均值会撒谎；产品经理必须考虑个体用例，避免弃用对少数群体至关重要的功能。
    Averages lie; PMs must consider individual use cases and avoid deprecating features that are critical for a small group.
  • 直播电商和智能体电商满足不同需求；智能体电商不会取代社交性、低意图的购物体验。
    Live commerce and agentic commerce serve different needs; agentic commerce won't replace the social, low-intent shopping experience.
