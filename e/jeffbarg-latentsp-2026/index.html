本期速览 · Overview

Clay 人工智能负责人 Jeff Barg 讲述 Clay 如何把表格变成 GTM 智能体平台，并塑造出“GTM 工程师”这一角色。

Jeff Barg, Head of AI at Clay, explains how Clay turned the spreadsheet into a platform for GTM agents and coined the GTM engineer.

要点 · TL;DR

  • Clay 构建的 GTM 智能体像销售代表一样，在整个表格中完成资格审核、数据丰富和研究。
    Clay builds GTM agents that qualify, enrich, and research across the whole table like sales reps.
  • 模型路由是关键：前沿模型用于编排，更便宜的模型用于简单任务；自动模式胜过手动选择。
    Model routing is key: frontier models for orchestration, cheaper ones for simple tasks; auto mode beats manual selection.
  • 机构勇气是最重要的；持续推动，不要过度设计智能体或过早进入后训练。
    Institutional courage is the most important thing; keep pushing and don't over-engineer agents or jump into post-training early.

核心观点 · Key points

  • Go-to-market 是一门创造性的工程学科；Clay 是为 GTM 工程师这一核心人群打造的。
    Go-to-market is a creative engineering discipline; Clay is built for the GTM engineer persona.
  • Clay 智能体应完成销售代表的工作：在整张表上做资格筛选、数据丰富与研究。
    Clay agents should do what sales reps did: qualify, enrich, and research across the whole table.
  • 模型路由很重要：编排层用前沿智能，简单任务用更便宜的模型。
    Model routing matters: frontier intelligence for orchestration, cheaper models for simple tasks.
  • 大多数用户讨厌模型选择器；自动模式应在任务层面胜过手动选择。
    Most users hate the model selector; auto mode should beat manual selection at task level.
  • 后训练与开放权重模型正变得可行，主要为了降本提速。
    Post-training and open-weight models are becoming viable mainly for cost and speed gains.
  • 机构勇气是当下公司最重要的东西；要持续坚持推动。
    Institutional courage is the most important thing at companies right now; keep pushing.

反共识 · Contrarian takes

  • 不要让自己的智能体明显强于 Clay 加 Claude Code；应把平台开放给所有智能体。
    Don't make your own agent markedly better than Clay plus Claude Code; expose the platform to all agents.
  • 训练前沿模型仍然极难；大多数公司过早投入后训练。
    Training a frontier model is still really hard; most companies jump into post-training too early.
  • 只有推理支出达到约 1000 万至 1 亿美元以上，降本才有意义；低于此则过早。
    Cost savings only matter above roughly $10-100M inference spend; below that it's premature.
  • 智能体仍不擅长诊断自己的轨迹；自我改进闭环大多行不通。
    Agents are still bad at diagnosing their own traces; self-improvement loops mostly don't work.
  • MCP 并非终局；团队已转向 CLI，接口形态本不该那么重要。
    MCP is not the endgame; the team shifted focus to a CLI, and form factor shouldn't matter much.
  • 现代数据栈已经过时；Clay 大部分产品数据基础设施在 Postgres 和 ClickHouse 上手写。
    Modern data stack is cooked; Clay hand-rolls most product data infrastructure on Postgres and ClickHouse.
