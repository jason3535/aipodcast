本期速览 · Overview

Alphabet 营收增长 24%，得益于 AI 投资，搜索增长 17%，云增长 82%，Gemini 模型快速普及。

Alphabet reports 24% revenue growth driven by AI investments, with Search up 17%, Cloud up 82%, and Gemini models seeing rapid adoption.

要点 · TL;DR

  • AI 投资推动 Alphabet 2026 年第二季度营收增长 24%.
    AI investments drove 24% revenue growth across Alphabet in Q2 2026.
  • Google Cloud 营收增长 82%，积压订单达 5140 亿美元，受企业 AI 需求推动.
    Google Cloud revenue surged 82% with $514B backlog, fueled by enterprise AI.
  • Gemini 模型深度集成到搜索、云和 YouTube 中，提升了变现能力.
    Gemini models are deeply integrated into Search, Cloud, and YouTube, boosting monetization.

核心观点 · Key points

  • AI 投资正在重新定义 Alphabet 业务的每个部分，推动收入增长 24%。
    AI investments are redefining every part of Alphabet's business, driving 24% revenue growth.
  • Google Cloud 收入增长 82%，积压订单达 5140 亿美元，受企业 AI 需求驱动。
    Google Cloud revenue grew 82% with backlog reaching $514 billion, driven by enterprise AI demand.
  • Gemini 模型深度集成到搜索、云和 YouTube 中，改善了变现和用户体验。
    Gemini models are deeply integrated across Search, Cloud, and YouTube, improving monetization and user experience.
  • 由于供应限制和强劲的 AI 需求，2026 年资本支出指引上调至 1950-2050 亿美元。
    CapEx guidance raised to $195-205 billion for 2026 due to supply constraints and strong AI demand.
  • Alphabet 仍致力于前沿模型开发，Gemini 4 的预训练显示出有希望的进展。
    Alphabet remains committed to frontier model development with Gemini 4 pre-training showing promising progress.

反共识 · Contrarian takes

  • Alphabet 使用第三方容量作为过渡策略，尽管面临利润率压力，但优先考虑长期客户价值。
    Alphabet uses third-party capacity as a bridging strategy despite margin pressure, prioritizing long-term customer value.
  • 向客户数据中心销售 TPU 系统扩大了总可寻址市场，但收入确认集中在 2027 年。
    TPU system sales to customer data centers expand TAM but revenue recognition is back-end loaded to 2027.
  • Alphabet 通过股权和债务融资以维持弹性资产负债表，而不仅仅是为了增长资金。
    Alphabet raised equity and debt to maintain a resilient balance sheet, not just for growth funding.
  • Gemini Flash 模型优先考虑性价比，而前沿领导力需要更大的基础模型，如 Gemini 4。
    Gemini Flash models are prioritized for cost-performance, while frontier leadership requires larger base models like Gemini 4.
  • Alphabet 首先将算力分配给 AGI 开发，然后是核心产品，平衡内部和外部需求。
    Alphabet allocates compute first to AGI development, then to core products, balancing internal and external demand.
