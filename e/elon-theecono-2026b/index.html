CNBC's Squawk Pod covers Elon Musk's wide-ranging interview, Lina Khan's new NYC role, Trump's latest tariffs, and Wall Street's race to access his Truth Social posts.

要点 · TL;DR

  • 马斯克预测 AI 将在 5 年内超越人类智能，使金钱变得无关紧要。
    Musk predicts AI will surpass human intelligence in 5 years, making money irrelevant.
  • 他现在接受 AI 加速，并主张前沿公司之间共享模型以进行安全测试。
    He now embraces AI acceleration and advocates for model sharing among frontier companies.
  • 他对欧洲移民和美国国际开发署持有争议观点，并坚决辩护。
    He holds controversial views on Europe's immigration and USAID, defending them adamantly.

核心观点 · Key points

  • AI 很可能在 5 年内超越人类智能，开启一个金钱无关紧要的富足时代。
    AI will likely surpass human intelligence within 5 years, ushering an age of abundance where money becomes irrelevant.
  • AI 加速不可避免，条条大路通加速，与其抗拒不如加入。
    AI acceleration is inevitable; all roads lead to it, so one must join the club rather than resist.
  • 前沿 AI 公司在发布模型前应相互分享进行安全测试。
    Frontier AI companies should share models with each other for safety testing before releasing them.
  • 尽管与 Sam Altman 有私怨，他仍愿意为了人类利益合作。
    He is willing to work with Sam Altman for the good of humanity despite personal animosity.
  • 他的政治立场仅支持安全边境、安全城市和理性支出，而非极右翼极端主义。
    His political stances merely favor secure borders, safe cities, and sensible spending, not far-right extremism.

反共识 · Contrarian takes

  • 他从称 AI 为最可怕的事物转向拥抱其必然性并主动加速。
    He shifted from calling AI the scariest thing to embracing its inevitability and actively accelerating it.
  • 竞争对手可能比缺乏深度技术理解的政府监管者更好地监督 AI 安全。
    Competitors may police AI safety better than government regulators who lack deep technical understanding.
  • 他坚称欧洲因失控移民正走向内战，许多人认为此观点言过其实。
    He insists Europe is heading for civil war due to unchecked immigration, a view many dismiss as exaggerated.
  • 他声称关停美国国际开发署未造成任何死亡，采访者强烈反驳。
    He claims shutting down USAID caused zero deaths, a statement the interviewer strongly disputed.
  • 人类将像拉布拉多犬一样臣服于超级智能 AI，他似乎接受这种丧失主导权的未来。
    Humans will become like Labradors to superintelligent AI, a disempowering outcome he seems to accept.
