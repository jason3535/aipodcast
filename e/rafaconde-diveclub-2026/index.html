本期速览 · Overview

Rafael Conde 分享了他的起源故事，以及他如何设计引发情感共鸣的软件，从第一台 Mac 到创造令人愉悦的体验。

Rafael Conde shares his origin story and how he designs software that evokes emotion, from his first Mac to creating delightful experiences.

要点 · TL;DR

  • 情感设计比效率更能让人记住软件；细小的人性化细节能创造连接。
    Emotional design beats efficiency for memorable software; small human details create connection.
  • 副项目让设计师摆脱压力，得以注入个人表达并承担创作风险。
    Side projects free designers from pressure, allowing personal expression and creative risk.
  • 相信用户能适应陌生模式；为情感冲击失去部分用户是可以接受的。
    Trust users with unfamiliar patterns; losing some sign-ups for emotional impact is acceptable.

核心观点 · Key points

  • 软件的目标是让人有所感受；情感化的瞬间会留下持久的记忆。
    The goal of software is to make people feel something; emotional moments create lasting memories.
  • 视频是传达设计想法最有效的方式，所以要像做广告一样用视频把想法卖出去。
    Video is the most effective way to communicate design ideas, so design videos should sell the idea like an ad.
  • 好的设计往往从你会做的事情出发，再不断迭代；核心主题是在协作和意外中浮现出来的。
    Great design often starts with what you know, then iterates; the core motif emerges through collaboration and accidents.
  • 副项目是承担风险、注入幽默与人性、制造惊喜和愉悦感的地方。
    Side projects are the place to take risks, inject humor and humanity, and create surprise and delight.
  • 像挥手表情这样微小的人性化细节，不用费很多功夫就能让软件显得有人情味、令人难忘。
    Small human touches like a waving emoji can make software feel personal and memorable without much effort.
  • 做设计时要相信用户能自己搞定，别再过度迁就边缘情况和非快乐路径。
    When designing, trust users to figure things out and stop over-accommodating edge cases and unhappy paths.

反共识 · Contrarian takes

  • 如果体验能建立更深的情感连接，流失 20%–30% 的注册用户也是值得的。
    Losing 20–30 percent of sign-ups can be worth it if the experience creates a deeper emotional connection.
  • 为所有人设计会让令人难忘的体验变得不可能；有时应该瞄准特定受众并接受取舍。
    Designing for everyone makes memorable experiences impossible; you should sometimes target specific audiences and accept trade-offs.
  • 用户并不笨；移除 iOS 小白条说明几十亿用户都能适应，而且没有任何反弹。
    Users are not dumb; removing the iOS home indicator showed billions of people can adapt without any backlash.
  • 不要硬造幽默或意义；而是保持开放，让‘这样会不会很有趣’的小念头来引导你。
    Don't force humor or meaning; instead stay open and let small 'wouldn't it be funny' ideas guide you.
  • 设计师在公司内部应该像 TikTok 创作者一样，用音乐、幽默和生活镜头来推销想法。
    Designers should act like TikTok creators internally, using music, humor, and b-roll to sell their ideas.
  • 大公司会回避有意义的风险；真正有情感的软件往往出自小团队和独立项目。
    Big companies avoid meaningful risks; small teams and indie projects are where truly emotional software gets made.
