本期速览 · Overview

Rafael Conde 分享了他的起源故事，以及他如何设计引发情感共鸣的软件，从第一台 Mac 到创造令人愉悦的体验。

Rafael Conde shares his origin story and how he designs software that evokes emotion, from his first Mac to creating delightful experiences.

要点 · TL;DR

  • 情感设计比效率更能让人记住软件；细小的人性化细节能创造连接。
    Emotional design beats efficiency for memorable software; small human details create connection.
  • 副项目让设计师摆脱压力，得以注入个人表达并承担创作风险。
    Side projects free designers from pressure, allowing personal expression and creative risk.
  • 相信用户能适应陌生模式；为情感冲击失去部分用户是可以接受的。
    Trust users with unfamiliar patterns; losing some sign-ups for emotional impact is acceptable.

核心观点 · Key points

  • 软件的目标是让人有所感受；情感化的瞬间会留下持久的记忆。
    The goal of software is to make people feel something; emotional moments create lasting memories.
  • 视频是传达设计想法最有效的方式，所以要像做广告一样用视频把想法卖出去。
    Video is the most effective way to communicate design ideas, so design videos should sell the idea like an ad.
  • 好的设计往往从你会做的事情出发，再不断迭代；核心主题是在协作和意外中浮现出来的。
    Great design often starts with what you know, then iterates; the core motif emerges through collaboration and accidents.
  • 副项目是承担风险、注入幽默与人性、制造惊喜和愉悦感的地方。
    Side projects are the place to take risks, inject humor and humanity, and create surprise and delight.
  • 像挥手表情这样微小的人性化细节，不用费很多功夫就能让软件显得有人情味、令人难忘。
    Small human touches like a waving emoji can make software feel personal and memorable without much effort.
  • 做设计时要相信用户能自己搞定，别再过度迁就边缘情况和非快乐路径。
    When designing, trust users to figure things out and stop over-accommodating edge cases and unhappy paths.

反共识 · Contrarian takes

  • 如果体验能建立更深的情感连接，流失 20%–30% 的注册用户也是值得的。
    Losing 20–30 percent of sign-ups can be worth it if the experience creates a deeper emotional connection.
  • 为所有人设计会让令人难忘的体验变得不可能；有时应该瞄准特定受众并接受取舍。
    Designing for everyone makes memorable experiences impossible; you should sometimes target specific audiences and accept trade-offs.
  • 用户并不笨；移除 iOS 小白条说明几十亿用户都能适应，而且没有任何反弹。
    Users are not dumb; removing the iOS home indicator showed billions of people can adapt without any backlash.
  • 不要硬造幽默或意义；而是保持开放，让‘这样会不会很有趣’的小念头来引导你。
    Don't force humor or meaning; instead stay open and let small 'wouldn't it be funny' ideas guide you.
  • 设计师在公司内部应该像 TikTok 创作者一样，用音乐、幽默和生活镜头来推销想法。
    Designers should act like TikTok creators internally, using music, humor, and b-roll to sell their ideas.
  • 大公司会回避有意义的风险；真正有情感的软件往往出自小团队和独立项目。
    Big companies avoid meaningful risks; small teams and indie projects are where truly emotional software gets made.

推销创意 Sell the idea

Rafa

当你做一件事时，你有自己的愿景。你知道自己想达成什么。目标就是尽可能清晰地把这个想法传达给别人。如果我有一个功能创意，不要只设计功能并分享原型图。不要只做个原型，再录屏演示你怎么用。要做一支广告，对吧？把这个想法卖出去。

When you are working on something, you have the vision. You know what you're trying to do. The goal is to communicate this idea as cleanly as you can to someone else. If I have this idea for a feature, don't just design the feature and share mock-ups. Don't just make a prototype and do a screen recording of you using the feature. Make an ad, right? Sell this idea.

你其实并不知道自己一无所知，只是边做边摸索。一个很好的入门方法是挑一件你觉得很棒、真正喜欢的东西，然后用 Sketch 或 Figma 照着重新做一遍。光是摆放屏幕或页面上的元素，你就能慢慢学到什么是 padding 和基线，然后恍然大悟：‘哦，这些东西原来是这样对齐的。’仅仅是复刻你喜欢的作品，这个动作本身就能帮你学到东西。这有点像难过的时候强迫自己微笑：哪怕是装的，这个动作也会让你好受一点。设计屏幕时也是这样——你不是从零开始设计，而是在描摹它。但正是这个动作让你学到了一些东西，而且有些东西会留下来。

You don't really know that you don't know anything; you're just figuring it out. One great way to start is to pick something you think is great, something you really like, and recreate it in Sketch or Figma. Just by laying out elements on a screen or a page, you slowly learn about padding and baseline. You realize, 'Oh, all these things are aligned here.' The simple act of recreating things you like helps you learn. It's a bit like forcing yourself to smile when you're sad: even if it's fake, the act itself makes you feel a little happier. That's how it is—you're not designing the screen from scratch, you're tracing over it. But the act of doing it teaches you things, and some of it stays with you.

记录交互笔记 Make notes of interactions

Rafa

而且在这种情况下，与其告诉你‘有想法就去做’，更有用的做法是让自己进入一种状态：当你看到其他产品或其他人做的那些小交互时，把它记下来。‘哦，我刚才用了某个 App，鼠标悬停时出现了一个小小的挥手表情，真好，真可爱。’无论触发的是哪种情绪，都记下一笔。把它存起来。也许你有一个文件夹专门收集截图，或者用 Apple 备忘录随手写下来。

And in this case, rather than just telling you 'if you have an idea, do it,' it's more useful to put yourself in a mindset where, when you see little interactions from other products or other people, you make a note of it. 'Oh yes, I was using someone's app and I hovered, and a little wave emoji appeared. That was nice. That was cute.' Whatever emotion it triggers, take note. Save it somewhere. Maybe you have a folder where you keep screenshots, or an Apple Note where you jot it down.

标志性轶事 The signature anecdote

For instance, I remember reading a blog post years ago. I'm quite old, so I don't remember exactly when. I read a really nice piece, and at the end there was the author's signature—a vector signature. I remember thinking, 'That's so cool.' Why? Nowhere else on the page was there any kind of handwriting. But that signature—so cool. So guess what? For one of my first side projects—an Apple Watch game called Break the Safe—I wrote a case study. And at the end, I converted a signature into a vector and placed it there. Because, yes, a little detail like that is nice.

保持观察 Stay observant

Rafa

所以要时刻留意。当你刷 Twitter 或别的什么时，你会看到很多优秀的作品。其中有些会让你觉得‘哇，这个真酷’。那就记下来、存起来。等下次你做类似的事情时——就像我下次写博客文章时，就会想：‘我记得有人用了签名，感觉很优雅，那我也这么做。’

So be on the lookout. When you're scrolling through Twitter or anything else, you see so much good work. But some pieces will strike you: 'Oh, that's cool.' Make a note, save it. Then the next time you're doing something similar—like the next time I wrote a blog post, I thought, 'I remember someone used a signature, and it felt classy. I'll do that.'
