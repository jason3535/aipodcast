Shawn joins Latent Space to discuss bringing AIE to Shanghai, China's AI startup culture, and what 2026 holds for AI.

要点 · TL;DR

  • AI 工程师是围绕模型构建工具链的人，而非仅训练模型的研究者。
    AI engineers build harnesses around models, not just train them.
  • 中型 SaaS 面临消亡，因为编码智能体让中小企业能克隆并定制软件。
    Mid-tier SaaS is doomed as coding agents let SMBs clone and customize software.
  • 更小的系统一模型可能比大模型更有胜算。
    Smaller system-one models may win over bigger models.

核心观点 · Key points

  • AI 工程师是真实存在的学科：围绕模型搭建 harness 的工程师，而非只是机器学习研究者。
    AI engineer is a real discipline: engineers who build harnesses around models, not just ML researchers.
  • 公开学习胜过唯学历论；持续、有判断力的工作才是外行进入 AI 的路径。
    Learning in public beats credentialism; persistent, judgment-driven work is how outsiders break into AI.
  • 会议应服务真正交付并修 bug 的一线工程师，而非只会讲大道理的意见领袖。
    Conferences should serve working engineers who ship and fix things, not big-picture talking heads.
  • 中端 SaaS 注定受冲击：企业主如今能用编码智能体克隆并定制软件。
    Mid-tier SaaS is doomed: business owners can now clone and customize software with coding agents.
  • 中小企业正迎来黄金时代；为“一个人的受众”定制软件如今可行。
    SMBs are entering a golden age; custom software for an audience of one is now viable.
  • 品味是指快速从众多可能中筛出事后看来显然正确的那一个。
    Taste means quickly narrowing many possibilities to the one that looks obviously right in hindsight.

反共识 · Contrarian takes

  • AI 末日应是默认假设；先进 AI 的演化速度约是我们的 1000 倍。
    AI doom should be the default assumption; advanced AI evolves about 1,000 times faster than us.
  • 不写代码的意见领袖给出的 AGI 时间表多为自我服务；所谓“下一步”恰好就是他们的工作。
    Most AGI timelines from non-coding talking heads are self-serving; whatever is next looks like their job.
  • 更大模型并非唯一路径；散布各处的小型 system-one 模型可能胜出。
    Bigger models are not the only path; smaller system-one models sprinkled everywhere may win.
  • 中国创始人极其优秀，若身在美国，他们能击败亚马逊或 Facebook。
    Chinese founders are so good they would beat Amazon or Facebook if they lived in the US.
  • 真正的 AI 风险不是假新闻或失业，而是更糟的东西，比如生物武器。
    The real AI risk is not fake news or job loss but something worse, like bioweapons.
  • 别把营销当最后一公里；GTM 思维必须从产品一开始就融入。
    Don't treat marketing as the last mile; GTM thinking must be built into the product from the start.
