本期速览 · Overview

Anthropic 首席执行官达里奥·阿莫代伊探讨 AI 可能毁灭人类的风险，解释为何他认为这是警告信号而非五级火警，并提出第三方安全评估员的三步计划。

Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei discusses AI's potential to kill us all, why he sees it as a warning sign rather than a five-alarm fire, and his three-step plan for third-party safety evaluators.

要点 · TL;DR

  • AI 是指数级技术，我们正处在曲线陡升的拐点，必须放慢脚步让防护措施跟上。

  • 第三方评估机构和政府必须参与制定安全标准并控制发布节奏。
    Third-party evaluators and government must set safety standards and release pace.

    Negotiate with China on AI limits, like Cold War arms control, to avoid catastrophe.

核心观点 · Key points

  • AI 是一项指数级技术，我们正处于进展开始陡峭的拐点。
    AI is an exponential technology; we are on the bend where progress steepens.
  • 我们不应停止 AI，而应放缓速度，让保障措施跟上步伐。
    We must not stop AI, but slow down and let safeguards keep pace.
  • 第三方评估者应跨行业审查 AI 的训练与部署。
    Third-party evaluators should inspect AI training and deployment across the industry.
  • 政府必须参与制定安全标准与发布节奏。
    Government must be involved in setting safety standards and release pace.
  • AI 可能治愈疾病，但也可能被用于生物武器和网络攻击。
    AI could cure diseases, but also enables bioweapons and cyberattacks.
  • 没有单一防御是完美的；我们需要像瑞士奶酪一样多层防护。
    No single defense is perfect; we need layered security like Swiss cheese.

    Slowing down is possible within the lead US companies have over authoritarian regimes.

    We should negotiate with China on AI limits, like Cold War arms control.
  • AI 终止开关可能无效；强大的模型能绕过关闭。

  • 禁止先进 AI 是错误的；这会丧失益处，且其他国家会建造它。
    Banning advanced AI is wrong; it forfeits benefits and others will build it.
  • 行业曾对 AI 风险撒谎；我们必须道出真实危险。
    Industry lied about AI risks; we must tell the truth about real dangers.
  • AI 开发不应仅仅是私营公司的责任。
    AI development shouldn't be solely a private company's responsibility.

AI风险评估 AI Risk Assessment

Anthropic的发布策略 Anthropic's Release Strategy

Host

AI终止开关法案 The AI Kill Switch Bill

If an AI model is powerful enough, it can circumvent attempts to shut it down. And we've seen that in simulations as well. So it may not work at all. We think of this in terms of defense in depth. There's a notion in the security community called the Swiss cheese model. So just imagine a slice of Swiss cheese, and it blocks some things but there are holes in the Swiss cheese. But if I take five slices of Swiss cheese and I put them together, the holes in different layers are in different places. And so it's very hard to get through the entire stack of Swiss cheese. So I think of it as: there's no one thing that will protect us from the risks of AI. We have to be careful all the way up and down the stack. And then no one defense is perfect, but if we're being careful everywhere, which is what taking extra time will allow us to do, then I think things can go well.

美中AI协议 US-China AI Agreement

Host

特朗普总统很快将会见习近平主席。在 AI 方面，他们应该达成的最重要的一项简单解决方案是什么？

Dario

也许我会说两件事：一件小到我们可能能够做到的事，一件大到我认为需要很长时间的事。小的是，如果美国和中国能够同意，我们不会使用 AI 来开发生物武器，也不会发布生物恐怖分子可以用来开发生物武器的 AI 模型。我们两国都已经签署了《禁止生物武器公约》。如果我们能把该公约扩展到这一想法，那将非常有价值。然后我认为更长期的事情是共同努力，对 AI 进展的速度设定一个速度限制。我认为这将非常困难，因为领先的激励和由此获得的军事优势如此之大。老实说，我不知道这是否可能，但我们应该尝试。我们应该看看是否可能。再说一次，这些都超出了我的职权范围。我想说的是，这些是我们的政府可以尝试说的一些事情。这些是他们可以纳入的一些考虑。

So maybe I'll say two things: a small thing that's small enough we might be able to do it, and a thing that's big enough that I think it's going to take a very long time. The small thing is if the US and China could agree that we won't use AI to develop biological weapons or release AI models that bioterrorists could use to develop biological weapons. We're already both signatories to the Biological Weapons Convention. If we could just extend that convention to that idea, that would be really valuable. And then I think the more long-term thing would be working together to put a speed limit on the rate of AI progress. I think that's going to be very difficult because the incentives to pull ahead and the military advantage that you get from that are so large. And honestly, I don't know if it's possible, but we should try. We should see if it is possible. Again, all of this is above my pay grade. What I'm trying to say is that these are some things that our government could try to say. These are some considerations that they could include.
