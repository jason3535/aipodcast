  • 星舰完全可复用是让生命延伸到地球之外的关键突破。

    Peer review among AI companies—testing each other's models—is the most practical safety measure.

    Hiring the absolute best people and giving them hard problems with no BS is the management secret.
  • 星链和算力租赁都是主要收入引擎，增长空间巨大。
    Starlink and compute rental are both major revenue engines with huge headroom for growth.

    AI models are dangerous and companies should believe Anthropic and OpenAI when they warn about risks.

    Regulation is a one-way ratchet; self-regulation through peer review is better than waiting for government.

    Physics is the only law; everything else is a recommendation—you can't fool physics.
