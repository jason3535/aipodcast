本期速览 · Overview

Lauren 分享如何攀登智能体信任阶梯，在 SpaceX 实现每月交付数千个 PR。

Lauren shares how she climbed the agent trust ladder to ship thousands of PRs a month at SpaceX.

核心观点 · Key points

  • 验证是最重要的技能；它让智能体能够迭代并攀爬信任阶梯。
    Verification is the single most important skill; it lets agents iterate and climb the trust ladder.
  • 领域专业知识比以往更重要；瓶颈在于向智能体清晰表达意图。
    Domain expertise matters more than ever; the bottleneck is expressing intent clearly to agents.
  • 工程师的新工作是构建环境：技能、约束、lint 规则和类型系统。
    The engineer's new job is building the environment: skills, constraints, lints, and type systems.
  • 将确定性部分提取为脚本/CLI，只把需要判断的工作留给智能体。
    Extract deterministic parts into scripts/CLIs, leaving only judgment-heavy work to agents.
  • 将外循环（Slack、邮件、Linear）连接到内循环，让智能体自行获取上下文。
    Connect the outer loop (Slack, email, Linear) to the inner loop so agents fetch their own context.
  • 抽样审查 PR，并在出现失败模式时修复环境，而非单个智能体。
    Sample PRs and fix the environment, not individual agents, when patterns of failure emerge.

反共识 · Contrarian takes

  • 软件工厂是个误导性术语；米其林厨房更能体现工艺和质量。
    Software factory is a misleading term; the Michelin kitchen better captures craft and quality.
  • 智能体可以在你睡觉时合并自己的代码；在落地后审查，而非之前。
    Agents can merge their own code while you sleep; review after landing, not before.
  • 问题缓冲队列而非立即修复，有助于看清全局。
    A buffer queue of issues, not immediate fixes, helps you see the big picture.
  • 技能应变得更小更紧凑，聚焦工作流而非实现细节。
    Skills should get smaller and more compact, focusing on workflow rather than implementation details.
  • 过去的聊天记录是宝库；挖掘它们，将纠正转化为可复用技能。
    Past chat transcripts are a treasure trove; mine them to turn corrections into reusable skills.
  • 每个人都应携带自己的刀具；信任来自你自己磨利的工具。
    Everyone should carry their own set of knives; trust comes from your own sharpened tools.
