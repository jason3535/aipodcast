设计师的超级力量：AI 时代中的同理心

Designers Have a Superpower: Empathy in the AI Era

罗恩·戈尔丁 Ron Goldin · Dive Club · 2026-06-09 · 约 54 分钟 · 原视频 ↗

打开互动全文版（中英对照 + 朗读 + 问答）→

本期速览 · Overview

一位设计领袖分享沉浸式实地研究与同理心如何驱动产品创新。

A design leader shares how immersive field research and empathy can drive product innovation.

要点 · TL;DR

  • 借助 AI，设计师可以自己构建原型并验证想法，让“不可能”变成“试试看”。
    With AI, designers can prototype and prove ideas themselves, turning 'impossible' into 'try it'.
  • 真正的共情来自于亲身体验用户流程，比如亲自送货来感受痛点。
    True empathy comes from living the user experience, like making deliveries to see pain firsthand.
  • 到 2026 年，设计师需要产品思维和动手能力，连领导层也要亲自上手。
    By 2026, designers need product thinking and hands-on skills; even leaders are expected to build.

核心观点 · Key points

  • 设计师可以用AI快速构建可用原型,让想法变得可触摸,并用原型赢得领导层支持。
    Designers can use AI tools to quickly build working prototypes, making ideas tangible and winning arguments with leadership.
  • 在工作之外完整构建产品(如 Matchmaker)所展现的主人翁意识、产品思维和技术能力,越来越被雇主看重。
    Building products end-to-end outside work, like Matchmaker, demonstrates ownership, product thinking, and technical fluency valued by employers.
  • AI让构建软件更简单,但获取用户依然很难,因此分发和真实用户至关重要。
    AI makes building software easier, but growing an audience is still as hard as ever, making distribution and real customers crucial.
  • 设计管理者应成为“球员兼教练”,和团队一起动手,用原型代替幻灯片和PRD推动决策。
    Design managers should be player-coaches who build alongside their teams, using prototypes instead of slide decks and PRDs to drive decisions.
  • 高效使用AI构建,应先在策略文档中定义目标和用户旅程,再用它生成提示词并分阶段构建。
    Effective AI building starts by defining goals and user journeys in a strategy document, then using that to generate prompts and build in phases.
  • 产品思维、轻量级研究和品牌/动效设计,是设计师的核心差异化能力。
    Product thinking, scrappy research, and brand and motion design are key differentiating skills for designers.

反共识 · Contrarian takes

  • 非工程师背景的设计师可以用 vibe coding 快速证明领导认为“不可能”的功能其实可行。
    Non-engineer designers can use vibe coding to quickly prove a feature leaders said cannot be done actually works.
  • 现在没人能自称AI工具专家,因为下个月它们就更聪明20%,而且变得完全不同。
    No one can claim to be an expert on AI tools today, because next month they will be 20 percent smarter and completely different.
  • AI生成的产品往往同质化,真正脱颖而出需要刻意经营品牌与动效设计,而非仅靠AI输出。
    AI-generated products tend to look homogeneous, so deliberate branding and motion design are needed to stand out.
  • 如今人人都能快速搭出软件,但获取受众的难度从未降低,分发才是真正的瓶颈。
    Anyone can build software quickly now, but growing an audience is as hard as ever; distribution is the real bottleneck.
  • 高级设计领导者也需要亲自上手;曾有初创公司在面试中当场看总监候选人操作 Figma 和自动布局。
    Senior design leaders are expected to be hands-on; one startup even watched a directorial candidate use Figma and auto layout during an interview.
