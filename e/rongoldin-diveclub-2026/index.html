设计师的超级力量：AI 时代中的同理心

Designers Have a Superpower: Empathy in the AI Era

罗恩·戈尔丁 Ron Goldin · Dive Club · 2026-06-09 · 约 54 分钟 · 原视频 ↗

打开互动全文版（中英对照 + 朗读 + 问答）→

本期速览 · Overview

一位设计领袖分享沉浸式实地研究与同理心如何驱动产品创新。

A design leader shares how immersive field research and empathy can drive product innovation.

要点 · TL;DR

  • 借助 AI，设计师可以自己构建原型并验证想法，让“不可能”变成“试试看”。
    With AI, designers can prototype and prove ideas themselves, turning 'impossible' into 'try it'.
  • 真正的共情来自于亲身体验用户流程，比如亲自送货来感受痛点。
    True empathy comes from living the user experience, like making deliveries to see pain firsthand.
  • 到 2026 年，设计师需要产品思维和动手能力，连领导层也要亲自上手。
    By 2026, designers need product thinking and hands-on skills; even leaders are expected to build.

核心观点 · Key points

  • 共情来自于亲身体验自己的产品——哪怕亲自去送货——从而真正理解用户的痛点。
    Empathy comes from experiencing your own product firsthand—even making deliveries—to truly understand user pain.
  • 原型能赢得争论；它让想法变得可感知，比幻灯片或 PRD 更能激发人们的热情。
    Prototypes win arguments; they make ideas tangible and excite people far more than slide decks or PRDs.
  • AI 工具赋予了设计师一种超能力：快速构建可运行的原型，去探索和验证想法。
    AI tools give designers a superpower: building working prototypes quickly to explore and prove ideas.
  • 当今的设计领导者必须是球员兼教练：既保持亲自动手，又引导更小型、更扁平化的团队。
    Design leaders today must be player-coaches, staying hands-on while guiding smaller, flatter teams.
  • 2026 年，设计师需要具备产品思维、务实研究和品牌差异化能力，才能持续创造价值。
    In 2026, designers need product thinking, scrappy research, and brand differentiation to remain valuable.
  • 个人项目或从 0 到 1 的创业经历，能展现主动性、产品思维和韧性，这正是雇主所看重的。
    Personal projects or 0-to-1 ventures demonstrate initiative, product thinking, and resilience that employers value.

反共识 · Contrarian takes

  • 当工程师或副总裁说某事做不到时，设计师现在可以借助 AI 自己把它做出来——所以『不可能』不再那么有说服力。
    When engineers or VPs say something can't be done, designers can now build it themselves with AI—so 'impossible' is less persuasive.
  • 构建软件不再是瓶颈；增长受众仍然和以前一样困难。
    Building software is no longer the bottleneck; growing an audience remains just as hard as ever.
  • AI 生成的设计往往千篇一律；尽早注入品牌灵魂是脱颖而出的关键。
    AI-generated designs tend to look alike; infusing brand soul early is essential to stand out.
  • 即使是资深设计领导者也被要求亲自动手，面试甚至可能考查 Auto Layout 和 Figma 技能。
    Even senior design leaders are expected to be hands-on, with interviews testing auto-layout and Figma skills.
  • 单纯依赖 AI 输出会有局限；把它与 Figma 等工具结合，能产出更有差异化的结果。
    Relying solely on AI output is limiting; combining it with tools like Figma yields more distinctive results.
  • 高保真原型制作成本已大幅下降，领导者甚至可以在会议间隙花几分钟把想法做出来。
    The cost of high-fidelity prototyping has dropped so much that leaders can prototype ideas between meetings in minutes.
