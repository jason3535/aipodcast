本期速览 · Overview

Nate Parrott 分享了他如何从 Snap 的 iOS 工程师转变为 Arc 浏览器的创始设计师，强调原型制作和动手设计的重要性。

Nate Parrott shares how he transitioned from an iOS engineer at Snap to the founding designer of the Arc browser, emphasizing the importance of prototyping and hands-on design.

要点 · TL;DR

  • 原型制作对浏览器设计至关重要，因为网络环境复杂，想法需要通过日常使用来验证。
    Prototyping is crucial for browser design because the web is messy and ideas need daily use to validate.
  • 设计团队应允许设计师留下个人印记，培养创作自由和个人表达。
    Design teams should let individual designers leave fingerprints, fostering creative freedom and personal expression.
  • 在浏览器等实用产品中注入情感和个性，能创造令人难忘和愉悦的体验。
    Injecting feeling and personality into utilitarian products like browsers creates memorable and delightful experiences.

核心观点 · Key points

  • 原型制作对于设计浏览器至关重要，因为网络是混乱的，在构建并日常使用之前，你无法知道一个想法是否有效。
    Prototyping is essential for designing a browser because the web is messy and you can't know if an idea works until it's built and used daily.
  • 设计团队应该允许每个设计师在产品上留下自己的印记，培养一种创意自由和个人表达的文化。
    Design teams should allow individual designers to leave their fingerprints on products, fostering a culture of creative freedom and personal expression.
  • 将情感和个性注入产品，即使是像浏览器这样实用的产品，对于创造令人难忘和愉悦的体验至关重要。
    Injecting feeling and personality into products, even utilitarian ones like browsers, is crucial for creating memorable and delightful experiences.
  • 设计师应该追随好奇心，动手尝试新技术来产生想法，因为有趣的探索可以带来创新功能。
    Designers should follow their curiosity and tinker with new technologies to generate ideas, as playful exploration can lead to innovative features.
  • 平衡直觉和数据是关键；用数据来指导决策，但依靠强烈的愿景和感觉来引导最终设计。
    Balancing intuition and data is key; use data to inform decisions but rely on strong vision and feeling to guide the final design.

反共识 · Contrarian takes

  • 设计团队不应该总是作为一个整体行动；允许个人印记和多样化的声音可以带来更好的作品。
    Design teams should not always behave as one cohesive unit; allowing individual fingerprints and diverse voices can lead to better work.
  • 给AI工具赋予名字和面孔，有助于提醒用户它们可能犯错和存在偏见。
    Anthropomorphizing AI tools with names and faces can be useful for reminding users of their fallibility and biases.
  • 当你对新技术有强烈信念时，先有技术再寻找问题来解决是一种可行的方法。
    Starting with a new technology and then searching for problems to solve can be a valid approach when you have strong conviction.
  • 设计师不应该过度理性化；相反，他们应该开始行动并快速迭代，即使是粗糙的原型。
    Designers should not over-intellectualize; instead, they should start doing things and iterate quickly, even with rough prototypes.
  • 将侧边栏放在浏览器右侧可以减少鼠标移动，但可能不值得消耗新颖性预算。
    Putting the sidebar on the right side of a browser can reduce mouse travel, but it may not be worth the novelty budget.

有很多。有一个我非常喜欢的，还有一个想法——我们有几个想法反复回来尝试，但一直没能做好。其中之一是多人模式。我们一直对这个想法着迷：如果有一个多人浏览器会怎样？然后每个人都会说，酷，如果有多人浏览器会怎样？然后你问，好吧，你希望你的多人浏览器能做什么？他们说，我不知道。我们目前就卡在这里。但另一个想法是拥有一个主页，一个互联网上的家。这个想法在各种产品中反复出现。我记得小时候用 iGoogle，你可以在 Google 上设置自己的主页，带各种小工具。当然，各种操作系统的桌面也反复添加和移除小工具，大概有 16 次了。但我一直对这个想法很感兴趣：一个浏览器能给你一个为你打造的个人主页，包含你想要的内容、媒体，或者只是你想要的感受。所以我们最开始构建的原型之一就是这个主页想法，你可以自定义打开新标签页时看到的内容。你可以只放一张照片，或者一个时钟，或者一个记事本，或者一个巨大的数据仪表盘，包含你的邮件、日历和你最近的所有标签页。那感觉真的很酷，因为它非常个性化，而且也是一个真正全新的可以消磨时间的空间。感觉就像——互联网对很多人来说没有重心，而这个想法似乎在某种程度上把互联网集中了起来，这是你很少见到的。所以我们尝试了，然后意识到想法和氛围感觉很好，但在产品中的定位感觉不对。你知道，你不应该把它放在新标签页上，因为当你打开新标签页时，你想要一个新标签页，你不想看到一堆内容。所以那感觉不对，但背后的想法是对的。我希望有一天我们能带回类似的东西，因为它感觉很特别。

There were a bunch. One that I really enjoyed, and one kind of idea that we have—we have a couple ideas that we've come back to again and again and haven't been able to nail. One of them is multiplayer. We've always been fascinated with this idea of, what if there was a multiplayer browser? And everyone's like, cool, what if there was a multiplayer browser? And then you're like, okay, what would you want your multiplayer browser to do? And they're like, I don't know. That's kind of where we're at with that. But then the other one is having a homepage, a home on the internet. This is an idea that has come around again and again in various products. I think back to iGoogle when I was a kid—you'd be able to set up your own homepage on Google with widgets and various things. And of course, the home screens on various OSes have added and removed widgets like 16 times. But I've always been very interested in this idea of a browser that gives you a personal homepage that is built for you, with the content or the media or just the feeling that you want to feel. So one of the prototypes we built at the very beginning was this homepage idea where you could customize what you saw when you opened a new tab. You could just have a photo, or you could have a clock, or you could have a notepad, or you could have this huge dashboard of data with your emails, your calendar, and all of your most recent tabs. And it felt really cool because it was really personal, and it was also just a genuinely new space that you could spend time in. And it felt like—the internet kind of has no center of gravity for a lot of people, and it felt like it kind of centered the internet in a way that you don't really see. So we tried that, and we realized that the ideas and the vibe felt really good, but the positioning in the product felt wrong. You know, you shouldn't put it on the new tab page because when you open a new tab, you want a new tab, you don't want to be shown a bunch of content. So that felt wrong, but the idea behind it feels right. And I hope at some point we can bring something like that back because it felt very special.

在你离开之前，我想再问几个更个人化的问题。一个我特别喜欢问很多人的问题，而且我对你的观点特别感兴趣：关于设计，你个人相信什么，而你认为很多其他设计师可能不同意？

Before I let you go, I want to ask a few more personal questions. One that I really like to ask a lot of people, and I'm particularly interested in your perspective: what's something that you personally believe about design that you think a lot of other designers might disagree with?

Nate

在这个设计时代，很多人非常崇拜像史蒂夫·乔布斯这样的人。但你可以认为乔布斯创造了你真正喜爱的所有技术，但实际上是团队里的人——团队里有人，而他们作为公司决定除了他之外不提升任何其他人。而我认为 Browser Co 非常幸运能做到的一件事是，我们非常擅长让每个设计师都能发声，有自己的声音，来播客上谈论我们构建的东西，并且对我们构建的东西有各自的风格。我可以看着团队里任何人创作的东西，就能知道是谁做的，甚至不用别人告诉我。我就能看出来。我喜欢这样。在很多领域，这是非常非常正常的，但出于某种原因，设计领域不是这样。所以这不是常态，但我非常珍视这一点。不管怎样，我认为很多公司即将模仿这种工作方式和展示作品的方式，因为我认为你正因这个原因而看到成功。这是新鲜的。

A lot of people in this era of design really lionize people like Steve Jobs. And it's like, you could think that Steve Jobs made all of the technology that you really love, but it was people—there were people on the team, and they as a company made a decision to not elevate any of those people besides him. And one of the things that I think that Browser Co has been really fortunate to do is we've been really good at letting the individual designers speak up and have voices, and come on podcasts and talk about what we've built, and have individual styles for what we've built. And I can look at something that anyone on the team has created and kind of know who made it without even being told. I can just tell. And I like that. And there are a lot of fields where that's very, very normal, and for some reason design it's not. So it's not really the status quo, but it's something that I very much value. For what it's worth, I think a lot of companies are about to copy the style of working and showing the work, because I think you are seeing success for that reason. It's fresh.

I feel like I was just kind of fed slop. I mean, there's a lot of great things on Instagram, but I felt like I wanted to be a little more in control over what I was seeing. 在Arc作为设计师的成长 Growth as a Designer at Arc

Nate

第一个我觉得是意识到“感觉”不只属于 Snapchat 这样的应用，它属于浏览器这样的应用，甚至属于表单这样最无聊的东西。我学到了很多关于简单隐喻的价值。我觉得作为设计师早期，我非常想为每件事想出完美的系统。后来我意识到，其实不，你不应该试图为人们想出完美的系统。你应该构建真正简单的工具，让人们能够理解并凭自己的判断去使用，去做他们需要做的任何事。很容易想要对某件事苦思冥想、把它高度理性化，但通常更好的做法是直接参与进来，开始动手做。
