本期说明 About this episode

Editorial Recap

本文为英伟达 2026 年 11 月 17 日财报电话会的中英双语要点整理。单集的原始出处指向英伟达投资者关系页面，可收听完整录音。

This bilingual editorial recap covers NVIDIA’s November 17, 2026 earnings call. The episode’s source link opens NVIDIA Investor Relations, where the full audio replay is available.

Editorial Recap

临时首席执行官兼首席财务官科莱特·克雷斯介绍了财务表现。董事会主席哈维·琼斯谈及管理层交接，负责超大规模计算和高性能计算的高管伊恩·巴克参加了分析师问答。

Interim CEO and CFO Colette Kress presented the results. Board chair Harvey Jones addressed the leadership transition, and hyperscale and high-performance computing executive Ian Buck joined the analyst Q&A.

季度业绩 Quarterly results

Editorial Recap

截至 10 月 25 日的季度，英伟达营收为 1,158 亿美元，环比增长 20%，同比增长 103%。数据中心营收为 1,076 亿美元，环比增长 21%，同比增长 110%。

For the quarter ended October 25, NVIDIA reported $115.8 billion in revenue, up 20% sequentially and 103% year over year. Data Center brought in $107.6 billion, up 21% from the previous quarter and 110% from a year earlier.

Editorial Recap

按美国通用会计准则（GAAP）计算，毛利率为 74.8%，净利润为 653 亿美元。GAAP 摊薄每股收益为 2.70 美元，非 GAAP 摊薄每股收益为 2.68 美元。

GAAP gross margin was 74.8%, and GAAP net income reached $65.3 billion. Diluted EPS was $2.70 on a GAAP basis and $2.68 on a non-GAAP basis.

Vera Rubin 出货 Vera Rubin rollout

Editorial Recap

英伟达开始交付 Vera Rubin NVL72，并表示首批系统已在云合作伙伴处投入运行。公司还介绍了 Vera CPU 的云端部署，以及与合作伙伴在网络和 AI 基础设施方面的进展。

NVIDIA began shipping Vera Rubin NVL72 and reported that early systems were already operating at cloud partners. The company also highlighted cloud deployments of the Vera CPU and continued work with partners on networking and AI infrastructure.

Editorial Recap

本季度的一个具体里程碑是 Vera Rubin 从产品发布进入首批出货。客户后续部署机架的速度，会影响产品放量转化为营收的节奏。

The concrete milestone for the quarter was the move from announcements to initial shipments. The ability of customers to deploy additional racks will influence how quickly that ramp translates into revenue.

第四季度指引 Fourth-quarter outlook

Editorial Recap

管理层给出的第四季度营收指引为 1,300 亿美元，上下浮动 3%。该季度比通常多一周，指引未计入来自中国的数据中心计算收入。

Management guided to $130 billion in fourth-quarter revenue, plus or minus 3%. The forecast includes an extra fiscal week and excludes Data Center compute revenue from China.

Editorial Recap

英伟达预计第四季度 GAAP 和非 GAAP 毛利率均为 71% 至 72%，低于第三季度的实际水平。公司预计第四季度 GAAP 营业费用约为 110 亿美元。

NVIDIA expects both GAAP and non-GAAP gross margins of 71% to 72% for Q4, below the reported third-quarter levels. The company projected GAAP operating expenses of about $11.0 billion in Q4.

分析师问答 What analysts asked

Editorial Recap

分析师询问管理层为何有信心上调 2028 财年的增长预期。克雷斯将调整归因于需求增强和供应改善。

Analysts asked what gave management confidence to lift its fiscal 2028 growth expectation. Kress cited both stronger demand and improved supply availability as the basis for management’s revised outlook.

Editorial Recap

问答还涉及管理层交接期间的人才留任，以及英伟达的竞争优势能否延续。克雷斯和琼斯谈到人才与团队对公司执行力的重要性。

The Q&A also covered employee retention and the durability of NVIDIA’s competitive position during the leadership transition. Kress and Jones discussed the importance of the people and teams behind the company’s execution.
