本期速览 · Overview

Isomorphic Labs 总裁 Max Jaderberg 探讨如何利用 AI 在临床试验前将生物学理解搬上计算机，从而变革药物发现。

Isomorphic Labs President Max Jaderberg discusses how AI can transform drug discovery by bringing biological understanding onto computers before clinical trials.

要点 · TL;DR

  • AI 能将药物发现从经验试错转变为预测性的计算机驱动设计。
    AI can turn drug discovery from empirical trial-and-error into predictive computer-driven design.
  • 在患者之前建模人体毒性是最大机会，而不仅仅是靶点结合。
    Modeling human toxicity before patients is the biggest opportunity, not just target binding.
  • 通用的疾病无关模型能跨药物积累学习，而定制项目做不到。
    General disease-agnostic models enable compounding learnings across medicines, unlike bespoke projects.

核心观点 · Key points

  • 药物发现仍以经验为主；AI 能将其转变为预测性、计算机驱动的设计。
    Drug discovery remains largely empirical; AI can shift it to predictive, computer-driven design.
  • AlphaFold 证明深度学习在生物学中具有泛化能力，为成立专门攻克所有疾病的公司提供了依据。
    AlphaFold proved deep learning generalizes in biology, justifying a dedicated company for all disease.
  • 最大的机会是在患者之前模拟人体临床毒性，而不仅仅是靶点结合。
    The biggest opportunity is modeling human clinical toxicity before patients, not just target binding.
  • 以实验精度将药物设计搬到计算机上，可节省数年时间并提高成功概率。
    Bringing drug design onto computers with experimental accuracy saves years and boosts success probability.
  • 通用的、疾病无关的模型能在不同药物间实现累积学习，而非定制化项目。
    General, disease-agnostic models enable compounding learnings across medicines, unlike bespoke projects.
  • 患者安全仍是核心，但风险承受度应因适应症和患者需求而异。
    Patient safety remains central, but risk appetite should vary by indication and patient need.

反共识 · Contrarian takes

  • 大鼠模型对人类毒性的预测率仅约 30%；我们需要与人类相关的预测终点。
    Rat models are only ~30% predictive of human toxicity; we need human-relevant predictive endpoints.
  • 我们应致力于脱离现实世界，而不仅仅是用机器人加速现实世界的迭代。
    We should aim to get out of the real world, not just speed up real-world iteration with robots.
  • 公共和药企数据是带有偏差的副产品；生成专有数据能带来更好的泛化。
    Public and pharma data are biased byproducts; generating proprietary data yields better generalization.
  • 毒性限制了药物鸡尾酒疗法；更精准设计的分子可能实现复杂的多靶点组合。
    Toxicity limits drug cocktails; better-engineered molecules could enable complex multi-target combinations.
  • AI 设计的药物应展现出统计上不同的成功率和安全性，从而逐渐改变监管。
    AI-designed drugs should show statistically different success and safety, changing regulation over time.
  • 五年内，预测性疾病生物学和精准分子设计应发生巨大跃变。
    Within five years, predictive disease biology and precise molecule design should step-change massively.
