核心观点 · Key points

  • AI已经从聊天转向执行，进入了智能体时代，AI可以自主完成任务。
    AI has shifted from chatting to doing, entering the agentic era where AI can execute tasks autonomously.
  • 大多数AI用户处于第一或第二级；进阶需要构建自定义GPT、工作流和智能体。
    Most AI users are at level one or two; advancing requires building custom GPTs, workflows, and agents.
  • 将AI拟人化为团队成员有助于训练和采用；像对待实习生一样对待它，而不是一次性的工具。
    Personifying AI as a team member improves training and adoption; treat it like an intern, not a disposable tool.
  • 建立个人故事和语音样本的上下文库，使AI能够生成真实、非垃圾的内容。
    Building a context vault of personal stories and voice samples enables AI to produce authentic, non-slop content.
  • 工作的未来是转变，而非大规模淘汰；AI提高了期望，并创造了像机器人经理这样的新角色。
    The future of work is a shift, not mass elimination; AI raises expectations and creates new roles like robot managers.
  • 要让团队参与，展示AI如何使个人受益；将其定位为赋能，而非替代。
    To get teams engaged, show how AI benefits them personally; frame it as empowerment, not replacement.

  • ChatGPT相比Claude有所退步，OpenAI正将其重新定位为超级应用，因为失去了优势。
    ChatGPT has deteriorated compared to Claude, and OpenAI is repositioning it as a super app due to lost ground.
  • 美国大学生的反AI抗议与中国拥抱AI形成鲜明对比，中国孩子通过AI应用赚钱。
    American college students' anti-AI protests contrast sharply with China's embrace, where kids monetize AI apps.
  • AI CEO们的悲观预测是错误的；工作会转变，不会消失，就像电梯操作员一样。
    Doom-and-gloom predictions from AI CEOs are wrong; jobs will shift, not disappear, as seen with elevator operators.

    AI slop can be avoided by training AI on your authentic voice and stories, making output indistinguishable from human.
  • 将AI视为人，给它名字和性别，比仅仅将其视为工具能获得更好的结果。
    Treating AI as a person, giving it names and genders, leads to better results than viewing it as a mere tool.
