本期速览 · Overview

一位远见者探讨如何将物理世界数字化，从交通到制造，推动下一次工业革命。

A visionary discusses how digitizing the physical world, from transportation to manufacturing, is driving the next industrial revolution.

要点 · TL;DR

  • 工业 AI 自动化食品、采矿和运输，是下一次工业革命。
    Industrial AI automating food, mining, and transport is the next industrial revolution.
  • 创业者必须为最佳想法而战，即使这意味着得罪人。
    Entrepreneurs must fight for the best idea, even if it means stepping on toes.
  • 对变革的抵制，而非竞争对手，是进步路上的终极 BOSS。
    Resistance to change, not competitors, is the final boss for progress.

核心观点 · Key points

  • 工业AI，自动化食品、采矿和运输等整个行业，是下一次工业革命。
    Industrial AI, automating entire sectors like food, mining, and transport, is the next industrial revolution.
  • 要成功，你必须为最好的想法而战，即使这意味着踩到别人的脚趾。
    To succeed, you must fight for the best idea, even if it means stepping on toes.
  • 企业家必须坚强且有韧性，将变革阻力视为最终Boss。
    Entrepreneurs must be hardcore and resilient, facing resistance to change as the final boss.
  • 当事情变得容易时，是时候更加努力；自满会导致失败。
    When things get easy, it's time to push harder; complacency leads to failure.
  • 媒体格局已变，创始人可以直接沟通，无需传统媒体把关。
    The media landscape has changed, allowing founders to communicate directly without traditional media gatekeepers.

反共识 · Contrarian takes

  • Uber的成功是尽管其激进文化，而非因为；过于接近底线是危险的。
    Uber's success was despite its aggressive culture, not because of it; being too close to the line is dangerous.
  • 复仇作为燃料是肮脏的；爱上新使命更干净、更有效。
    Revenge as fuel is dirty; falling in love with the new mission is cleaner and more effective.
  • 愤怒使你成为优秀企业家，但习惯逆境会削弱这种锋芒。
    Being pissed off makes you a good entrepreneur, but getting used to adversity can dull that edge.
  • 进步的最后Boss不是竞争对手，而是来自社会和媒体的变革阻力。
    The final boss for progress is not competitors but resistance to change, often from society and media.
  • 埃隆·马斯克收购推特是言论自由的转折点，使创始人能够畅所欲言。
    Elon Musk buying Twitter was a turning point for free speech, enabling founders to speak their minds.
