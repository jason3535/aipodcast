本期速览 · Overview

Handshake AI 的 Garrett 深入解析 AI 训练数据如何从廉价标注转向专家构建的强化学习环境，以及为何定义“好”是评估中最难的问题。

Garrett Lord of Handshake AI breaks down how AI training data has shifted from cheap labeling to expert-authored RL environments and why defining "good" is the hardest problem in evals.

要点 · TL;DR

  • 数据标注已转向由专家编写环境和评分标准，以训练前沿模型。
    Data labeling has shifted to expert-authored environments and rubrics for frontier model training.
  • 前沿模型在生命科学和金融等领域的基准测试中得分低于 50%。
    Frontier models score below 50% on domain benchmarks like life sciences and finance.
  • 模型校准危险地差，错误时仍表达高置信度，阻碍高风险场景部署。
    Models are dangerously uncalibrated, expressing high confidence when wrong, hindering high-stakes deployment.

核心观点 · Key points

  • 数据标注已从低技能标注演变为专家编写复杂环境和评分标准。
    Data labeling has evolved from low-skill annotation to expert authoring of complex environments and rubrics.
  • 前沿模型在生命科学和金融等特定领域基准上表现不佳，通常低于50%。
    Frontier models perform poorly on domain-specific benchmarks like life sciences and finance, often below 50%.
  • 高质量数据需要专家与智能体协作，由人类引导合成生成并审核真实性。
    High-quality data requires expert-agent collaboration, with humans steering synthetic generation and auditing for realism.
  • 评分标准必须确定且由专家构建，以捕捉半可验证领域中的细微质量差异。
    Rubrics must be deterministic and expert-built to capture nuanced quality in semi-verifiable domains.
  • 模型通常校准不佳，即使错误也表达高置信度，这对高风险应用很危险。
    Models are often uncalibrated, expressing high confidence even when wrong, which is dangerous for high-stakes use.
  • 可解释性和人机交互对于金融等高风险行业的部署至关重要。
    Interpretability and human-agent interaction are critical for deployment in high-consequence industries like finance.

反共识 · Contrarian takes

  • 如果模型95%正确，那就是100%错误，因为修复错误与从头构建耗时相当。
    If a model is 95% right, it's 100% wrong because fixing errors takes as much time as building from scratch.
  • 合成数据通常真实或简单，但几乎从不既真实又困难，因为缺乏边缘案例。
    Synthetic data is often realistic or easy, but almost never realistic and hard due to edge cases.
  • 没有以人类工作流程为基础的递归自我改进将缺乏现实科学所需的直觉。
    Recursive self-improvement without grounding in human workflows will lack the intuition needed for real-world science.
  • 大多数银行家宁愿从头开始，也不愿使用79%正确的模型输出。
    Most bankers would rather start from scratch than use a model output that is 79% correct.
  • 全职专家招聘正在增加而非减少，因为数据质量直接影响模型品味。
    Hiring full-time experts is increasing, not decreasing, as data quality directly influences model taste.
  • AI在能够解读Western blot或组织切片等基本图像之前，不会治愈癌症。
    AI is not going to cure cancer before it can interpret basic images like Western blots or histology slides.
