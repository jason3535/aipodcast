本期速览 · Overview

SF Compute 的 Evan Conrad 讲述如何通过实物交割与专用订单簿，把算力变成可交易的市场。

Evan Conrad of SF Compute explains how physical settlement and a purpose-built order book turn compute into a tradable market.

要点 · TL;DR

  • 算力市场必须先实现实物交割，金融化应随后进行。
    Compute markets need physical settlement first; financialization must follow.
  • 算力并非同质化，芯片代际、存储和中断条款都重要。
    Compute is not fungible; chip generation, storage, and interruption terms matter.
  • SF Compute 保持独立，以避免操纵远期市场。
    SF Compute stays independent to avoid manipulating the forwards market.

核心观点 · Key points

  • 实物交割是可信算力市场的基础；金融化必须在其后，而非之前。
    Physical settlement is essential for a credible compute market; financialization must follow, not precede it.
  • 算力并非同质化；芯片代际、存储、中断条款等众多维度都重要。
    Compute is not fungible; many dimensions like chip generation, storage, and interruption terms matter.
  • 算力远期曲线呈驼峰状：近期未售出容量价格下跌，随后上升，再下降。
    The compute forward curve is humped: near-term unsold capacity drops in price, then rises, then declines.
  • 当前B300推理收入（约$40/小时）远超租赁成本（约$5-6/小时）；两者必有一变。
    Current B300 inference revenue (~$40/hr) far exceeds rental cost (~$5-6/hr); one must adjust.
  • SF Compute必须保持独立（不拥有硬件），以避免操纵远期市场。
    SF Compute must stay independent (not own hardware) to avoid manipulating the forwards market.
  • 算力合约标准化尚远；先统一运营，再标准化。
    Standardizing compute contracts is far off; first make operations uniform, then standardize.

反共识 · Contrarian takes

  • 英伟达无法建立算力交易所，因为它不够独立，难以用于交易。
    Nvidia can't build a compute exchange because it wouldn't be independent enough for trading.
  • 超大规模云厂商无法运营此市场；他们必须扼杀核心业务才能做到。
    Hyperscalers and cloud providers can't run this market; they'd have to kill their core business.
  • 在实物交割前推出现金结算期货会制造可操纵市场和高基差风险。
    Cash-settled futures before physical settlement create manipulable markets with high basis risk.
  • 尽管现货价格低，但由于锁定需求，未来算力价格可能更高。
    Compute prices may be higher in the future even if spot is low, due to lock-in demand.
  • 早期GPU批次（H100、GB200）存在固件缺陷；成熟买家愿为后期批次支付溢价。
    Early GPU cohorts (H100, GB200) had firmware bugs; sophisticated buyers pay more for later batches.
