AI 时代的设计系统：从令牌到核心

Design Systems in the AI Era: From Tokens to Centerpiece

本期速览 · Overview

设计系统已成为 AI 驱动工程的核心，其作用从质量控制转变为指导代码生成。

Design systems have become the centerpiece of AI-driven engineering, shifting their purpose from quality control to guiding code generation.

要点 · TL;DR

  • 设计系统在 AI 时代并未消亡，反而成为核心，为 AI 代码生成提供基础。
    Design systems are thriving in AI era, not dead; they're the centerpiece enabling AI code generation.
  • AI 工具模糊了角色边界，让设计师、工程师和产品经理从任意起点协作迭代。
    AI tools are blurring roles, letting designers, engineers, and PMs iterate from any starting point.
  • 质量门槛成为共同责任，设计系统则是最终的衡量标准。
    The quality bar is now a shared responsibility, with design systems as the ultimate standard.

核心观点 · Key points

  • 设计系统已成为工程工作的核心；AI代码生成依赖它们来产出高质量成果。
    Design systems have become the centerpiece of engineering work; AI code generation depends on them to produce quality output.
  • 文档和指南不再仅仅面向人；它们是可预测AI工作流的基础。
    Documentation and guidelines are no longer just for humans; they are the foundation for predictable AI workflows.
  • 跨职能协作正在扩展，设计师、工程师和产品经理在不同阶段共同贡献，而非各自为政。
    Cross-functional collaboration is expanding as designers, engineers, and PMs contribute across phases instead of working in silos.
  • 质量标杆是每个人的责任，设计系统就是那个标杆，而不是某个孤立团队的职责。
    Quality bar is everyone's responsibility, and a design system is that bar, not a siloed team's job.
  • AI工具使非技术设计师能更快地从画布迭代到浏览器，加深他们对产品决策的参与。
    AI tools enable non-technical designers to iterate faster from canvas to browser, deepening their involvement in product decisions.
  • 新的工作方式是“从任何地方开始”——代码、画布或文档——然后在工具间跳跃来完善想法。
    The new workflow is start from anywhere—code, canvas, or document—then bounce between tools to refine ideas.

反共识 · Contrarian takes

  • “设计系统已死”的说法是错误的；在AI驱动的开发中，它们比以往更重要。
    The claim that design systems are dead is wrong; they matter more than ever for AI-driven development.
  • 与流行说法相反，原型不是新的PRD；在动像素之前，写文档仍是获得认可的最佳方式。
    Contrary to popular jargon, prototypes are not the new PRDs; writing documents remains the best way to gain buy-in before pixels.
  • 过度使用token可能适得其反；在实验中原始值可以接受，系统需要适应变化。
    Over-tokenization can be counterproductive; raw values may be acceptable for experiments, and systems should adapt.
  • 在这个快速变化的环境中，长期职业规划是徒劳的；抓住机会并享受过程更重要。
    Long-term career planning is fruitless in this fast-changing landscape; taking shots and enjoying the process matters more.
  • 大多数软件很平庸，许多用户并不在乎像素；设计师应优先考虑业务成果而非视觉完美。
    Most software is mediocre, and many users don't care about pixels; designers should prioritize business outcomes over visual perfection.
  • 角色模糊不只是为了削减成本；前端开发最终会被视为UX设计的一部分。
    Role blurring isn't just cost-cutting; front-end development will eventually be recognized as part of UX design.
