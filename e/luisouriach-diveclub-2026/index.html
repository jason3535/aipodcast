Design Systems in the AI Era: From Tokens to Centerpiece

本期速览 · Overview

设计系统已成为 AI 驱动工程的核心，其作用从质量控制转变为指导代码生成。

Design systems have become the centerpiece of AI-driven engineering, shifting their purpose from quality control to guiding code generation.

要点 · TL;DR

  • 设计系统在 AI 时代并未消亡，反而成为核心，为 AI 代码生成提供基础。
    Design systems are thriving in AI era, not dead; they're the centerpiece enabling AI code generation.
  • AI 工具模糊了角色边界，让设计师、工程师和产品经理从任意起点协作迭代。
    AI tools are blurring roles, letting designers, engineers, and PMs iterate from any starting point.
  • 质量门槛成为共同责任，设计系统则是最终的衡量标准。
    The quality bar is now a shared responsibility, with design systems as the ultimate standard.

核心观点 · Key points

  • 设计系统如今是工程工作的核心，因为AI生成代码需要系统来引导。
    Design systems are now the centerpiece of engineering work, because AI code generation needs systems to guide it.
  • 文档和指南仍然至关重要，如今它们塑造AI的输出，以获得一致和可预测的结果。
    Documentation and guidelines remain vital, now framing AI output for consistent and predictable results.
  • 质量是每个人的职责；设计系统是组织的质量标准，而非孤立团队的专利。
    Quality is everyone's job; a design system is an organizational quality bar, not a siloed team.
  • 设计师应拥抱不断扩展的角色和跨职能协作，而非固守旧的孤岛。
    Designers should embrace widening roles and cross-functional collaboration, not protect old silos.
  • AI让非技术创造者能快速从想法走向可用的产品；想法本身胜出。
    AI empowers non-technical makers to move from idea to working product quickly; the idea wins.

反共识 · Contrarian takes

  • 设计系统没有死；随着AI使其成为速度的关键驱动力，钟摆已回摆。
    Design systems are not dead; the pendulum swung back as AI makes them key drivers of speed.
  • 若没有严格的品牌指引，仅靠AI生成代码会让所有产出千篇一律；品牌感受至关重要。
    Relying on AI code generation without strict brand guidance makes all output look the same; brand feeling matters.
  • 在即时且廉价的AI生成世界里，原始值可以被接受；过度令牌化反而会阻碍实验。
    Raw values can be acceptable in a world of instant, cheap AI generation; over-tokenization may hinder experimentation.
  • 高保真原型旨在快速验证；将它们扔掉往往才是其真正的目的。
    High-fidelity prototypes exist for fast validation; throwing them away is often their true purpose.
  • AI工具的可及性拉大了初创公司和传统企业之间的差距，制造了新的数字鸿沟。
    AI tool access is widening the gap between startups and legacy enterprises, creating a new digital divide.
  • 大多数软件平庸，用户并不在意像素，因此设计师应聚焦于营收影响。
    Most software is mediocre and users don't care about pixels, so designers should focus on revenue impact.
