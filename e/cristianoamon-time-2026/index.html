本期速览 · Overview

高通 CEO 克里斯蒂亚诺·安蒙阐释为何 AI 将超乎预期、地缘政治如何影响半导体行业，以及科技领袖为何必须具备角斗士心态。

Qualcomm CEO Cristiano Amon explains why AI will be bigger than expected, how geopolitics shapes semiconductors, and why tech leaders must embrace a gladiator mentality.

要点 · TL;DR

  • 长期来看 AI 被低估，它将像互联网一样重塑计算。
    AI is underhyped long-term and will transform computing like the internet did.
  • 高通押注分布式边缘 AI，覆盖手机、PC、汽车和机器人。
    Qualcomm bets on distributed edge AI across phones, PCs, cars, and robots.
  • 在高通这类工程驱动的公司，工程师当 CEO 可能比 MBA 更出色。
    Engineers can outperform MBAs as CEOs in engineering-driven companies like Qualcomm.

核心观点 · Key points

  • AI 将比人们想象的更宏大，像互联网一样改变计算、设备和各个行业。
    AI will be bigger than people think, transforming computing, devices, and industries like the internet did.
  • 科技行业的成功是暂时的；公司必须持续创新并赢得下一个周期。
    Success in tech is temporary; companies must keep innovating and winning the next cycle.
  • AI 将分布式运行在边缘，覆盖手机、PC、汽车和机器人。
    AI will be distributed, running on the edge across phones, PCs, cars, and robots.
  • 智能眼镜将成为重要的个人 AI 设备，因为它们靠近眼睛、耳朵和嘴巴。
    Smart glasses will be a major personal AI device because they are close to eyes, ears, and mouth.
  • 高通的工程文化以及在残酷市场中竞争的能力使其能够多元化发展。
    Qualcomm's engineering culture and ability to compete in brutal markets enable its diversification.
  • 与中国的贸易关系提供稳定性，对半导体公司很有价值。
    Trade relationships with China provide stability and are valuable for semiconductor companies.

反共识 · Contrarian takes

  • 从长远看，AI 可能被低估了，而不是像许多人认为的那样被高估。
    AI is probably underhyped in the long run, not overhyped as many believe.
  • AI 的赢家尚未确定；许多模型和公司都会成功，不同于早期互联网的假设。
    The AI winners are not determined yet; many models and companies will succeed, unlike early internet assumptions.
  • 高通不需要给自己贴上 AI 公司的标签；AI 是其计算业务的内在部分。
    Qualcomm doesn't need to label itself an AI company; AI is inherent in its computing business.
  • 工程师可以比 MBA 成为更好的 CEO，至少在像高通这样工程驱动的公司是如此。
    Engineers can make better CEOs than MBAs, at least at engineering-driven companies like Qualcomm.
  • 向中国公司提供美国芯片对美国经济和全球稳定都有好处。
    Providing American chips to Chinese companies is good for the U.S. economy and global stability.
  • 智能眼镜的隐私问题将通过用户许可和监管来管理，而不是通过回避技术来解决。
    Privacy concerns around smart glasses will be managed by user permission and regulation, not by avoiding the technology.
