本期速览 · Overview

SpaceX 公布创纪录营收，并阐述星舰巨大轨道运力、星链 V3 及 AI 计算扩展计划。

SpaceX reports record revenue and outlines plans for Starship's massive orbital capacity, Starlink V3, and AI compute expansion.

要点 · TL;DR

  • 星舰将把入轨载荷从每年 2500 吨提升到超过 100 万吨，这是深刻的变革。
    Starship will boost payload to orbit from 2,500 to over a million tons yearly, a profound shift.
  • 星链 V3 卫星能力提升 10 倍，数量增加 10 倍，带宽提升 100 倍。
    Starlink V3 satellites are 10x more capable and numerous, enabling 100x bandwidth increase.
  • AI 算力需求每年增长 200%，超过供给，因此价格将上涨而非下跌。
    AI compute demand grows 200% annually, outpacing supply, so prices will rise, not fall.

核心观点 · Key points

  • Starship 将把年入轨载荷从 2500 吨提升到超过 100 万吨，这是一个深刻的转变。
    Starship will increase payload to orbit from 2,500 tons to over a million tons per year, a profound shift.
  • Starlink V3 卫星能力提升 10 倍，数量增加 10 倍，带宽将增加 100 倍。
    Starlink V3 satellites are 10x more capable and 10x more numerous, leading to a 100x bandwidth increase.
  • AI 算力需求每年增长 200%，超过供给，因此价格将上涨而非下跌。
    AI compute demand is growing 200% annually, outpacing supply, so prices will rise, not fall.
  • SpaceX 预计到 2026 年 12 月达到 1000 亿美元年化收入，到 2030 年达到 1 万亿美元营收。
    SpaceX expects to reach $100 billion ARR by December 2026 and $1 trillion revenue by 2030.
  • Starship 的热防护问题已被视为解决，从而实现快速复用。
    The heat shield problem for Starship is considered solved, enabling rapid reusability.

反共识 · Contrarian takes

  • SpaceX 鼓励竞争对手模仿他们，甚至以公平价格发射竞争对手的卫星。
    SpaceX encourages competitors to copy them, even launching their satellites at fair prices.
  • Starlink 可能在不到 10 年内提供全球大部分互联网服务。
    Starlink could deliver the majority of the world's internet in less than 10 years.
  • 人类平均每秒输出不到 1 比特，而 AI 需要数十亿比特，推动带宽需求。
    Average human outputs less than one bit per second, while AI needs billions, driving bandwidth demand.
  • SpaceX 计划在轨道上部署 AI 数据中心，使用优化的 NVL72 计算机。
    SpaceX plans to deploy AI data centers in orbit, using optimized NVL72 computers.
  • Starlink 移动服务可能利用 Starlink 天线上的小型基站成为美国主要运营商，而非传统基站。
    Starlink mobile could become a major US carrier using small cells on Starlink dishes, not traditional towers.
