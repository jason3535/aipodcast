本期速览 · Overview

StarCloud 的 Philip Johnston 解释了为什么尽早预订发射对航天公司来说是一种推动力，以及他的公司如何从太空太阳能转向太空数据中心。

Philip Johnston of StarCloud explains why booking a launch early is a forcing function for space companies, and how his company pivoted from space-based solar to data centers in space.

要点 · TL;DR

  • 在建造产品之前预订发射，以迫使专注和加速。
    Book a launch before building the product to force focus and speed.
  • 由于发射成本下降和地面限制，太空数据中心可行。
    Space data centers are viable due to falling launch costs and terrestrial constraints.
  • 创始团队的技术人才是成功的最关键因素。
    Technical talent on the founding team is the most critical success factor.

核心观点 · Key points

  • 每家太空公司应该做的第一件事是预订第一个可用的发射，即使在建造产品之前。
    The first thing every space company should do is book the first available launch, even before building the product.
  • 由于发射成本下降和地面限制增加，太空数据中心是可行的。
    Data centers in space are viable due to falling launch costs and increasing terrestrial constraints.
  • 创始团队的技术人才是成功最关键的因素。
    Technical talent on the founding team is the most critical factor for success.
  • 天基太阳能由于传输中95%的能量损失而效率低下。
    Space-based solar power is inefficient due to 95% energy loss in transmission.
  • 使用现成组件并测试其辐射耐受性是降低成本的关键。
    Using off-the-shelf components and testing them for radiation is key to reducing costs.

反共识 · Contrarian takes

  • 太空数据中心在每公斤500美元的发射成本下可以盈利。
    Data centers in space can be profitable at a launch cost of $500 per kilogram.
  • 在太空中运行像H100这样的数据中心级GPU是可能的。
    It is possible to run data center-grade GPUs like the H100 in space.
  • 数据中心不消耗大量水；一个大型数据中心用水量大约相当于一家麦当劳。
    Data centers do not consume significant water; a large one uses about as much as a McDonald's.
  • 从监管角度看，在太空建设数据中心可能比在地球上更容易。
    Building data centers in space may be easier from a regulatory perspective than on Earth.
  • 增加数据中心可以增加电网容量，并随时间降低价格。
    Adding data centers can increase grid capacity and lower prices over time.
