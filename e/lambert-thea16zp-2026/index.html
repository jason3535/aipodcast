Nathan Lambert breaks down the frontier of AI through compute, data, and talent, and explores whether open-weight models can keep pace with closed labs like OpenAI and Anthropic.

  • 开源模型能快速跟随前沿实验室；真正的差距在算力，而非人才。

    RSI won't cause fast takeoff due to human bottlenecks and exponential costs.
