Farza 分享打造真正能转化的发布视频的方法论，用算法平台上的持续小样演示取代大预算的首发式。

Farza shares his playbook for making launch videos that actually convert, replacing big-budget premieres with constant mini demos on algorithmic platforms.

要点 · TL;DR

  • 发布不是一次性事件，而是在算法平台上持续交付演示。

  • 从演示倒推：先写脚本、钩子和故事，再动手开发。

核心观点 · Key points

  • 发布不是一次性事件，而是在算法平台上持续发布演示。
    Launching is not a one-time event but continuous demo shipping on algorithmic platforms.
  • 创始人应从演示反向工作：在构建前先构思脚本、钩子和故事。
    Founders should work backwards from the demo: script, hook, and story before building.
  • 钩子和前10秒对于阻止滚动和传达价值至关重要。
    The hook and first 10 seconds are critical to stop scrollers and convey value.
  • 展示你的过程，而不仅仅是精美的最终结果；真实性会引起共鸣。
    Show your process, not just the polished end result; authenticity resonates.
  • 每个渠道的转化率不同；跟踪并优化实际注册。
    Every channel has different conversion rates; track and optimize for actual signups.
  • 音频质量是最重要的制作元素；投资一个好的麦克风。
    Audio quality is the most important production element; invest in a good mic.

反共识 · Contrarian takes

  • 不要花费时间和金钱制作大型精美的发布视频；简单的iPhone视频往往表现更好。
    Don't spend time and money on a big fancy launch video; a simple iPhone video often performs better.
  • 频繁发布并接受尴尬是学习和改进的必要条件。
    Posting frequently and accepting cringe is necessary to learn and improve.
  • 不要痴迷于浏览量；关注留存和实际用户参与度。
    Don't obsess over views; focus on retention and actual user engagement.
  • Twitter尽管浏览量高但转化率低；YouTube和Instagram转化更好。
    Twitter has low conversion despite high views; YouTube and Instagram convert better.
  • 你不需要昂贵的制作；一部手机和笔记本电脑就能让你强大。
    You don't need expensive production; a phone and laptop make you powerful.
  • 有针对性的小浏览量（例如4,000）对于潜在客户生成可能比病毒式浏览量更有价值。
    Targeted small views (e.g., 4,000) can be more valuable than viral views for lead generation.
