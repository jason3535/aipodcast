本期速览 · Overview

Kyle Xantos 分享如何利用 HTML 工件和链式 AI 技能自动化设计审计，以可视化提案供审批。

Kyle Xantos shares how he uses HTML artifacts and chained AI skills to automate design audits, presenting visual proposals for approval.

要点 · TL;DR

  • HTML 工件使设计师能够快速可视化和迭代，超越传统设计工具。
    HTML artifacts enable designers to rapidly visualize and iterate, surpassing traditional design tools.
  • 代理生成多个变体并进行视觉审查，显著提升设计效率。
    Agents generating multiple variations and visual review boost design efficiency significantly.
  • 语音听写和视频反馈对于向 AI 传达设计意图非常有效。
    Voice dictation and video feedback are highly effective for communicating design intent to AI.

核心观点 · Key points

  • HTML 工件是设计师快速可视化和迭代想法的强大工具。
    HTML artifacts are a powerful tool for designers to visualize and iterate on ideas quickly.
  • 使用智能体生成多个变体，然后视觉上审查它们，比传统设计工具更高效。
    Using agents to generate multiple variations and then reviewing them visually is more efficient than traditional design tools.
  • 语音听写和视频反馈（例如通过 Supercut）对于向 AI 传达设计意图非常有效。
    Voice dictation and video feedback (e.g., via Supercut) are highly effective for communicating design intent to AI.
  • 设计师应该拥有其设计的前端实现，以确保最终产品符合他们的愿景。
    Designers should own the front-end implementation of their designs to ensure the final product matches their vision.
  • 设计工程师的角色正变得越来越被接受，融合了设计和工程技能。
    The role of design engineer is becoming more accepted, blending design and engineering skills.

反共识 · Contrarian takes

  • 使用 Opus 5 和 Sonnet 5.6 等新模型时，不再需要计划模式。
    Plan mode is no longer necessary with newer models like Opus 5 and Sonnet 5.6.
  • 构建自定义工具通常是浪费时间；简单的 HTML 工件可以取代它们。
    Building custom tools is often a waste of time; simple HTML artifacts can replace them.
  • 使用视频反馈（Supercut）比预期的更准确，准确率高达 80-90%。
    Using video feedback (Supercut) is more accurate than expected, with 80-90% accuracy.
  • AI 可以产生比设计师更好的想法，例如 Fable 建议为入职流程创建机器人 PR。
    AI can generate better ideas than designers, as seen when Fable suggested a bot-created PR for onboarding.
  • 设计师应该花更多时间在规划上，少花在执行上，类似于工程师。
    Designers should spend more time on planning and less on execution, similar to engineers.
