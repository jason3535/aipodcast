本期速览 · Overview

Gemini 产品负责人 Tulsee Doshi 解析模型研发为何既是科学也是魔法，以及这对她招募的产品经理意味着什么。

Tulsee Doshi, Head of Product for Gemini, explains why model development is part science and part magic, and what that means for the PMs she hires.

要点 · TL;DR

  • 产品经理因品味和洞察力能穿透 AI 噪音而重获价值。
    PMs regain value as taste and insight cut through AI noise.
  • 为未来模型构建：60%现在可用，20%初现曙光，20%未来愿景。
    Build for future models: 60% works now, 20% glimmers, 20% vision.
  • 安全需要监控思维链，人类洞察胜过 AI 垃圾。
    Safety needs monitoring chain of thought and human insights over AI slop.

核心观点 · Key points

  • 模型开发不可预测；产品经理既要塑造输入，也要识别意外涌现的优势。
    Model development is unpredictable; PMs must shape inputs and identify surprising emergent strengths.
  • 谷歌旨在通过 Gemini 赢得前沿竞争，同时以成本-质量曲线服务多样化用户。
    Google aims to win the frontier with Gemini while serving diverse users across cost-quality curve.
  • 安全需要有意识地监控思维链并进行干预以确保对齐。
    Safety requires intentional monitoring of chain of thought and interventions to ensure alignment.
  • 产品经理需要理解模型感觉、编写评估，并随着模型快速演进保持适应性。
    PMs need to understand model vibes, write evals, and be adaptive as models evolve rapidly.
  • 为模型未来能力构建：60% 当前可用，20% 初见曙光，20% 未来愿景。
    Build for where models will be: 60% works now, 20% glimmers, 20% future vision.
  • AI 可以通过消除繁琐工作来减少倦怠，让更多创造性和有意义的工作成为可能。
    AI can reduce burnout by removing grunt work, enabling more creative and meaningful work.

反共识 · Contrarian takes

  • 不要只构建可用的最小可行产品；要构建尚未完全可用的功能以捕捉未来的魔力。
    Don't just build an MVP that works; build features that don't fully work yet to capture future magic.
  • 在 AI 时代，产品经理比以往更关键，因为品味和洞察能穿透易于构建的噪音。
    In the AI era, PMs are more critical than ever because taste and insight cut through easy-to-build noise.
  • AI 生成的文档有助于数据综合，但洞察必须来自人类以避免垃圾内容。
    AI-generated docs are useful for data synthesis, but insights must come from humans to avoid slop.
  • 倦怠不仅仅来自长时间工作；还来自反复无常、混乱和不喜欢的工作。
    Burnout isn't just from long hours; it's from churn, chaos, and unenjoyable work.
  • 定期重新测试并移除你的框架；模型现在可能已经和你的优化一样好。
    Regularly retest and remove your harness; the model may now be as good as your optimizations.
  • 通过将自己置于挑战性、不舒服的位置来寻求偶然性，从而获得幸运。
    Seek serendipity by putting yourself in challenging, uncomfortable positions to get lucky.
