Designing the Future: Brian Lovin on AI, Notion, and Blurring Roles

本期速览 · Overview

Brian Lovin 分享了设计师如何拥抱适应性与 AI，结合他在 Notion 的紧张入职经历和 AI 代理工作。

Brian Lovin shares how designers must embrace adaptability and AI, drawing from his intense onboarding at Notion and work on AI agents.

要点 · TL;DR

  • AI 将设计师的角色从视觉设计转变为构建基于代码的框架和原型。
    AI changes the designer's role from visual design to building code-based harnesses and prototypes.
  • 理解技术系统和精确的提示词使 AI 成为强大工具，而非魔法。
    Understanding technical systems and precise prompts makes AI a powerful tool, not magic.
  • 在 AI 炒作和可能的倦怠中，专注力和适应力是关键的竞争优势。
    Focus and adaptability are key competitive advantages amid AI hype and potential burnout.

核心观点 · Key points

  • 设计师不能再仅用 Figma 模拟 AI 体验，而必须在代码中做原型，并直接塑造智能体所需的操控框架（harness）。
    Designers can no longer mock AI experiences in Figma; they must prototype in code and shape the agent harness itself.
  • 对 AI 工具保持流动而非门派之见：按任务选最佳工具，例如用 Claude 做规划、用 Cursor 打磨像素。
    Stay fluid and non-tribal with AI tools: choose the best tool per task, like Claude for planning and Cursor for pixel polish.
  • 公司应通过 API、CLI 与 MCP 让系统对 AI 可理解（legible），才能在智能体式未来中生存。
    Companies should make their systems legible to AI via APIs, CLIs, and MCP to survive the agentic future.
  • 在少数核心基础组件上死磕细节；只要基础组件足够精良，AI 非常擅长复用并外推它们。
    Sweat details on a few core primitives; AI excels at reusing and extrapolating them once they are polished.
  • 高质量的输入 token 会带来更好的模型输出；要用精准词汇和明确焦点来引导 AI 生成。
    High-quality input tokens produce better outputs; use precise vocabulary and explicit focus to steer AI generation.
  • 不要执着于设计师/PM/工程师的头衔；专注于发布好软件、解决真问题。
    Don't obsess over designer/PM/engineer titles; focus on shipping good software and solving real problems.

反共识 · Contrarian takes

  • AI 真正的影響不是速度或效率，而是让设计师能进入以前够不到的邻近领域。
    AI's real impact is not speed or efficiency—it lets designers reach adjacent fields that were previously out of reach.
  • 尽管流行“不再写代码”的说法，质量仍然要求人类阅读并编写代码完成最后一公里的打磨。
    Against the 'no code' meta, quality still demands reading and writing code for last-mile fit and finish.
  • 用两个模型互相评审并不可靠——一个发现 10 个问题，另一个说全是“安全剧场”。
    Pairing two models for review is inconsistent: one may find ten issues while the other calls them security theater.
  • AI 热潮退却即将到来；每天能保持两小时不被干扰专注，会成为巨大竞争优势。
    An AI hype comedown is coming; being undistractable for two hours daily becomes a major competitive advantage.
  • 早期采用者能容忍不完美的 AI 体验，因此粗糙上线没问题——但要重新校准自己的质量标准。
    Early adopters tolerate sub-par AI experiences, so shipping rough is okay—but reset your own quality expectations.
  • AI 往往带来更多后续修复，让人感觉更有产出，但客观质量却受损。
    AI often creates more follow-up fixes, making people feel more productive while objective quality suffers.
