核心观点 · Key points

  • 世界模型是AI的下一个前沿，超越语言模型，理解、生成和模拟世界。
    World models are the next frontier in AI, moving beyond language models to understand, generate, and simulate worlds.
  • 世界模型没有统一的定义；它们包含隐式、生成式和基于强化学习的概念。
    There is no single agreed-upon definition of world models; they encompass implicit, generative, and RL-based notions.
  • 高斯溅射是一种可微的3D表示，与神经网络集成良好，支持显式3D世界建模。
    Gaussian splats are a differentiable 3D representation that integrates well with neural networks, enabling explicit 3D world modeling.
  • 世界模型可以根据其在POMDP循环中的输出分为规划器、模拟器或渲染器。
    World models can be categorized as planners, simulators, or renderers based on their output in the POMDP loop.
  • 未来的统一世界模型将把规划、模拟和渲染能力结合到一个系统中。
    Future unified world models will combine planning, simulation, and rendering capabilities into one system.
  • Transformer仍然强大，但世界模型需要长上下文处理和损失函数的演进。
    Transformers remain powerful, but long-context handling and loss functions need evolution for world models.

反共识 · Contrarian takes

  • 仅靠语言模型不会自然产生世界理解；我们需要新的模型类型。
    Language models alone won't naturally lead to world understanding; we need new model types.
  • 隐式3D方法可能扩展到无限，而显式3D如高斯溅射适用于低算力场景。
    Implicit 3D approaches may scale to infinity, while explicit 3D like Gaussian splats are for low-compute settings.
  • 世界模型的一致性来自数据，而不仅仅是架构；高斯溅射通过构造保证一致性。
    Consistency in world models comes from data, not just architecture; Gaussian splats are consistent by construction.
  • 在架构中硬编码对称性适得其反；简单表示加上规模效果更好。
    Hard-coding symmetries in architectures is counterproductive; simple representations with scale work better.
  • 世界模型的理论构建者概念是一个无人知晓如何实现的圣杯。
    The theory-builder notion of world models is a holy grail that no one knows how to achieve.
  • 世界模型需要数百万个令牌的上下文，与LLM不同，长上下文成为日常问题。
    World models need millions of tokens of context, unlike LLMs, making long-context a daily problem.
