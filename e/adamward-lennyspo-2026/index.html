本期速览 · Overview

Adam Ward 分享了他关于在招聘中避免“厄运漏斗”并打造精英团队的见解。

Adam Ward shares his insights on avoiding the 'funnel of doom' in recruiting and building elite teams.

要点 · TL;DR

  • 招聘应像高管寻访一样，瞄准前 1%的候选人，而非申请者漏斗。
    Hiring should be treated like executive search, targeting the top 1% of candidates, not a funnel of applicants.

核心观点 · Key points

  • 招聘应像高管寻访一样，聚焦于顶尖1%的候选人，而不是申请者的漏斗。
    Hiring should be treated like executive search, focusing on the top 1% of candidates, not a funnel of applicants.
  • 明确界定技能和经验是有效招聘的基石，为寻源和评估奠定基础。
    Clear scoping of skills and experiences is the cornerstone of effective hiring, setting up sourcing and assessment.
  • 对顶尖候选人的不懈追求需要长期播种和保持联系，而非仅仅即时招聘。
    Relentless pursuit of top candidates involves planting seeds and maintaining contact over time, not just immediate hiring.
  • 候选人体验应个性化和关怀备至，双向交流，以建立强大的雇主品牌。
    Candidate experience should be personalized and caring, with a two-way exchange, to build a strong employer brand.
  • 工作样本和并肩项目是成功的最佳预测指标，为招聘决策提供强信号。
    Work samples and side-by-side projects are the highest predictors of success, providing strong signals for hiring decisions.
  • 收尾是团队协作，通过每日站会和个性化关怀来转化候选人。
    Closing is a team sport, involving daily stand-ups and personalized touches to convert candidates.

反共识 · Contrarian takes

  • “厄运漏斗”方法只能招到平庸之才，而非顶尖人才，因为它依赖那些主动申请或回应的人。
    The funnel of doom approach yields average hires, not top talent, because it relies on those who apply or respond.
  • 招聘人员在90%-110%负荷时表现出色，超出此范围则表现糟糕，因此需要合适的工作量。
    Recruiters are excellent at 90-110% capacity but terrible outside that range, so they need the right workload.
  • 最重要的面试是招聘经理的首次对话，而非现场面试，后者常被低估。
    The most important interview is the hiring manager's first conversation, not the onsite, which is often underestimated.
  • 公司不应关注简历上的公司标志，而应定义适合自身具体情境的优秀标准。
    Companies should not focus on logos on resumes; instead, they should define what great looks like for their specific context.
  • 招聘团队应成为公司中薪酬最高的团队之一，因为他们的影响力和稀缺技能。
    The recruiting team should be among the highest paid in the company due to their impact and rare skill set.
  • 人才密度关乎团队整体，而非单个10倍人才，因为优秀的人在糟糕的团队中也会受限。
    Talent density is about the collective team, not just individual 10Xers, as a great person on a bad team is throttled.
