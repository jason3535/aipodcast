本期速览 · Overview

Legora 创始人 Max 分享其 AI 法律科技公司如何在 21 个月内成为史上最快从 100 万美元增长到 1.5 亿美元 ARR 的企业。

Max, founder of Legora, shares how his AI legal tech startup became the fastest company to grow from $1M to $150M ARR in just 21 months.

要点 · TL;DR

  • 为 3-6 个月后的 AI 模型能力而构建，而非当下。
    Build for where AI models will be in 3-6 months, not today.
  • 招聘是特权而非义务；领导者必须面试每一位总监及以上级别的新人。
    Recruiting is a privilege; leaders must interview every director+ hire.
  • 法律 AI 需要技术娴熟的驾驶员；凭感觉起草不像凭感觉编程那样可行。
    Legal AI needs skilled drivers; vibe drafting doesn't work like vibe coding.

核心观点 · Key points

  • 为模型3-6个月后的能力构建产品，而非当下或一年后。
    Build for where models will be in 3-6 months, not today or a year out.
  • 招聘是特权而非义务；领导者必须面试每一位总监及以上级别的候选人。
    Recruiting is a privilege, not an obligation; leaders must interview every director+ hire.
  • 绩效管理是已解决的问题；不要重新发明，只需严格执行。
    Performance management is a solved problem; don't reinvent it, just execute rigorously.
  • 在顺境时投资文化和团队，为低谷期建立缓冲。
    Invest in culture and team during good times to build a cushion for downturns.
  • AI现在是最差的阶段；我们才刚跑完漫长马拉松的第一英里。
    AI is the worst it will ever be; we're only at mile one of a long marathon.
  • 领导者应通过为关键岗位招聘更聪明的人，让自己变得可替代。
    Leaders should make themselves redundant by hiring smarter people for key roles.

反共识 · Contrarian takes

  • 大多数组织过于害怕给出明确反馈，尤其是在欧洲。
    Most organizations are too afraid to give clear feedback, especially in Europe.
  • 法律AI需要非常熟练的驾驶员；氛围起草不像氛围编程那样可行。
    You need a very skilled driver for legal AI; vibe drafting doesn't work like vibe coding.
  • 在绩效管理上冒险是愚蠢的风险；不要重新发明它。
    Taking risk in performance management is a stupid risk; don't reinvent it.
  • 一些最佳表现者最初得到低分；短期痛苦换取长期收益。
    Some best performers were given low scores first; short-term pain for long-term gain.
  • 在法律服务交付中不使用AI几乎是不道德的。
    It's almost unethical to not use AI in legal services delivery.
  • 法律服务需求是无限的；增加供给将扩大获取途径，而不仅仅是降低成本。
    Legal services demand is infinite; growing supply will expand access, not just cut costs.
