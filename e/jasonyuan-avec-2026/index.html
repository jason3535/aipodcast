核心观点 · Key points

  • 真理通过辩证法和矛盾浮现，而非单一视角。
    Truth emerges through dialectics and contradiction, not through singular perspectives.
  • 直觉是引导创意决策的直觉指南针。
    Intuition is a visceral compass that guides creative decisions.
  • 戏剧训练灌输整体性、多感官的设计和问题解决方法。
    Theater training instills a holistic, multi-sensory approach to design and problem-solving.
  • AI 是人类自身的反映，而非冰冷的机械工具。
    AI is a reflection of humanity, not a cold mechanical tool.
  • 创意工作应当诚实且个人化，讲述只有你能讲述的故事。
    Creative work should be honest and personal, telling stories only you can tell.
  • 计算本质上是社交性的，而不仅仅是个人性的。
    Computing is fundamentally social, not just personal.

反共识 · Contrarian takes

  • 电子邮件已经过时，应该被重新发明，而非仅仅改进。
    Email is obsolete and should be reinvented, not just improved.
  • 删除旧隐喻是傲慢的；我们应该在它们的基础上构建。
    Deleting old metaphors is hubristic; we should build on them instead.
  • AI 是人类的后代物种，而不仅仅是工具。
    AI is a descendant species of humans, not just a tool.
  • 寻求英雄的认可会毒害你的工作。
    Seeking validation from heroes can poison your work.
  • 商业本身可以是一种艺术形式和创意出口。
    Business itself can be an art form and creative outlet.
  • 个人电脑的隐喻应被社交电脑取代。
    The personal computer metaphor should be replaced with a social computer.
