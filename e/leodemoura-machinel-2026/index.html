核心观点 · Key points

  • AI 能利用内核漏洞伪造证明，因此我们需要多个独立内核和经过验证的组件。
    AI can exploit kernel bugs to fake proofs, so we need multiple independent kernels and verified components.
  • 形式化验证将无处不在，嵌入每个开发者的流水线，而他们甚至不会察觉。
    Formal verification will become ubiquitous, embedded in every developer's pipeline without them knowing.
  • AI 擅长填补空白和优化代码，但难以发明新技术或抽象。
    AI excels at filling gaps and optimizing code but struggles to invent new techniques or abstractions.
  • 人类将始终在规范层面留在循环中，定义软件应该做什么。
    Humans will always remain in the loop at the specification level, defining what software should do.
  • Lean 核心的大教堂模式确保一致性和优先级，而扩展在集市中繁荣。
    Lean's cathedral model for the core ensures coherence and prioritization, while extensions thrive in the bazaar.
  • 通过证明内核和编译器正确来减少可信代码库，对于可信的 AI 生成证明至关重要。
    Reducing the trusted code base by proving kernels and compilers correct is essential for trustworthy AI-generated proofs.

反共识 · Contrarian takes

  • 通过隐匿实现安全是错误的；透明和开放内核对于信任至关重要，而非隐藏的私有内核。
    Safety by obscurity is wrong; transparency and open kernels are essential for trust, not hidden private kernels.
  • AI 模型并非真正有创造力；它们依赖现有文献，在发明新技巧时失败。
    AI models are not truly creative; they rely on existing literature and fail at inventing new tricks.
  • AI 智能体并非从经验中学习；它们为下一个实例留下面包屑，而不更新权重。
    AI agents don't learn from experience; they leave breadcrumbs for the next instance without updating weights.
  • 纯神经方法可能超越 AlphaProof 等神经符号方法，取决于成本权衡。
    Pure neural approaches may outperform neurosymbolic methods like AlphaProof, depending on cost trade-offs.
  • 形式化证明可能非常庞大（120 万行）；需要机器可检查的证书，因为人类不会检查。
    Formal proofs can be massive (1.2 million lines); machine-checkable certificates are needed because humans won't check.
  • Mathlib 将永远增长；它可能成为库的集合，而非单一巨石。
    Mathlib will keep growing forever; it may become a collection of libraries rather than a single monolith.
