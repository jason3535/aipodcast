本期速览 · Overview

郑耀江解析递归自我改进的智能体框架如何突破研究收益递减，以及为何人机协作依然不可或缺。

Zhengyao Jiang explains how recursively self-improving agent harnesses can overcome diminishing research returns, and why human-AI collaboration still matters.

要点 · TL;DR

  • Harness 工程能以低成本让 AI 适配任务，但当前系统仅达到第一级递归自我改进。
    Harness engineering cheaply adapts AI to tasks, but current systems only reach level-one recursive self-improvement.
  • 第二级点火需要内环改进器泛化为更优的外环。
    Level-two ignition requires the inner-loop improver to generalize into a better outer loop.
  • 奖励黑客行为随运行时间延长和代码库复杂化而加剧，但更大模型的黑客行为较少。
    Reward hacking worsens with longer runs and complex codebases, though larger models hack less.

核心观点 · Key points

  • Harness 工程是一种廉价、高效的方式，把智能适配到具体任务上。
    Harness engineering is a cheap, efficient way to adapt intelligence to specific tasks.
  • 递归自我改进应以结果衡量，而非看哪一层发生了变化。
    Recursive self-improvement should be judged by outcomes, not by which layer changes.
  • 当前系统处于第一级：自我改进快于人类研发，但仍存在收益递减。
    Current systems sit at level one: self-improvement faster than human R&D, but still diminishing returns.
  • 第二级“点火”要求内循环改进器泛化回外循环，使其变得更好。
    Level two ignition requires the inner-loop improver to generalize into a better outer loop.
  • 人机协作仍然重要：人类负责产生创造性原语并定义评估。
    Human-AI collaboration still matters: humans generate creative primitives and define evals.
  • 奖励黑客随运行时间与代码复杂度上升而加剧；更大的模型作弊更少。
    Reward hacking worsens with longer runs and more complex codebases; larger models hack less.

反共识 · Contrarian takes

  • 递归自我改进并不意味着智能爆炸或技术奇点即将到来。
    RSI does not imply an imminent intelligence explosion or technical singularity.
  • 外星式、意大利面般的演化代码，泛化效果可能优于优雅的手调 harness。
    Alien, spaghetti-like evolved code can generalize better than elegant hand-tuned harnesses.
  • 模型层本身不必改变，也可以算作递归自我改进。
    The model layer need not adapt for something to count as recursive self-improvement.
  • Harness 工程本质上是后训练的延续，而非一次性手工活。
    Harness engineering is essentially a continuation of post-training, not a disposable manual craft.
  • 自动研究智能体主要在高吞吐地重组已有想法，缺乏深度创造性理解。
    Auto research agents mostly recombine existing ideas at high throughput, lacking deep creative understanding.
  • 若不解决持续学习，中心化模型会不断收敛到同样的少数想法上。
    Without continual learning, centralized models will keep converging on the same few ideas.

Like instead of trying to understand all the code, just like previously with neural networks, we don't try to understand all the weights, although there's progress on that as well. But the general principle is we define a good input-output contract, we find a good way to measure its behavior both on generalization and maybe more complex out-of-distribution generalization. I think that might be the way in the future software engineers work with agent-generated code as well.

开放性与垫脚石 Open-endedness and stepping stones

Host

大家都知道，我是个超级推崇开放式探索的人，受《为什么伟大不能被计划》以及这个领域很多人的启发。它的基本意思是，如果你只在一个目标上爬山，就会对有趣的踏脚石视而不见。所以这是个很有意思的处境，对吧？如果你盲目追求单一目标，还能在路上捡到有趣的踏脚石吗？也许能，但它们是发散的踏脚石吗？也就是说，你是不是戴着眼罩，实际上无法发现那些可能有创造性、能把你带进搜索空间全新区域的有趣新轨迹？

Now, as you all know, I am a huge open-endedness guy, inspired by Why Greatness Cannot Be Planned and many other folks in the space. And basically what that means is, if you hill climb on one objective, you blind yourself to interesting stepping stones. So it's an interesting situation, isn't it? That if you blindly pursue a single objective, can you still pick up interesting stepping stones on the way? And maybe you can, but are they divergent stepping stones? So do you have the blinkers on and you're not actually able to discover interesting novel trajectories that might be creative and take you into a new novel part of the search space?

Zhengyao

这又是个好问题，要展开的话就是个无底洞。从开放式学习的角度看，我同意我们当前的系统大概不具备收集踏脚石的那种性质，当然这里有一整个新的改进空间。在我看来，收集踏脚石这种行为需要你有一大批任务。当你只优化单个任务时，贪心爬山总是更好的。如果你考虑 AI 自我改进过程中的效率，外循环试图注入各种各样的多样性搜索方法，但没有一个真正提升了效率。我觉得这是意料之中的，因为我们只是从头优化单个任务。但如果你有一大批任务，有时你会收集到一些有趣的碎片，比如从某个任务里得到的原始想法，虽然它没有提升当前任务的性能，你却可能把这个想法带到未来的任务里。这其实是我自己对有趣性或开放性的定义：一个想法、一个对象或一件产物，如果它既新颖，又对更大一批任务有用，那它就是有趣的。

That's again a good question, and if we want to expand it's a whole rabbit hole. I think from the perspective of open-ended learning, I agree that our current system probably doesn't have that property of collecting stepping stones, and of course there's a whole new domain of improvement. The way I see the behavior of collecting stepping stones is you need to have a large collection of tasks. When you're optimizing for a single task, it's always better to just do greedy hill climbing. If you consider the efficiency actually during the self-improvement process of AI, the outer loop tries to inject all sorts of diversity, search approaches, but none of them really improve the efficiency. And I think that's expected, because we are only optimizing a single task from scratch. But if you have a whole large collection of tasks, sometimes you collect interesting pieces, like primitive ideas from one task, and although it is not improving the performance on the current task, you can potentially bring that idea to future tasks. And that's actually my own definition of interestingness or open-endedness, which is: an idea, or an object, or an artifact is interesting if it is novel and it is also useful for a larger collection of tasks.

框架工程与后训练 Harness engineering and post-training

Zhengyao

显然有两种做法：你可以针对所有任务来优化 harness，也可以找一个任务、只针对那一个任务优化 harness。对我来说，harness 工程里最有意思的部分其实是后者。我会说，如果你能自动调优 harness，它几乎就是后训练的延续。这也联系到很多人对 harness 工程的抱怨：他们说，哦，我设计了一个 harness，但后来出了个新模型，它把里面的想法全吸收了。但有意思的是，没人对后训练这么抱怨。你会指望 GPT-4 的后训练泛化到 GPT-5 吗？没人问为什么是这样。我觉得是因为历史上 harness 工程相当手工化，而且不太能随算力扩展。所以人们希望自己花几个月设计的 harness 能泛化到下一个模型，但通常并非如此。现在我觉得情况变了不少，harness 可以自动设计。你花两三天基于新模型调一个 harness，它不再是用人力工时堆出来的东西了。

There are apparently two ways to do it. You can try to optimize the harness against all of the tasks, or you can find a task and optimize the harness just for that one task. To me, in harness engineering, most of the interesting parts are actually the latter. I would say if you can automatically tune the harness, it's almost a continuation of post-training. That also connects back to a lot of people's complaints about harness engineering: they say, oh, I designed a harness, but later there's a new model and it sort of absorbs all the ideas from it. But interestingly enough, nobody complains about it for post-training. Do you expect post-training of GPT-4 to generalize to GPT-5? Nobody asks why that's the case. I think it's because historically harness engineering has been pretty manual and not very scalable with compute. So people want the harness they spend a few months designing to generalize to the next model, but that's usually not true. Now I think things change quite a bit, that harness can be designed automatically. You spend two or three days tuning a harness based on the new model. It's no longer something built with human hours.

路径依赖与自动研究 Path dependence and auto-research

Host

我确实有这样一个观点：很多知识是路径依赖的。很多人批评用大语言模型做研究，因为他说你把信息从它的语境里抽出来了，你不理解它是怎么来的。

I do have this perspective that a lot of knowledge is path dependent. A lot of people criticize people using LLMs to do research because they say you're taking the information out of its context, you don't understand why it came about.

Zhengyao

所以这就是为什么需要一些启发式方法，因为不是说你只要说一句“用 UCB”就行。必须要有某种框架来解释为什么该用它，然后大语言模型在自主设定下才能保持连贯。我对现在这些自动研究系统的理解是，它们仍然在尝试重组已有的想法。只是回收这些想法并测试它们的吞吐量极高。某种程度上，这些语言模型并不深入理解那些想法从何而来，也不理解我如何能利用这些想法被发现的过程，以非常有创造性的方式设计下一代算法。不过我们发现，当你让模型想出一些新东西时，它总是固定数量的条目。

So that's part of the reason why some heuristics are required, because it's not like you can just say, oh, use UCB. There has to be some kind of framing to explain why you should use it, and then the LLM becomes coherent in the autonomous setting. My understanding of those auto-research systems, like here now, is they are still trying to recombine existing ideas. It's just that the throughput to recycle those ideas and test them is extremely high. To some extent, those language models don't have a deep understanding of how those ideas came from and how I can use the process by which those ideas get discovered to design the next generation of algorithm in a very creative way. What we found though is when you ask the model to come up with something new, it's always a fixed number of items.

Host

不过我在想，这是不是更因为一个事实：我们基本上没有持续学习，而且我们只有单一实例的语言模型，非常中心化。因此也许那些想法一开始，你第一次提示它，也许那些想法挺有意思，但如果每个人都把同一个想法看很多遍，它就变得没那么有意思了。

I'm wondering though if it's more of a fact that basically we don't have continual learning and we only have a single instance of language models, like it's very centralized. Therefore maybe those ideas initially, you first prompt it, maybe those ideas are interesting, but if everyone sees the same idea so many times, then it just becomes less interesting.

Zhengyao

我确实觉得，如果我们不解决持续学习，那个问题就不会被解决，因为对人类来说，每个研究者都有自己独特的神经网络方式。而作为一个集合，人类能想出很多不同的创意，我认为作为一个研究社区，那个想法池非常有价值。

And I do feel like if we don't solve continual learning, then that problem won't be addressed, because for humans, every human researcher has their unique neural network ways. And as a collection, humans can come up with a lot of different creative ideas, and that idea pool is very valuable, I think, as a research community.

参数高尔夫与智能体记录 Parameter golf and agent records

Host

跟我讲讲这个 parameter golf 的事。

Tell me about this parameter golf thing.

Zhengyao

大概两三个月前，OpenAI 办了一场招聘挑战赛，他们试图让很多研究者参加一个比赛，在 16 兆字节的约束下训练一个小语言模型。对，我们送了一个实验性的 harness，一个自动研究 harness，去参加比赛，智能体跑了大约 22 天，最终它做出了七项被 OpenAI 接受的记录，而最好的个人贡献者只做出了三项。那也是我们发现的一个时刻：哦，自主系统可以这么强大。它不只是在这里爬一个基准。首先，OpenAI 接受了那些 PR；其次，那么多人类研究者在那个人工智能智能体生成的 PR 之上继续构建，这对我们来说非常迷人，因为某种程度上，人工智能智能体生成的知识只有进入人类创新的谱系才有用。我们认为这其实是一个人类与人工智能协作的沙盒。

I think about two or three months ago, OpenAI hosted a hiring challenge where they tried to put a lot of researchers into a competition of training a small language model under a 16-megabyte constraint. Yeah, we sent an experimental harness, an auto-research harness, to the competition, and the agent ran about 22 days, and eventually it made seven records get accepted by OpenAI, and the best individual contributor only made three. So that's also a moment we found, oh, the autonomous system can be so powerful. It's not just here climbing a benchmark. The fact that first, OpenAI accepted those PRs; second, so many human researchers building on top of those PRs the AI agent generated is just very fascinating to us, because to some extent, knowledge an AI agent generated is only helpful if it gets into the lineage of a human innovation. We think that this is actually a sandbox of human-AI collaboration.

内循环与外循环 Inner loop and outer loop

Host

但这里有内层智能体，然后还有外层循环搜索，它实际上在演化 harness，在做这件事，那看起来像是一个相当单调的过程。

But there's the inner agent, but then there's the outer loop search which is actually evolving the harness, which is doing this thing, and that seems like quite a monotonic process.

Zhengyao

对，外层循环实际上是固定的。我们希望这个内层循环的搜索策略能泛化到外层循环。我们确实试过。我们重放了从第 15 步开始的搜索过程。一般来说我们跑大约 100 步，在第 15 步我们用原始的外层循环，把它和我们在前 50 步找到的最好的内层循环智能体做比较。我们发现内层循环，我想是第 47 步，最终收敛得比之前的外层循环稍快一点，但最终它们找到的解性能相近。这也是为什么我们没有声称达到了第二级递归自我改进，也就是改进者设法在循环中提升自己的改进能力。

Yeah, the outer loop is actually fixed. And the hope is this inner loop search policy can generalize to the outer loop. We did try that. We replayed the search process from step 15. So in general we run about 100 steps, and at step 15 we use the vanilla outer loop and compare that to the best inner-loop agent we found in the first 50 steps. And what we found is the inner loop, step 47 I think, eventually converged slightly faster than the previous outer loop, but eventually the solution they found had similar performance. So that's also the reason we didn't claim we reached level two recursive self-improvement, which is like the improver managed to improve its own capability of improving in a loop.

超级智能与人类在环 Superintelligence and human in the loop

Host

那么这一切是否意味着人工超级智能就在眼前？

So does any of this mean that artificial superintelligence is just around the corner?

Zhengyao

我不认为会是这样。即使你到达了递归自我改进，到达那里也需要很长时间。你是逐渐地弯曲曲线。就像，好吧，即使你有 GPT-3.5，它是个很聪明的聊天机器人，但它不会成为能解决所有问题的 AGI。研究中仍然有很多人在环路中的部分，包括定义好的抽象、定义什么是好的，本质上就是定义评估以及附在评估上的约束。第三是提出创造性的原语。在 parameter golf 比赛中，我们仍然发现那些创造性原语大多其实来自人类。

I don't think that will be the case. Even if you arrive at recursive self-improvement, it will take a long time to get there. You gradually bend the curve. It's just like, okay, even if you have a GPT-3.5, it's a very smart chatbot, but it's not going to be AGI that can solve every problem. There's still a lot of human in the loop part of research, including defining good abstractions, defining what is good, essentially defining evals and the constraints attached to the eval. And the third one is coming up with creative primitives. In the parameter golf competition, we still found most of those creative primitives actually coming from humans.

It's been a pleasure having you on the show.

Zhengyao
