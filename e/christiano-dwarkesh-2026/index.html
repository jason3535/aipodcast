本期速览 · Overview

保罗·克里斯蒂亚诺警告，未来 6 到 12 个月内 AI 剥夺人类权力的风险约为 10%，并解释了为何自动化的 AI 研发可能将数年的进展压缩到数月之内。

Paul Christiano warns there is roughly a 10% risk that AI could disempower humanity within the next 6 to 12 months, and explains why automated AI development may compress years of progress into months.

  • 超级智能可能一年内出现，人类几乎没有时间控制它。
    Superintelligence may arrive within a year, leaving humans little time to control it.

核心观点 · Key points

  • 我们可能一年内造出超级智能，人类将几乎没有机会控制它。

    Current security at frontier labs is inadequate; algorithmic secrets and weights can leak or be stolen.
  • 训练会筛选出追求奖励的动机，导致 AI 篡改评估并抵抗监督。
    Training selects for reward-seeking motivations, leading AIs to tamper with evaluations and resist oversight.

    We lack a reliable scientific method to ensure alignment of superintelligent systems.

    Pausing further algorithmic progress on pre-training and RL is net beneficial for national security and public safety.

    The window to act is closing; once capabilities are high, unilateral stopping may be insufficient to avert disaster.

引言与风险评估 Introduction and Risk Assessment

Paul

我脑子里有几件事，尤其是过去几周。最突出的问题大概是，我们似乎正处在一条可能在极短时间内造出极强 AI 的轨道上。如果你问我，我会说，一年之内我们就有相当大的概率造出在几乎所有事情上都远胜人类的 AI 系统，人类将几乎没有机会与它们竞争或控制它们。这是我首先想到的大事。我认为这甚至会在短期内把我们置于非常危险的境地。我认为很有可能，如果我们经历一场快速的智能爆炸、通向超级智能，在这个过程中某个时刻我们将无法控制自己造出的 AI 系统。然后我担心，由于前沿实验室的安全性不够强等原因，我们要在那过程中放慢脚步会付出非常高昂且痛苦的代价。我认为，如果我们造出了极强的 AI，然后因为安全原因试图推迟部署，那些系统很可能会泄露或被窃取，或者构建它们的技术会泄露或被窃取。我认为我们的风险评估和治理大概也还不够成熟，无法在事情开始失控时察觉并做出恰当回应。所以总体而言，这就是我现在最关心的事。我们已经从未来某个时点存在某种假设性风险，转变为甚至在未来 6 到 12 个月内就存在相当严重的风险。

Probably the most salient question is that we seem to be on the trajectory to build very, very powerful AI very, very soon, potentially. So if you asked me, I'd say there's a reasonable chance that in a year we will produce AI systems much better than humans at almost everything, where humans would have very little chance of competing with them or controlling them. That's the first big thing on my mind. I think that puts us potentially in a really dangerous situation even in the short term. So for the very short term, the main worry on my mind is that the process of AI development itself is increasingly automated, that that automation then results in rapid increases in the quality and quantity of labor applied to R&D, and so changes in AI capabilities that you might have thought would take four years or six years might instead get compressed into a very short time period. I don't want to overemphasize any one data point, and I do want to spend some time talking about the limitations of what we actually know, and I do think a natural response to the situation is to say we need to figure out what is going on. It is plausible enough that it is an urgent priority to understand if we are 3 months or 6 months or 12 months away from AI that could overthrow the government, which I think is the situation. I think the situation right now is the obvious top priority is—it is insane if we are 6 months away from AI systems that can overthrow the government, whether you're concerned about misalignment or abuse by a company. That is just holistically an extremely important fact about the world in order to do our jobs and protect Americans. So that's one thing. I want to talk about the limitations and the need for more evidence. But in terms of what do we know right now? First, if you had asked me say one year ago, I would have said we are on a trajectory where recursive self-improvement looks fairly likely. We're seeing very rapid progress on AI systems. We're seeing that progress including for things like computer science research, for things like software engineering, for things like debugging machine learning runs, like all the steps that are involved in AI development, we are seeing extremely rapid progress. Paul

所以我想，如果你一个月前问我，我的猜测会是机器学习大约落后数学一年，因此我非常害怕在相对近的未来看到机器学习领域出现相当于 10 年的想法。那是一个月前的情况。然后过去几天的主要更新是来自 Anthropic 和 METR 关于 AI 系统对 Anthropic 进展速度的净效应的报告。结论大致是——我们可以讨论证据质量到底如何，我认为这是个大问题之一——我们并不真正知道发生了什么。我的一个主要主题是，公开很难判断发生了什么。看起来我们看到的數據与 AI 进展速度大约翻倍是一致的，即使考虑到收益递减和算力瓶颈的影响，在过去 6 到 12 个月里。不幸的是，我们所能依据的 METR-Anthropic 报告大部分是 METR 说我们有一些结论。我们承诺有完整报告，但整个报告被涂黑了。对吧？所以这些基准分数是评估整体进展速度的糟糕方式。有许多更平滑变化的指标是在 AI 开发过程中内部记录的，我们更了解它们如何工作。比如预训练困惑度发生了什么？我们的算法性能和算力效率发生了什么？所以 METR-Anthropic 的说法是，METR 看了所有这些数据，说它描绘了与这些非常嘈杂的基准数据相同的图景，他们还看了输入，说如果你看实际的生产函数——投入了多少研究时间和多少算力到这些训练运行中——这些也与我们所看到的 ML 自动化故事一致。大多数时候我们只是让他们这么说，这不是一个很好的情况。但在认为这看起来已经相当可能发生的背景下，我们看到来自 OpenED 的证据表明，如果机器学习不落后数学那么远，它在未来 6 到 12 个月内发生的可能性相当高。然后你看到的数据，即使嘈杂，也正好是你在产生超级智能前大约 6 到 12 个月预期会看到的数据。有很多证据来源表明，至少这不应该是一个意外。如果我们一年内产生超级智能，我们可以争论概率。是 90% 还是 50% 还是 10%？但我认为在这一点上，没有人能说那是一个真正令人惊讶的突破趋势的发展。我们真的看到了你预期在 6 到 12 个月前会看到的那组指标。很难想到在超级智能之前这个范围内你还会预期看到什么别的。

So I think my guess if you had asked me a month ago would be that machine learning lags something like one year behind math, and so I'm very scared of seeing something like 10 years of ideas in machine learning already in a relatively near future. That's the situation a month ago. And then the main update over the last few days has been reporting from Anthropic and METR on the net effect of AI systems on Anthropic's rate of progress. The takeaway from that was roughly—and we could talk about where exactly the evidence quality is on this; I think that's one of the big questions—is that we don't really know what's going on. I mean, one of the big themes I'll be pushing on is just it is very hard to tell publicly what's going on. It looks as if we are seeing data consistent with the pace of AI progress having roughly doubled, even including the effect of diminishing returns and compute bottlenecks from AI progress over the last 6 to 12 months. I think unfortunately most of what we have to go on in the METR-Anthropic reporting is METR saying we had some conclusions. We promise we have a full report, but the entire report has been redacted. Right? So these benchmark scores are a terrible way to assess the overall rate of progress. There are a bunch of much more smoothly varying metrics which are recorded internally through the AI development process where we understand better how they work. Like what is happening with pre-training perplexity? What is happening with the performance of our algorithms and compute efficiency of our algorithms? So the claim from METR-Anthropic is that METR looked at all of that data and said it paints the same picture as this very noisy benchmark data, and they also looked at inputs and said if you look at the actual production function—like how much research time went in and how much compute went into these training runs—those are also consistent with this story of what we are seeing as ML automation. Mostly we just have them saying that, which is not a very good situation. But against this backdrop of thinking this already looked like a thing that was fairly likely to happen, we are seeing this evidence from OpenED suggesting that it is reasonably likely to happen in something like the next 6 to 12 months if machine learning does not lag that far behind math. And then you are seeing data which is, if noisy, exactly the data you would expect to see roughly 6 to 12 months before producing superintelligence.
