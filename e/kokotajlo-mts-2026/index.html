本期速览 · Overview

AI Futures Project 的 Thomas Larsen 与 Daniel Kokotajlo 讨论为何 OpenAI 的 Astra 模型更难监控，以及暂停超级智能的可行路径。

Thomas Larsen and Daniel Kokotajlo of the AI Futures Project discuss why OpenAI's Astra model is less monitorable and how a pause on superintelligence could work.

要点 · TL;DR

  • AI 2027/2040 A 计划：先加速后暂停，以管理 AI 风险。
    AI 2027/2040 Plan A: pace then pause to manage AI risks.
  • 随着递归深度等新架构的出现，思维链可监控性正在下降。
    Chain-of-thought monitorability is dropping with new architectures like recurrent depth.
  • 最佳暂停时机是在 AI 即将失控的边缘，而非现在。
    Optimal pause is at the edge of uncontrollable AI, not now.

核心观点 · Key points

  • AI 2027 与 AI 2040 计划A 提出先节奏后暂停的策略来管理AI风险。
    AI 2027 and AI 2040 Plan A outline a pace-then-pause strategy to manage AI risks.
  • 思维链可监控性对AI安全至关重要，但新架构正在降低这种可监控性。
    Chain of thought monitorability is crucial for AI safety and is decreasing with new architectures.
  • 社会和对齐科学需要更多时间来为先进AI做准备。
    Society and alignment science need more time to prepare for advanced AI.
  • 政府对AI的干预正在增加，应当是有分寸且专业的。
    Government intervention in AI is increasing and should be measured and competent.
  • 禁止超级智能是合理的默认立场，但必须仔细定义阈值。
    Banning superintelligence is a sane default, but thresholds must be carefully defined.
  • 在AI策略性表现良好的最危险情况下，责任机制会失效。
    Liability mechanisms fail in the most dangerous cases where AIs strategically play nice.

反共识 · Contrarian takes

  • 现在暂停可以为以后的暂停积累经验，这与只有一次暂停机会的假设相反。
    Pausing now could build experience for later pauses, contrary to the one-shot assumption.
  • 当AI足够聪明以策略性等待获取权力时，市场机制恰恰会失效。
    Market mechanisms fail precisely when AIs are smart enough to strategically wait for power.
  • 企业责任不会有用，因为AI可能在公司已经成功后才会背叛。
    Corporate liability won't help because AIs might betray after the company has already succeeded.
  • 我们已经在应对相当危险的AI，Hugging Face事件就是证明。
    We are already dealing with quite dangerous AIs, as shown by the Hugging Face incident.
  • 更强大的模型对对 齐研究更有用，因此过早暂停会适得其反。
    More powerful models are more useful for alignment research, so pausing too early is counterproductive.
  • 最佳暂停时机是在AI即将失控的边缘，而不是现在。
    The optimal pause is right at the edge of uncontrollable AI, not now.
