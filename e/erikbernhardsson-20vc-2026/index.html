本期速览 · Overview

Modal 联合创始人兼 CEO Erik 解析为何专业化推理模型有望造就千亿美元公司、开源大模型推理为何尚未见顶，以及创始人对算力成本的常见误判。

Modal co-founder and CEO Erik explains why specialized inference models could build a $100B company, why open-source LLM inference hasn't peaked, and what founders miss about compute costs.

要点 · TL;DR

  • 推理是新的云，是千亿美元级的平台机会，远不止卖 token。
    Inference is the new cloud, a $100B+ platform opportunity beyond selling tokens.
  • 拥有广泛的产品套件能创造转换成本；只卖 token 是价格战，没有赢家。
    Owning a broad product suite creates switching costs; selling tokens alone is a race to the bottom.
  • 新云厂商向上做推理，很难复制 Modal 五年的基础设施积累。
    Neoclouds moving up into inference will struggle to replicate Modal's five-year infrastructure.

核心观点 · Key points

  • 推理将成为一个巨大的市场，如同新的云，需要包含后训练、沙箱和智能体工具的全平台。
    Inference will be an enormous market, like the new cloud, requiring a full platform of post-training, sandboxes, and agentic tools.
  • 拥有广泛的产品套件能创造转换成本和差异化；仅卖token是逐底竞争。
    Owning a broad product suite creates switching costs and differentiation; selling tokens alone is a race to the bottom.
  • 推理市场将碎片化为专业产品，但Modal可以在一个平台下托管许多产品。
    The inference market will fragment into specialized products, but Modal can host many of them under one platform.
  • 后训练在编码等反馈闭环有限的任务上效果很好，但在更长周期的任务上可能面临限制。
    Post-training works well for tasks with finite feedback loops like coding, but may face limits for longer-horizon tasks.
  • 软件工程需求将增长；提高工程师效率会增加对软件的整体需求。
    Software engineering demand will grow; making engineers more efficient increases overall demand for software.
  • 市场将出现回调，但AI的长期机会如此之大，值得忍受。
    There will be a market pullback, but the long-term opportunity in AI is so large it's worth enduring.

反共识 · Contrarian takes

  • 创业不应像吃玻璃；你可以享受日常工作，而不以退出为目标。
    Founding a company shouldn't feel like eating glass; you can enjoy the daily work and not aim for an exit.
  • 以较低估值少融资可能更好；让VC对下一轮保持渴望。
    Raising less money at a lower valuation can be better; keep VCs hungry for the next round.
  • 如今大多数公司缺乏软件利润率；由于多层堆栈，90%利润率的时代已经结束。
    Most companies today lack software margins; the 90% margin era is gone due to the multi-layer stack.
  • CUDA不是护城河；它是一个烦人的编程模型，尽管生态系统的内核优化很重要。
    CUDA is not the moat; it's an annoying programming model, though the ecosystem's kernel optimizations matter.
  • 向上进入推理层的Neoclouds将举步维艰；很难复制Modal五年的基础设施。
    Neoclouds moving up the stack into inference will struggle; it's very hard to replicate Modal's five-year infrastructure.
  • 并非每家公司都需要拥有自己的智能；延迟、成本和平台服务往往更重要。
    Not every company needs to own its intelligence; latency, cost, and platform services often matter more.
