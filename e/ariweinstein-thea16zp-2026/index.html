OpenAI's Ari Weinstein and James Sun explain how GPT-5 Astra pushes computer-using agents past average human speed toward superhuman reliability, and what that means for consumer and enterprise adoption.

    Computer use now beats average human speed, nearing superhuman, transforming interaction.
  • 通用电脑使用能像人一样操作任何应用，不同于 API 集成，实现广泛自动化。

核心观点 · Key points

  • 计算机使用现已快于普通人，并接近超人速度，这将是交互方式的巨变。
    Computer use is now faster than the average human and nearing superhuman speed, a sea change for interaction.
  • 计算机使用具有通用性：它像人一样操作任何应用，不同于基于API的集成。
    Computer use is universal: it works with any application like a human, unlike API-based integrations.
  • 企业需要确定性控制、沙箱和人工介入调节来建立对智能体的信任。
    Enterprises need deterministic controls, sandboxing, and human-in-the-loop dials to build trust in agents.
  • 近期最大的企业机会是自动化ERP、CRM和财务中的繁琐工作流。
    The biggest near-term enterprise opportunities are automating tedious workflows in ERPs, CRMs, and finance.
  • 主动性是关键：智能体应适应用户，而非用户适应它，从而降低采用摩擦。
    Proactivity is key: agents should adapt to users, not the other way around, reducing adoption friction.
  • 我们正同时处于消费级和企业级计算机使用的规模扩张时刻。
    We are at the scaling moment for computer use in both consumer and enterprise simultaneously.

反共识 · Contrarian takes

  • 传统软件不会一夜消失；计算机使用将与之共存并推动其改进。
    Legacy software won't disappear overnight; computer use will coexist with and pressure it to improve.

  • 智能体不会取代人类浏览；WebMCP让人类与智能体共享同一界面。
    Agents won't replace human browsing; WebMCP lets humans and agents share the same interface.
  • 最难的不是模型能力，而是让计算机使用在不同场景下连贯且达到消费级。
    The hardest part isn't model capability but making computer use coherent and consumer-grade across contexts.
  • 初创公司仍有护城河：深厚的行业专长和服务实验室忽视的复杂工作流。
    Startups still have a moat: deep industry expertise and servicing complex workflows that labs overlook.
