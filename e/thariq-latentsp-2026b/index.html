Thariq Shihipar 谈为何 AI 既是工业革命也是文艺复兴，以及为何把人类留在反馈回路中才是构建品味的关键。

Thariq Shihipar on why AI is both an industrial revolution and a renaissance, and why keeping humans in the feedback loop is the key to building with taste.

要点 · TL;DR

  • AI 既是工业革命也是文艺复兴，两者可以在同一个项目中同时发生。
    AI is both an industrial revolution and a renaissance; both can happen in one project.
  • 保持人类反馈闭环，并内部试用（dogfood）自己的工具，以避免粗制滥造。
    Keep human feedback in the loop and dogfood your tools to avoid slop.
  • 瓶颈不是智能或 token，而是与模型协作的工作方式。
    Bottleneck is not intelligence or tokens, but ways of working with models.

核心观点 · Key points

  • AI 既是工业革命也是文艺复兴；两者可以在同一个项目中同时发生。
    AI is both an industrial revolution and a renaissance; both can happen in the same project.
  • 保留人类反馈闭环；吃自己的狗粮，避免产出垃圾。
    Keep the human feedback loop in; dogfood your own tools to avoid slop.
  • 模型会变得更通用，能以你想要的任何格式工作，而不只是一种奇怪的方式。
    Models get more general, working in any format you want, not just one weird way.
  • 设计构建软件的体验；用脚本语言让代码感觉像规格说明。
    Design the experience of building software; use scripting languages to make code feel like specs.
  • 决策模型未被充分利用；它们在硬编码和完全推理之间提供了中间地带。
    Decision models are underutilized; they offer a middle ground between hardcoded and full inference.
  • RSI 将把 AI 研究变成知识工作，自动化困难的技术和实验工作。
    RSI will turn AI research into knowledge work, automating hard technical and experimental work.

反共识 · Contrarian takes

  • 完美平衡的游戏很无聊；不平衡和高方差才让游戏有趣。
    A perfectly balanced game is boring; imbalance and high variance make games interesting.
  • 模型不会只是循环并制造不可维护的混乱；你可以理解它们并与它们协作。
    Models won't just loop and create unmaintainable mess; you can understand and work with them.
  • 瓶颈不在智能或 token；瓶颈在于与模型协作的方式。
    Not bottlenecked by intelligence or tokens; bottlenecked by ways of working with the model.
  • 完全无头自我改进可能不是最佳选择；人类应该分配算力。
    Fully headless self-improvement may not be the best option; humans should allocate compute.
  • 当今模型不够有创造力，无法发明投机解码；它们会暴力优化内核。
    Models today aren't creative enough to invent speculative decoding; they'd brute-force kernels instead.
  • 一切都很不同，又没什么不同；劳动力市场尚未出现重大颠覆。
    Everything is very different and nothing is that different; no major labor market disruption yet.
