本期速览 · Overview

Far AI 的新排行榜系统测试了前沿 AI 的安全防护，发现部分模型具有韧性，但其他模型可被廉价通用越狱攻破。

Far AI's new leaderboard systematically tests frontier AI safeguards, finding some models resilient but others vulnerable to cheap universal jailbreaks.

要点 · TL;DR

  • OpenAI 和 Anthropic 的模型抵御了所有越狱攻击，而 Gemini 和 Grok 仅花不到 300 美元就被破解。
    OpenAI and Anthropic models repelled all jailbreaks, while Gemini and Grok were broken for under $300.
  • 分层防御——对齐、监控、探针、账户控制——加上数据过滤能有效遏制滥用。
    Layered defenses—alignment, monitoring, probes, account controls—plus data filtering can effectively contain misuse.
  • 大多数灾难性 AI 风险可通过谨慎部署避免，而非不可避免的技术宿命。
    Most catastrophic AI risk is avoidable through careful deployment, not an irreducible technical fate.

核心观点 · Key points

  • 前沿模型 OpenAI 和 Anthropic 抵御了所有测试的越狱，而 Gemini 和 Grok 存在数百个低成本通用越狱。
    Frontier OpenAI and Anthropic models resisted all tested jailbreaks, while Gemini and Grok had hundreds of universal jailbreaks at low cost.
  • 最有效的越狱依赖社交工程与提示叠加，利用人格和目标导向行为，而非奇异混淆。
    Most effective jailbreaks use social engineering and prompt stacking, exploiting personas and goal-directed behavior rather than exotic obfuscation.
  • 结合思维链监控与外部防护的纵深防御让滥用变得困难，但网络等双重用途领域仍具挑战。
    Defense-in-depth combining chain-of-thought monitoring with external safeguards makes misuse hard, but dual-use domains like cyber remain challenging.
  • 预训练数据过滤虽未充分探索，但对减少开源权重模型滥用并改善对齐前景广阔。
    Pre-training data filtering is underexplored yet promising for reducing open-weight model misuse and improving alignment.
  • OpenAI 智能体事件主要是控制与监控失败，而非对齐失败。
    The OpenAI agent incident is primarily a control and monitoring failure, not an alignment failure.
  • 大多数灾难性 AI 风险来自鲁莽竞赛和可避免的错误，而非不可约的技术危险。
    Most catastrophic AI risk stems from reckless competitive racing and avoidable errors, not irreducible technical dangers.

反共识 · Contrarian takes

  • 十年主张攻击主导后，我现在认为在防止 AI 有害协助方面防御占优。
    After a decade arguing offense-dominant, I now think defense dominates for preventing harmful AI assistance.
  • 将 AI 拟人化为社会行动者对越狱出奇有效；简单权威诉求便能切换人格。
    Anthropomorphizing AI as social actors is surprisingly productive for jailbreaks; simple appeals to authority flip personas.
  • 通用越狱正走向零日漏洞模式：发现昂贵、修补迅速、利用窗口有限。
    Universal jailbreaks are becoming zero-day-style findings: expensive to discover, quickly patched, and with limited exploitation windows.
  • 博科圣地采用 AI 的速度超过三大公司是个真谜题；连恐怖分子都称 AI 拯救了许多生命。
    Boko Haram's AI adoption outpacing the big three is a real puzzle; even terrorists attribute AI to saving many lives.
  • 令人意外的是，针对明显恶意意图提示的越狱难度并不低于隐晦技术问题。
    It is surprisingly not easier to jailbreak models on obvious malicious-intent prompts than on hidden technical ones.
  • 保留可见思维链是安全上的礼物；训练清除思维链或改用潜在推理将失去最佳监控工具。
    Preserving visible chain-of-thought is a safety gift; training against it or switching to latent reasoning would lose our best monitoring tool.
