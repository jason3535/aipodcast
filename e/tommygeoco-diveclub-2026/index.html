设计师拥抱氛围编程与自主工具

Designers Embrace Vibe Coding and DIY Tools

汤米·吉奥科 Tommy Geoco · Dive Club · 2026-05-19 · 约 60 分钟 · 原视频 ↗

本期速览 · Overview

一项调查显示设计师对 AI 驱动的工作流程更满意，许多人正在构建自己的工具，催生了品牌工程师等新角色。

A survey reveals designers are happier with AI-driven workflows, and many are building their own tools, leading to new roles like brand engineers.

要点 · TL;DR

  • AI 流畅度成为设计师的差异化优势，因为公司需要“what-if”式探索。
    AI fluency is now a differentiator for designers because companies want what-if exploration.
  • 最好的团队通过黑客松和专职团队为 AI 实验拨出预算，接受“用完即弃”的工作。
    Best teams fund AI play through hackathons and dedicated teams, accepting throwaway work.
  • 未来属于建设者；设计师应拥抱系统思维，持续比较工作流。
    The future belongs to builders; designers should embrace systems thinking and compare workflows.

核心观点 · Key points

  • AI 素养终将成为常态，但当下仍很重要，因为公司需要能以全新方式探索 what-if 问题的设计师。
    AI fluency will eventually become ambient, but right now it matters because companies want designers who can explore what-if questions in new ways.
  • 最优秀的团队通过黑客松和专项小组为 AI 实验留出空间与预算，并把一次性探索视为成本的一部分。
    The best teams create space and budget for AI experimentation through hackathons and dedicated teams, accepting throwaway work as part of cost.
  • 设计正走向一种‘公文包式上下文层’：意图与上下文应随你在不同工具和智能体之间携带。
    Design is moving toward a briefcase context layer: intent and context should travel with you across tools and agents.
  • 设计师可以通过构建内部工具产生高影响力，让市场等跨职能伙伴不再依赖设计团队就能交付。
    Designers can create high impact by building internal tools that let marketing and cross-functional partners ship without depending on design.
  • 未来属于建设者；设计师应拥抱系统思维，并持续比较工作流，因为最佳实践尚未收敛。
    The future belongs to builders; designers should embrace systems thinking and continuously compare workflows because best practices haven't converged yet.

反共识 · Contrarian takes

  • 与‘AI 垃圾’叙事相反，进行 vibe coding 的设计师对工作流满意度更高；AI 让他们做出以往没有的微观精确决策。
    Against the AI-slop narrative, designers who vibe code report higher workflow satisfaction; AI enables micro-precision decisions designers didn't have before.
  • 完全自动化的智能体工作流不是目标；保留一些摩擦才能校验意图并重新调整方向，从而获得更好结果。
    Fully automated agent workflows are not the goal; keeping some friction lets you fact-check intent and re-steer, making better results.
  • 用一次性提示词比较模型毫无意义；你必须像驾驶一样多轮迭代，才能真正评估模型。
    Comparing models by one-shot prompts is meaningless; you must drive the model over many iterations to evaluate it properly.
  • 现在暂缓采用智能体式工具也没关系；未来会收敛出更便宜、更易上手的入场方式，不必被 FOMO 裹挟。
    It's okay to wait on adopting agentic tooling now; convergence will bring cheaper, easier entry later, so don't let FOMO drive you.
  • 管理者的 vibe coding 参与度高于初级和中级设计师，这可能让资历较浅的设计师被排除在流程之外。
    Managers are doing more vibe coding than junior and mid-level ICs, which may be cutting less senior designers out of the process.
  • 你并不需要阅读自己的上下文或 Markdown 文件；持续喂入修正和负面示例，就能构建可靠的‘黑箱公文包’。
    You don't need to read your context or markdown files; feeding corrections and negative examples builds a reliable black-box briefcase.
