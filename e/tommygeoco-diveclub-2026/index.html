设计师拥抱氛围编程与自主工具

Designers Embrace Vibe Coding and DIY Tools

汤米·吉奥科 Tommy Geoco · Dive Club · 2026-05-19 · 约 60 分钟 · 原视频 ↗

打开互动全文版（中英对照 + 朗读 + 问答）→

本期速览 · Overview

一项调查显示设计师对 AI 驱动的工作流程更满意，许多人正在构建自己的工具，催生了品牌工程师等新角色。

A survey reveals designers are happier with AI-driven workflows, and many are building their own tools, leading to new roles like brand engineers.

要点 · TL;DR

  • AI 流畅度成为设计师的差异化优势，因为公司需要“what-if”式探索。
    AI fluency is now a differentiator for designers because companies want what-if exploration.
  • 最好的团队通过黑客松和专职团队为 AI 实验拨出预算，接受“用完即弃”的工作。
    Best teams fund AI play through hackathons and dedicated teams, accepting throwaway work.
  • 未来属于建设者；设计师应拥抱系统思维，持续比较工作流。
    The future belongs to builders; designers should embrace systems thinking and compare workflows.

核心观点 · Key points

  • 目前使用 vibe coding 的设计师对工作流程满意度更高，尽管有人对此嗤之以鼻。
    Designers who currently vibe code report higher workflow satisfaction, despite widespread dismissive noise.
  • 内部工具和品牌工程师角色正在兴起；设计师为跨职能需求打造定制工具。
    Building internal tools and brand engineer roles is rising; designers create custom tooling for cross-functional needs.
  • 设计的未来涉及便携式上下文层——一个携带意图的'公文包'——可在不同工具与智能体之间移动。
    The future of design involves a portable context layer — a 'briefcase' of intent — carried across tools and agents.
  • AI 改变了设计工作流程，带来更广泛的探索和精度，但人的把控和保留的摩擦依然不可或缺。
    AI changes design workflows by enabling wider exploration and precision, but human steering and preserved friction remain essential.
  • 公司必须通过黑客马拉松或专门团队为 AI 实验留出空间，即使有成本和时间代价。
    Companies must deliberately make room for AI experimentation, through hackathons or dedicated teams, despite cost and time.

反共识 · Contrarian takes

  • 更好的 AI 工作流程并非全自动；保留摩擦让设计师能核查意图并重新引导。
    Better AI workflows are not fully automated; keeping friction lets designers fact-check intent and re-steer.
  • 管理者比初级和中级设计师更多使用 vibe coding，暗示初级设计师可能被排除在 AI 流程外。
    Managers do more vibe coding than junior and mid-level designers, suggesting juniors may be excluded from AI processes.
  • 内部工具不是'AI 垃圾'；它们可以设计精美，并提升构建者在组织内的影响力。
    Internal tools are not AI slop; they can be beautifully designed and elevate the builder's influence inside the org.
  • 通过一次性提示比较模型毫无意义；评估应关注在迭代中它们如何'驾驶'。
    Comparing models through one-shot prompts is meaningless; evaluation should focus on how they drive through iterations.
  • 等待再采用智能体式工具也没关系；未来的主流工具会让切换成本更低、更易上手。
    It is okay to wait on adopting agentic tools; future mainstream tools will make switching cheap and easier.
  • 设计师应赋能跨职能伙伴完成设计任务，而非担心被蚕食或取代。
    Designers should enable cross-functional partners to do design tasks, rather than fear being cannibalized or replaced.
